Dear Parents and Guardians,As we prepare for the upcoming school year, we would like to share important information regarding student parking at Conway High School.Students who drive to school may purchase a parking permit, which authorizes them to park on campus. Parking permits will be sold on a first-come, first-served basis during schedule pickup. If permits remain available, they may also be purchased during Open House on Wednesday, August 12, from 4:00–7:00 p.m., or at any time during the school year through the main office.Beginning this school year, parking spaces will no longer be assigned. Instead, students may park in any available designated student parking space upon arrival each day. In previous years, assigned parking was necessary due to limited parking availability. Because additional parking is now available, we are transitioning to open parking.
