## Area of Concern:

Mattison Elementary’s past reading data has shown that the percentage of students performing at grade level is a significant concern.

Our students participated in the DIBELS (Dynamic Indicators of Basic Early Literacy Scales) Assessments. This assessment is a tool used to better understand student learning and performance better. DIBELS composite scores help indicate a student’s general reading proficiency and risk level for reading difficulties.

|2024-2025|Well Below Benchmark BOY|Well Below Benchmark MOY|Below Benchmark BOY|Below Benchmark MOY|At Benchmark BOY|At Benchmark MOY|Above Benchmark BOY|Above Benchmark MOY|

(BOY=Beginning of the Year; MOY=Middle of the Year) **Target Goal:** Mattison Elementary student scores will increase by five percentage points in each DIBELS category: At Benchmark, Above Benchmark.

## Strategies:

Mattison Elementary will continue to utilize a variety of supports and systems acquired through district leadership. Specific vision will be promoted in the following areas.

- Tier II Targeted Small Groups

- The school master schedule contains a grade-level Tier II time for teachers to work with small groups of students using the Amplify curriculum, which is targeted towards the students’ learning goal(s) in reading. All Interventionists and Activity teachers will assist with pulling small groups of students.
- Teachers will use BOY screeners, as well as Amplify benchmark measures and progress monitoring data, to target their instruction. These will identify the gaps in phonological awareness, phonics, comprehension, vocabulary, and fluency to provide the best explicit individualized instruction.
- HQIM 95% Group Phonics-Grades K-4 will fully implement the 95% Group curriculum.
- HQIM Benchmark Reading-Grades K-4 fully implement the Benchmark Reading curriculum.
- Teachers will meet weekly for PLC:
- Mattison teachers will work collaboratively within their grade level team, including the IF and administrators, to internalize HQIMs.
- A focus will be placed on continuous improvement and growth for collaborative teams to implement strong Tier I instruction utilizing our HQIMs, reflecting on data, and adjusting Tier I small groups accordingly.
- Teachers will analyze progress monitoring data and lessons to adjust Tier II small groups utilizing Amplify data.

## <u>Area of Concern:</u>

Mattison students have shown an increase in behavioral issues that include a lack of problem-solving skills and social skills.

<u>Target Goal:</u> Student discipline referrals will decrease by 10% from the previous year.

## Strategies:

- Teachers will implement a school-wide behavior plan.

- Teachers will make positive phone calls home.
- Classes will receive counseling lessons monthly.
- The school counselor will utilize our UCA counseling students to support staff, parents, and students.
- The school counselor will form small groups to address specific behavioral needs.
- Administrators and school counselors will consult with our campus school psychologist to support teachers, parents, and students.
- Administrators will communicate with families regularly about behavioral expectations and student progress
- Staff will collaborate with our Pinnacle Point and Arisa Health (formally known as Counseling Associates) team members.
- Back-to-school behavioral professional development w/ Sam Scott (District Behavior Coordinator)
- Counselor will sit in classrooms to offer support to students who are struggling with behavioral issues.
- Counselor will visit with students using the check-in/check out systems for struggling students.
- Counselor will communicate with students during their lunch period to model appropriate behavior for students with behavioral issues.
- Counselor will communicate with parents of students with behavior for suggestions on how to help the students at school.
- Counselor will make appropriate referrals to local counseling agencies for additional support for students who are struggling with behavior.
- Counselor will conduct observations for Functional Behavior Assessments for students whose behavior requires specialized support.
## <u>Implementation Plan:</u>

- RTI meetings are conducted to discuss students who require additional support with their behavior.
- The Campus X-tra mile behavior program will be implemented as well as the district mandated curriculum.
- Nine-week rewards will be conducted with a behavioral motivation component.
## <u>Professional Development:</u>

K-4 teachers will receive training on behavior supports in August 2025 to understand the impact it has on behavior/learning in our schools.

## <u>Evaluation:</u>

- Behavior data will be reviewed to determine areas of concern or patterns of behavior.

- Follow-up meetings with classroom teachers, counselor, and administrators for progress monitoring.
