# CAROLYN LEWIS ELEMENTARY SCHOOL ACTION PLAN

# 2025-2026

# Area of Concern/Need #1

An area of concern identified in our school improvement plan is overall student proficiency in literacy. Student achievement data from the Spring 2024 ATLAS assessment reveals a significant gap in proficiency levels between grade levels. Only 40% of 3rd-grade students achieved proficiency in literacy, compared to 68% of 4th-grade students. This disparity indicates a need for targeted interventions and instructional support in 3rd grade to ensure students are building strong foundational reading and writing skills. Improving literacy outcomes at this critical stage is essential to support long-term academic success across all subject areas.

# Goal #1

By the end of the 2025–2026 school year, the percentage of students achieving proficiency in literacy, as measured by the ATLAS assessment, will increase by at least 15 percentage points in each grade level, with a specific focus on closing the gap in 3rd grade by increasing proficiency from 40% to at least 55%. This goal will be supported through targeted literacy interventions, data-driven instruction, and ongoing progress monitoring.

# Strategies

- Utilization of data wall and monthly progress monitoring of all students. All students will receive intervention based on all assessment pieces. This includes students who are performing below, at, or above benchmark.
- Utilize the RTI process to ensure that students are making progress from their “at level” interventions. If a lack of progress is shown, adjustments will be made to the interventions provided.
- Implementation of WIN (What I Need) Teachers will ensure that daily interventions are taking place in the classroom as well as any available pull-out interventions that are needed with the support of interventionists and activity teachers.
- Teachers will review data and plan appropriately during weekly collaboration and scheduled RTI meetings. Additionally, teachers will participate in weekly PLC meetings.
- RISE-Grades K-4 fully implement and follow RISE, including the use of decodable texts to target specific reading levels
- Benchmark Reading-Grades K-4 fully implement Benchmark Reading instruction with fidelity as paced by the curriculum.
- 95% Group Phonics Instruction-Grades K-4 fully implement 95% Group Phonics instruction with fidelity as paced by the curriculum.
- Amplify-Grades K-4 and Activity Teachers will implement the Amplify Tier 2 Intervention Curriculum with fidelity.
- Administrators and Instructional Coach will support teachers in Lesson Internalization practices to ensure intentional and effective delivery of literary instruction that meet student needs.

# Professional Development

- Continuous support for teachers through the Instructional Facilitator and Interventionists.
- Continuous professional development and support on Tier 2 Interventions.
- Continuous professional development/coaching with Benchmark Reading, 95% Groups Phonics, and Amplify.
- Science of Reading professional development with continuous support from Administrators and Instructional Facilitators.
# Implementation Plan

- Administration of DIBELS and analysis of data.
- WIN Groups will be formed and rotations will begin.
- Amplify data (assessments and progress monitoring) will be added regularly to the data wall.
- Changes to interventions will be made on an as needed, individual basis.
- Teachers will participate in regular grade-level collaborative meetings with the Instructional Coach and Administrators to plan and internalize instruction.
- In addition to teacher monitoring of assessment data and progress monitoring, the data team will monitor as well. Initially, the data team will determine who is taken to the RtI committee.
# Evaluation

- Administrators and Instructional Facilitators will analyze results of classroom progress monitoring and adjust accordingly.
- Interim assessment data will be analyzed and instruction adjusted accordingly.
- Spring 2026 standardized testing data will be analyzed to determine if goals were met.
# Area of Concern/Need #2

An area of concern identified for our elementary school is the need for consistent understanding and implementation of behavior expectations and responses across all staff. There is a lack of building-wide calibration on Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 behaviors, which has led to inconsistencies in managing student behavior and applying interventions. Additionally, there is a need to clarify the distinction between office referrals and incident reports to ensure accurate documentation and appropriate follow-up. Staff members also require support in deepening their understanding of student behavior and how to respond effectively and appropriately. Aligning practices with the building behavior matrix and ensuring consistent application across all settings is essential to creating a safe, supportive, and predictable environment for all students.

# Goal #2

By the end of the 2025-2026 school year, the number of office referrals will decrease by 25%—from 186 to no more than 140—through the implementation of a calibrated school-wide behavior system, including clear definitions of Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 behaviors, consistent use of the building behavior matrix, staff training on appropriate behavioral responses, and clarification of the distinction between office referrals and incident reports. Progress will be monitored monthly to ensure consistent application and impact.

# Strategies

- Teachers will receive professional development with Bethany Hill about Trauma Informed Schools.
- Faculty and staff will conduct PLCs in which calibration of the building behavior matrix will occur.
- Support from district Behavior Specialist in implementation of behavior modification plans.
- Administrators will attend professional development focusing on multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support systems hosted by the district.
- Administrators will attend the Arkansas School Behavior Conference focusing on strategies and resources for school behavior teams.
- Administrators will establish a behavioral leadership team to lead the processes related to create a multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support system.
- Classroom teachers and the counselor will provide instruction on prosocial skills to all students as part of Tier I instruction.
- Staff members will utilize RtI if students are struggling to meet behavioral expectations.
# Professional Development

- Required professional development focusing on trauma. This professional development will be provided by Bethany Hill.
- Administrators will attend the Arkansas School Behavior Conference focusing on strategies and resources for school behavior teams.
- Administrators will attend Behavior Leadership Training provided by the district.
# Implementation Plan

- Professional development for behavior systems and behavior supports will be attended during the summer.
- Administration will create an MTSS behavior team to support teachers and students needing additional interventions
- Scheduled RTI meetings for students needing Tier 2 and Tier 3 behavior interventions.
# Evaluation

- Data reports will be analyzed to review disciplinary trends through Educators’ Handbook
- Grade level and individual reports will be shared throughout the year to verify interventions are working
- Follow up meetings with classroom teachers and behavior team for progress monitoring
