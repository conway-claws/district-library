## <u>1: Jointly Developed</u>

Describe how the School works with parents and families to develop and review relevant plans, policies, and strategies related to engagement.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***1.1:** How does the School-in collaboration with parents-establish an engagement plan that* *reflects the specific academic improvement needs of the School, and that includes programs* *and practices that enhance engagement and address the specific engagement needs of* *students and their families?*
[*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(a)*]

- ***1.2:** What efforts have been made to ensure adequate representation of parents and families* *of participating children in the process (Title I families)?*
[*ESSA § 1116(c)(3)*] Parents, staff members, and community leaders will meet with the school improvement chairperson, parent facilitator, and administrators to provide input on the development of the school parent and family engagement plan. The committee will be formed to represent the diversity of students that are present within our school. This committee of members will meet twice a semester to review, update, and make plans for improvement. ## <u>2: Communication</u>

Describe how the School will communicate with and distribute information to parents and families.

## Guiding Questions

- ***2.1:** How does the School distribute an informational packet appropriate for the age and grade* *of each child annually, ensuring to include:*
- *description of the engagement program*
- *recommended roles for parents, students, teacher, and the School*
- *ways for a family to get involved*
- *survey regarding volunteer interests*
- *schedule of activities planned throughout the school year*
- *regular, two-way, and meaningful system for parents/teachers to communicate* [*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(b)(3)(B)(i)*]
- ***2.2:** How will the School ensure information related to school and parent programs, meetings,* *and other activities is provided to parents in a format and in a language that parents can* *understand (to the extent practicable)?*
- *How is relevant information provided in a variety of ways? (For example, paper copies* *made available, as well as social media posts, website links, parent apps, etc.?* [*ESSA § 1116(e)(5)*]
- ***2.3:** How does the School offer flexible opportunities for meetings with families?*
[*ESSA § 1116(c)(2)*]

The staff at Theodore Jones Elementary school will strive to use a variety of meaningful, two-way communication tools and methods to increase parent involvement in order to support student success in ways that are linguistically appropriate.

- Classroom teachers will send home daily or weekly folders to share students' learning experiences from school with parents/guardians at home.
- Theodore Jones will post online a weekly newsletter called "PawPrint" to share effective parent strategies as well as school wide events and information. We will also have paper copies available for parents, if requested.
- Fourth grade students will use daily student planners as an organizational tool to maintain records of their daily activities and assignments. The planners will also serve as communication to parents.
- Parents will be encouraged to follow the school’s open Facebook and Instagram pages to stay informed about the events taking place within our school.
- Theodore Jones will assist parents in downloading the Conway Schools app on their electronic devices and learn how to use push notifications to receive information about current and upcoming school events.
- Theodore Jones will use the Parent Link as another form of communication (through e-mail, text, and phone) to share important information to parents.

- Teachers will make phone calls, send e-mails, and/or write notes to communicate with parents/guardians.
- Families will be invited to attend parental involvement meetings and activities. The meetings and events will be held at various times during the day or evening to better accommodate parents throughout the school year. These events will provide information on the following topics: 1.) What parents should expect of their child's education, what students will learn, and how students will be assessed. 2.) How a parent can make a difference in his/her child's education through role play and demonstration by trained volunteers on how to incorporate developmentally appropriate learning activities in the home environment. The school parent and family engagement plan is available online for families and local community members to view. Paper copies will be made available for those in need. Contact information for Anna Baxley, the parent facilitator/counselor, will be made available for parents as well.
- Informational packets will be provided annually to the parent of each child in the school and will also be available on the school website. It will include the following information: 1) the school's Parent and Family Engagement plan; 2) the recommended roles of the parent, teacher and child; 3) ways for the parent to become involved at school and/or his/her child's education; 4) a survey for parents' interest regarding volunteering at the school; 5) activities planned throughout the year; 6) an explanation on the process of communicating in a regular two-way meaningful manner with the child's teacher and principal; 7) contact information for the parent facilitator, Anna Baxley.
- Teachers will strive to meet with parents face-to-face arranging schedules to meet work schedules and/or allowing for home visits, phone calls, or through an online meeting program. If needed, teachers will ask for a translator to ensure that both parties have a clear understanding on how to meet the needs of the whole child.
- Teachers will provide parents with updates of student progress through interim reports, literacy reports, report cards, STAR Reading reports, and NWEA Map assessments.

.Theodore Jones Elementary will utilize community resources to provide experiences for our students to enhance instructional lessons.

- Reading events will be held once in the fall and once in the spring to promote a culture of reading within our school by community leaders. Business owners, service men and women and other community members will read books to our students and discuss their role in our community.
- During the Spring semester, we will plan an enrichment day that will provide students with learning opportunities about future jobs and the value of education within that specific career field.
- Community members are asked to send in a short video explaining their role in the community and what their job consists of in relation to standards that are being taught. ## <u>7: School-Parent Compact</u> (Title I schools)

Describe the process School will follow to jointly develop with parents a School-Parent Compact as required under Title I, Part A.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***7.1:** How does the School jointly develop a School-Parent Compact which does the following:*
- *Outlines how parents, the entire School staff, and students will share the responsibility* *for improved student academic achievement*
- *Addresses the importance of regular two-way, meaningful communication through:* *■ conferences (no fewer than 2 each year)* *■ frequent reports on progress* *■ reasonable access to staff* *■ opportunities to volunteer* *■ observation of classroom activities* [*ESSA § 1116(d)*]

- ***7.2:** How do families access the compact in order to understand the shared responsibility for* *improved student academic achievement?*
- *including parent-teacher conferences in elementary Schools, at least annually*
- *Include a link or insert the language of the compact to demonstrate this requirement* *has been met.* [*ESSA § 1116(d)(2)(A)*]
Theodore Jones will collaborate with a committee of students, parents, and teachers in September/October to review, update and maintain the Student-Parent-Teacher compact.The plan will outline how the entire school staff, parents, and the students share the responsibility for improving student academic achievement and the means by which the school and parents build and develop a partnership that helps children achieve high standards. All stakeholders, including the teacher, student, and parents will sign the student, parent, and teacher compact, which outlines the responsibility of each individual and their roles in ensuring student success. The compact also includes the importance of regular two-way meaningful conversation for parents and teachers. Teachers and parents will communicate through e-mails, phone calls, progress reports, and conferences. The compact also encourages parents to volunteer at the school. Volunteer training will be held at different times and dates throughout the year to ensure that parents have various opportunities to attend. After attending training, parents will be asked to fill out a survey indicating the volunteer opportunities that they are most interested in. Anna Baxley will create a binder documenting the parent volunteers and the volunteer opportunities that they are most interested in. A copy of these documents will be kept in the office as well.
