Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street August 12, 2025

<u>Members Present</u>: Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Interim Superintendent Jason Black; Interim Deputy Superintendent Dr. Karen Lasker; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Bryce Bennett; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Preston Echols, Kelli Gordon, Greg Hughes, Eric King, Shanda New; General Counsel Shasta Wagner; Business Manager Stacy Rimmer; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Interim Superintendent Black led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Board President Franklin spoke about the new law concerning public comments and explained that the sign-up list will be at the door at each meeting.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Kaitlin Smith – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized Kaitlin Smith for being an Arkansas Teacher of the Year Finalist.
- Video Series – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented the final video in the series, JROTC Program.
## <u>Patrons Comments</u>

- Floyd Balentine – Mr. Balentine spoke to the Board about starting the superintendent’s job search and to use Dr. Jeff Standridge in the process instead of hiring a search firm. He also talked about the tradition of how the Board’s officers are usually selected and the new policy about the number of years for the Board members terms.
- Jimmie Cavin – Mr. Cavin spoke to the Board about passing a motion to have a legislative audit and to make Dr. Naylor, Jr. the Board’s liaison in this matter.
- Gary Logan – Mr. Logan spoke to the Board about starting the superintendent job search, create a timeline for the search, and make the timeline public. Board members Dr. Naylor, Jr. and Trip Leach addressed concerns about a legislative audit regarding the investigation of some employees of the district. Mrs. Franklin asked General Counsel Mrs. Wagner to guide the Board in discussing this matter.
## <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u>

Items on the Consent Agenda were:

- Approval of Minutes – July 8, 2025, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of Minutes – July 23, 2025, Special Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in July 2025
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of Act 1599 Resolution Board member Dr. Petty asked to pull item C. List of Bills Paid in July 2025 from the Consent Agenda so that he could ask questions about some expenditures later in the meeting. **Dr. Petty made the motion to approve the consent agenda without item C List of Bills Paid in** **July 2025. The motion was seconded by Mr. Cummings III and passed unanimously at 6:24 p.m.**
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Strategic Planning Board Work Shop Update – Interim Superintendent Black presented an update from the Strategic Planning Board Work Shop.
- CPSD Reading Forum Workshops – Interim Superintendent Black gave an update on the Reading Forum Workshops.
- PD Palooza & Conway Employee Orientation – Interim Superintendent Black gave an overview of the PD Palooza and the Conway Employee Orientation.
- City Wide Safety Exercise – Interim Superintendent Black presented an overview from the City Wide Safety Exercise and thanked everyone from the community and district that participated. Superintendent Black recognized Eric King, Director of Safety and Security, for his hard work on this and presented him with a Wampus Cat coin.
- Back to School Teacher Fair and Convocation – Interim Superintendent Black asked Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, to share the Back to School Teacher Fair and Convocation plans on August 14
th.

- First Day of School – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, shared plans for back to school.
## <u>Action Item</u>

- Joining Potential Social Media Harm Class Action – Shasta Wagner, General Counsel, invited Jay Bequette, Bequette Billingsley & Kees P.A., to present to the Board the Potential Social Media Harm Class Action. **Mr. Leach made a motion to authorize the Superintendent to engage the law firm of** **Motley and Rice, LLC to represent the District in the Social Media Harm Class Action. The** **motion was seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr. and passed unanimously at 6:43 p.m.**
## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- District Financial Report – Dr. Andy Ashley presented the financial report for July. **Dr. Petty made the motion to approve the financial report for July, as presented. The** **motion was seconded by Mr. Cummings and passed unanimously at 6:51 p.m.**

## <u>Action Item</u>

- 2025-2026 Strategic Plan – Interim Superintendent Black invited Dr. Jeff Standridge to present to the Board the 2025-2026 Strategic Plan for the District. **Ms. Walton made a motion to approve the 2025-2026 Strategic Plan, as presented. The** **motion was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 7:12 p.m.**
## <u>Information Items</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Bennett presented the academic report. He presented updates on CPSD Math and Special Education Planning Fall 2025. He invited Dr. Kathi Sweere, District Reading Specialist, to present an update on the CPSD Reading Plan. Dr. Bennett invited Heather Leavell, Sallie Cone Preschool, to present an update on Fueling Brains.
- CPSD Teacher Supply Update – Shanda New, Director of Programs and Accountability, presented an update on the funds for teacher supplies.
- CPSD Behavior Plan Overview – Preston Echols, Director of Student Services, and Sam Carter-Scott, Behavioral Support Coordinator, presented an overview of the Behavior Plan.
- HR Updates – Dr. Karen Lasker, Interim Deputy Superintendent, talked about teacher multi- year contract process and presented a HR update.
- Conway Junior High and Bob and Betty Courtway Middle School Updates – Assistant Superintendent Ashley and Principal Marquis Rogers gave an update on the water damage at the junior high school and recognized Nabholz Construction for all their hard work getting the school ready for the first day of school. They also thanked ServPro, CPSD Maintenance team, and the CPSD Technology team for their hard work to get the school ready for students. Dr. Ashley will present an update on the renovations at Bob and Betty Courtway Middle School next month. Assistant Superintendent Ashley answered Board member Dr. Petty’s questions about the bill listing for July. **Dr. Petty made the motion to approve the List of Bills Paid in July 2025, as presented. The** **motion was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 8:11 p.m.**
- Executive Session Laws – General Counsel Shasta Wagner presented an overview of Executive Session Laws to the Board. Board President Franklin addressed concerns from Mr. Balentine and Mr. Logan about the superintendent search and said that the Board has not discussed the process in hiring a superintendent yet. Board member Dr. Naylor, Jr. thanked the principals of the District and asked the media to publish about the meaningful things going on in the district instead of negative things.
## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 8:28 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session to consider a personnel matter. The Board exited Executive Session at 10:02 p.m.

Board President Franklin invited Shasta Wagner, General Counsel, to speak about the notification to Legislative Audit. Mrs. Wagner told the Board that she had made a report to Legislative Audit. Legislative Audit told her they will contact the District if anything is needed and she will bring to the Board a report when she receives one.

Board President Franklin and members discussed the superintendent search. Board President Franklin asked for a motion for a Special Meeting on August 26, 2025 at 6:30 p.m. to discuss the superintendent search.

**Mrs. Hargis made the motion to call a Special Meeting on August 26, 2025 at 6:30 p.m. to** **discuss the superintendent search process. The motion was seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr. and passed unanimously at 10:05 p.m.**

- Resignations
