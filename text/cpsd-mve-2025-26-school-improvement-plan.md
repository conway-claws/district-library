# Marguerite Vann Elementary School Action Plan

# 2025-2026

## Area of Concern #1: Literacy Proficiency

Many Vann students are performing below grade level expectations in literacy as measured by the ATLAS assessment. Spring 2024 data indicate that only 35% of third and fourth grade students had scores at Levels 3 or 4.

## Goal #1

Vann teachers and leaders have identified methods and strategies that result in student growth in the area of literacy as evidenced by Spring 2024 ATLAS scores. These scores indicate that 69% of all third and fourth grade students and 71% of the lowest quartile of third and fourth grade students grew in this area. Therefore, Vann teachers will continue implementing effective literacy instruction aligned to the science of reading, using high-quality instructional materials (HQIM), and continually refining and improving their instructional practices. This will result in an increase of at least 10% in literacy proficiency of third and fourth grade students as measured by the Spring 2026 ATLAS.

## Strategies

- ➔ Implement district-adopted literacy HQIM (including 95 Percent Group for phonics/word study and Benchmark Universe for reading) with integrity, building capacity for increasing skillfulness in delivery of instruction through the practice of weekly lesson internalization in grade level teams. ➔ Screen/assess students regularly and analyze data in order to plan and adjust Tier 1 and Tier 2 instruction to maximize student growth in the area of literacy. ➔ Utilize the RTI process to collaboratively and strategically plan for instructional adjustments that may be required for individual students so as to meet their needs. ➔ Utilize Power Hour (daily Tier 2/Tier 3 intervention time in master schedule) to ensure that students have access to in-class and pull-out interventions without the loss of access to Tier 1 instruction. ➔ Use DESE’s *Key Levers for Instructional Leaders* to provide actionable feedback to teachers regarding their impact in the areas of structures for learning, aligned content, direct instruction, student practice, and assessment and differentiation for both the top and bottom of Scarborough’s Rope. ➔ Host school, family, and community engagement events that foster the enjoyment of reading, emphasize its importance to students’ futures, and provide parents with tools to utilize at home. These will include book fairs, Read Across America Week, family nights, author/guest reader visits, and other similar events.

## Professional Development

- ➔ Instructional coaches serving Vann will attend district-level training in literacy coaching strategies and the effective utilization of the district’s HQIM. ➔ Teachers, coaches, and leaders will attend curriculum and instruction professional development provided by CPSD in August 2025. ➔ Campus professional development in August 2025 will include training on topics including the science of reading, *Key Levers*, lesson internalization, student engagement, and skillful use of HQIM. ➔ Ongoing professional learning on topics related to literacy instruction will occur as part of weekly grade-level collaboration during the 2025-2026 school year.
## Implementation

- ➔ August 2025: Professional development activities for coaches, teachers, and leaders ➔ August 2025: Data analysis from 2025 ATLAS to determine and refine target areas within the content area of literacy ➔ Fall 2025: School, family, and community engagement event(s) focused on literacy ➔ Fall 2025-Spring 2026: Weekly grade-level collaboration with ongoing professional learning opportunities, lesson internalization, and data analysis ➔ Fall 2025-Spring 2026: Classroom walkthroughs and conferences ➔ Fall 2025-Spring 2026: Assessment and data analysis (DIBELS, state screeners, interim and classroom assessments) ➔ Fall 2025-Spring 2026: RTI committee meetings ➔ Spring 2026: School, family, and community engagement event(s) focuses on literacy
## Evaluation

- ➔ DIBELS, state screener, interim, and classroom assessment data will be analyzed on an ongoing basis by coaches, leaders, and teachers; instructional adjustments will be made as needed. ➔ Spring 2026 ATLAS data will demonstrate whether goals were met

## Area of Concern #2: Mathematics Proficiency

The vast majority of Vann students are performing below grade level expectations in literacy as measured by the ATLAS assessment. Spring 2024 data indicate that only one in five (21%) of third and fourth grade students had scores at Levels 3 or 4.

## Goal #2

In addition to low achievement, Spring 2024 ATLAS data indicate low overall growth in the area of math, as well. Therefore, we need to adapt and improve our instructional practices in this area. This will result in an increase of at least 10% in mathematics proficiency of third and fourth grade students as measured by the Spring 2026 ATLAS.

## Strategies

- ➔ Implement district-adopted mathematics HQIM (Eureka Math²) with integrity, building capacity for increasing skillfulness in delivery of instruction through the practice of weekly lesson internalization in grade level teams. ➔ Screen/assess students regularly and analyze data in order to plan and adjust Tier 1 and Tier 2 instruction to maximize student growth in the area of literacy. ➔ Utilize the RTI process to collaboratively and strategically plan for instructional adjustments that may be required for individual students so as to meet their needs. ➔ Utilize small groups during a portion of the daily math block to ensure that students have access to timely reteaching of Tier 1 content or Tier 2 intervention in the classroom ➔ Use teacher and instructional coach participants in the *Modern Classroom* *Project* as mentors and leaders in improving the quality of mathematics instruction, increasing differentiation, and laying the foundation to improve student math outcomes. ➔ Use DESE’s *Key Levers for Instructional Leaders* to provide actionable feedback to teachers regarding their impact in the areas of structures for learning, aligned content, direct instruction, student practice, and assessment and differentiation for mathematics instruction. (Pending availability from DESE by or during the 2025-2026 school year.) ➔ Host school, family, and community engagement events that foster math fluency, emphasize the importance and application of mathematics to students’ futures, and provide parents with tools to utilize at home. These will include family nights and a math fluency family involvement initiative.

## Professional Development

- ➔ Instructional coaches serving Vann will attend district-level training in mathematics coaching strategies and the effective utilization of the district’s HQIM. ➔ Teachers, coaches, and leaders will attend curriculum and instruction professional development provided by CPSD in August 2025. ➔ Campus professional development in August 2025 will include training on topics including math fluency development, *Key Levers*, lesson internalization, student engagement, and skillful use of HQIM. ➔ Ongoing professional learning on topics related to mathematics instruction will occur as part of weekly grade-level collaboration during the 2025-2026 school year.
