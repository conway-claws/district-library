# Simon Middle School School Action Plan

# 2024-2025

## <u>1.Area of Concern/ Need:</u>

After evaluating the 2023 Aspire data, the Simon Administrative team and Simon Leadership Team determined reading and math as areas of focus for the 2023-24 school year. An overall score of 40 percent of students scored exceeding or ready in reading and overall 42 percent of students scored exceeding or ready in math. Simon faculty will continue to take an active role frequently assessing and monitoring student achievement in all areas, such as English and science, but will have a focus in reading and math. The team will review the 2024 Atlas data and make adjustments as needed, when it is available.

<u>Goal:</u> To increase the number of students reading at grade level and proficient in math by 3 percent in all subgroups

## <u>Strategies for Reading:</u>

- At the beginning of the year, our Instructional Facilitator will provide training to the faculty on reading strategies to implement in all classes.
- An intervention time will be provided for all students to everyday to increase specialized instruction for gap areas
- Our 5th grade team will focus on disciplinary literacy.
- The school will assess each student at least three times to determine progress toward the reading goal. (District Reading assessment)
- The school will maintain a record of students who are below grade-level and document progress and increase support as needed. (Response to Intervention reports)
- Teachers will assess students using a variety of evaluation methods.
- Teachers and administrators will encourage reading using school wide book talks.
- We provide monthly school-wide Drop Everything and Read time.
- School administrators and Instructional Facilitators will schedule classroom walk-throughs to assess strengths and needs of instructional practices.
- Teachers will use Common Formative Assessments in literacy to track mastery standards.

- Literacy teachers will analyze newly adopted literacy standards and identify essential skills, while using highly qualified instructional materials.
- PLCs will be created with a focus on teacher efficacy.
<u>Strategies for Math:</u>

- At the beginning of the year, our math Instructional Facilitator will review the testing data, drilling down to specific skills.
- An intervention time will be provided for all students everyday to increase specialized instruction for gap areas.
- School administrators and Instructional Facilitators will schedule classroom walk-throughs to assess strengths and needs of instructional practices.
- Interim assessments will be scheduled, so teachers can monitor growth.
- Our 5th grade team will work on math fluency.
- PLCs will be created with a focus on teacher efficacy.
<u>Professional Development:</u>

- Our literacy and social studies Instructional Facilitators will work with the faculty on reading and writing strategies to implement in all classes.
- Our math and science Instructional Facilitators will work with the faculty in areas of science and to review the overall data.
- Our Instructional Facilitators will meet with teachers by content area throughout the year to discuss interim data and district reading data and make plans for improving achievement.
<u>Implementation Plan:</u>

- We (administration, teachers and counselors) will begin the year by analyzing test scores from the 2024 Atlas test, focusing on strengths and weaknesses. We will compare 2023 and 2024 test scores.
- At the beginning of the year, our Instructional Facilitators from each content area will work with the faculty on reading strategies to implement in all classes.
- At the beginning of the year, our math Instructional Facilitator will review the math scores and focus on areas of growth and areas that need more attention.

<u>Evaluation:</u> We will formally evaluate our progress by analyzing our 2024 Atlas data for improvement in all tested areas but especially in the area of reading and math. We will be specifically looking at all subgroups for improvement. This information will determine our next steps.

## <u>2. Area of Concern/ Need:</u>

In 2022-23, the Leadership Team created a Multi-tiered System of Support for Behavior for students. The team wanted to be sure students’ academic needs are met but also wanted to focus on behavior support and social emotional needs. In 2024- 25, the team will focus on improving the behavior support system, possibly re-working it to continue meeting the needs of students.

<u>Goal:</u> To improve the MTSS for behavior for Simon students, with an emphasis in consistency for incentives.

<u>Strategies:</u>

- The Leadership Team will continue with our school-wide system of expectations for students for Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 behavior, and post the expectations throughout the school.
- We will continue to improve our Positive Behavior Support and Reward System for students and discuss what was successful this year as an incentive.
- Administrators and counselors will work to create behavior plans for students.
- We focus on Classroom Behavior management- rules, routines, and relationships.
- The social and emotional learning curriculum *Character Strong* will continue to be used in health classes to help develop healthy, kind and thoughtful students.
- Our 5th grade team will have a focus on study skills.

<u>Professional Development:</u>

- All faculty and staff will review our current school-wide plan and make suggestions for improvement at the beginning of the year.
- Appointed staff are attending Handle with Care Training.
<u>Implementation Plan:</u> At the beginning of the 2024-25 school year, the faculty and staff will review the School-Wide Behavior plan and expectations. In summer professional development, the Leadership Team will organize what the MTSS will look like in the classroom and school-wide and will develop a reward system. At the beginning of the school year, a calendar will be set for the MTSS and stakeholders to meet.

<u>Evaluation:</u> At the end of the school year, we will compare discipline data from 2021-22, 22-23, and 23-24 to determine what has been successful and what we need to do to improve.
