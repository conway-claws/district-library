# Marguerite Vann Elementary School Action Plan

# 2026-2027

## Area of Concern #1: Literacy Proficiency

Many Vann students are performing below grade level expectations in literacy as measured by the ATLAS assessment. Spring 2025 data indicates that only 20% of third and 37% of fourth grade students had scores at Levels 3 or 4. Fall Interim 2026 data indicates that only 13% of third and 12% of fourth grade students have scores at Levels 3 and 4. Winter Interim 2026 data indicates that only 17% of third and 26% of fourth grade students have scores at Levels 3 and 4.

## Goal #1

Vann teachers and leaders have identified methods and strategies that result in student growth in the area of literacy as evidenced by Spring 2025 ATLAS scores. Our 2025/2026 Interim 1 and Interim 2 scores 28% of all third grade and 42% of all fourth grade students grew levels in ELA. Our 2026 Winter Interim data shows that 50% of our fourth grade students that were the lowest quartile for Summative 2025 are no longer in the bottom quartile. Therefore, Vann teachers will continue implementing effective literacy instruction aligned to the science of reading, using high-quality instructional materials (HQIM), and continually refining and improving their instructional practices. This will result in an increase of at least 10% in literacy proficiency of third and fourth grade students as measured by the Spring 2027 ATLAS.

## Strategies

- ➔ Implement district-adopted literacy HQIM (including 95 Percent Group for phonics/word study and Arts & Letters for reading) with integrity, building capacity for increasing skillfulness in delivery of instruction through the practice of weekly lesson internalization in grade level teams during PLC time. ➔ Utilize PLC time twice a month to analyze data, student misconceptions, class weaknesses in content, and to plan reteaching sessions for those concepts. ➔ Screen/assess students regularly and analyze data in order to plan and adjust Tier 1 and Tier 2 instruction to maximize student growth in the area of literacy. Teachers will utilize students IRPs to focus on goals and data collection for progress monitoring. ➔ Utilize the RTI process to collaboratively and strategically plan for instructional adjustments that may be required for individual students so as to meet their needs.

➔ Utilize Power Hour (daily Tier 2/Tier 3 intervention time in master schedule) to

- ensure that students have access to in-class and pull-out interventions without the loss of access to Tier 1 instruction.
➔ Use DESE’s *Key Levers for Instructional Leaders* to provide actionable feedback

- to teachers regarding their impact in the areas of structures for learning, aligned content, direct instruction, student practice, and assessment and differentiation for both the top and bottom of Scarborough’s Rope.
➔ Host school, family, and community engagement events that foster the enjoyment

- of reading, emphasize its importance to students’ futures, and provide parents with tools to utilize at home. These will include book fairs, Read Across America Week, family nights, author/guest reader visits, and other similar events.
## Professional Development

- ➔ Instructional coaches serving Vann will attend district-level training in literacy coaching strategies and the effective utilization of the district’s HQIM. ➔ Teachers, coaches, and leaders will attend curriculum and instruction professional development provided by CPSD in August 2026. ➔ Campus professional development in August 2026 will include training on topics including the *Key Levers*, lesson internalization, student engagement, and skillful use of HQIM. ➔ Ongoing professional learning on topics related to literacy instruction will occur throughout the 2026-2027 school year.
## Implementation

- ➔ August 2026: Professional development activities for coaches, teachers, and leaders ➔ August 2026: Data analysis from 2026 ATLAS to determine and refine target areas within the content area of literacy ➔ Fall 2026: School, family, and community engagement event(s) focused on literacy ➔ Fall 2026-Spring 2027: Weekly grade-level collaboration with ongoing professional learning opportunities, lesson internalization, and data analysis ➔ Fall 2026-Spring 2027: Classroom walkthroughs and feedback ➔ Fall 2026-Spring 2027: Assessment and data analysis (state screeners, interim and classroom assessments) ➔ Fall 2026-Spring 2027: RTI committee meetings

## Evaluation

- ➔ State screener, interim, and classroom assessment data will be analyzed on an ongoing basis by coaches, leaders, and teachers; instructional adjustments will be made as needed. ➔ Spring 2027 ATLAS data will demonstrate whether goals were met
## Area of Concern #2: Mathematics Proficiency

Many Vann students are performing below grade level expectations in math as measured by the ATLAS assessment. Spring 2025 data indicates that only 20% of third and 23% of fourth grade students had scores at Levels 3 or 4. Fall Interim 2026 data indicates that only 1% of third and 11% of fourth grade students have scores at Levels 3 and 4. Winter Interim 2026 data indicates that only 13% of third and 14% of fourth grade students have scores at Levels 3 and 4.

## Goal #2

Based on our Spring 2025 summative as well as Interim 2026 scores, our students are in high need of growth in math. Our 2025/2026 Interim 1 and Interim 2 scores 23% of all third grade and 15% of all fourth grade students grew levels in ELA. Our 2026 Winter Interim data shows that 7% of our fourth grade students that were the lowest quartile for Summative 2025 are no longer in the bottom quartile. Vann teachers will continue using high-quality instructional materials (HQIM) while using modern classroom techniques, and continually refine and improve their instructional practices. This will result in an increase of at least 10% in math proficiency of third and fourth grade students as measured by the Spring 2027 ATLAS.

## Strategies

- ➔ Implement district-adopted mathematics HQIM (Eureka Math²) with integrity, building capacity for increasing skillfulness in delivery of instruction through the practice of weekly lesson internalization in grade level teams. ➔ Utilize PLC time twice a month to analyze data, student misconceptions, class weaknesses in content, and to plan reteaching sessions for those concepts. ➔ Teachers will implement techniques within the *Modern Classroom* program. ➔ Screen/assess students regularly and analyze data in order to plan and adjust Tier 1 and Tier 2 instruction to maximize student growth in the area of math while implementing MIPs with goals and progress monitoring. ➔ Utilize the RTI process to collaboratively and strategically plan for instructional adjustments that may be required for individual students so as to meet their needs.

➔ Utilize small groups during Power Hour to ensure that students have access to

- timely reteaching of Tier 1 content or Tier 2 intervention in the classroom
➔ Use teacher and instructional coach participants in the *Modern Classroom*

- *Project* as mentors and leaders in improving the quality of mathematics instruction, increasing differentiation, and laying the foundation to improve student math outcomes.
➔ Use DESE’s *Key Levers for Instructional Leaders* to provide actionable feedback

- to teachers regarding their impact in the areas of structures for learning, aligned content, direct instruction, student practice, and assessment and differentiation for mathematics instruction.
➔ Provide families tools/resources that foster math fluency, emphasizing the

- importance and application of mathematics to students’ futures
## Professional Development

- ➔ Instructional coaches serving Vann will attend district-level training in mathematics coaching strategies and the effective utilization of the district’s HQIM. ➔ Teachers, coaches, and leaders will attend curriculum and instruction professional development provided by CPSD in August 2026. ➔ Campus professional development in August 2026 will include training on topics including math fluency development, *Key Levers*, lesson internalization, student engagement, and skillful use of HQIM. ➔ Ongoing professional learning on topics related to mathematics instruction will occur as part of weekly grade-level collaboration during the 2026-2027 school year.
## Implementation

- ➔ August 2026: Professional development activities for coaches, teachers, and leaders ➔ August 2026: Data analysis from 2026 ATLAS to determine and refine target areas within the content area of mathematics ➔ Fall 2026-Spring 2027: Weekly grade-level collaboration with ongoing professional learning opportunities, lesson internalization, and data analysis ➔ Fall 2026-Spring 2027: Classroom walkthroughs and feedback ➔ Fall 2026-Spring 2027: Assessment and data analysis (state screeners, interim and classroom assessments) ➔ Fall 2026-Spring 2027: RTI committee meetings

➔ Fall 2026-Spring 2027: Provide families with math resources to work on skills at

- home
## Evaluation

- ➔ State screener, interim, and classroom assessment data will be analyzed on an ongoing basis by coaches, leaders, and teachers; instructional adjustments will be made as needed. ➔ Spring 2027 ATLAS data will demonstrate whether goals were met
