Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street July 9, 2024

<u>Members Present</u>: Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mr. Trip Leach, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Bill Milburn, Mr. Carl Barger

## <u>Members Absent</u>: Mr. Andre’ Acklin

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Ben Darley, Keisha Jefferson, Eric King; Chief Financial Officer Robyn Keene; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board Vice President Naylor called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Board Member Recognition – Superintendent Collum recognized Mr. Jason Sandefer for serving on the Board for the last two years. The Board thanked him for his service to the district. Superintendent Collum led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance. <u>Reorganization of the Board</u> **A motion to appoint Dr. Naylor, Jr. as Board President was made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded** **by Mr. Leach. The motion passed unanimously at 6:11 p.m.** **A motion to appoint Mr. Leach as Board Vice President was made by Mr. Milburn and** **seconded by Mrs. Franklin. The motion was passed unanimously at 6:12 p.m.** **A motion to appoint Mrs. Hargis as Board Secretary was made by Mr. Leach and seconded by** **Dr. Naylor, Jr. The motion passed unanimously at 6:12 p.m.** <u>Resolution Naming Primary Board Disbursing Officer</u> **A motion to appoint Mrs. Linda Hargis as the Primary Board Disbursing Officer was made by** **Mrs. Franklin and seconded by Mr. Barger. The motion carried unanimously at 6:13 p.m.** <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:17 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mr. Leach and seconded by Mrs. Hargis.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – June 11, 2024, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in June 2024

- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of Act 1599 Resolution
- Approval of 2024-2025 Student Handbook
- Approval of 2024-2025 Athletic Handbook
- Approval of Fueling Brains Proposal 2024-2025
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Goal Setting Workshop – Superintendent Collum reminded the Board of the upcoming goal setting workshop on August 7
th.

- Back to School Convocation – Superintendent Collum shared Convocation plans on August 14th.
- Back to School Plans – Superintendent Collum shared back to school plans.
- Budget Workshop – Superintendent Collum reminded the Board of the upcoming budget workshop on August 28
th.

- High School Parking Update – Superintendent Collum invited Principal Worley, Conway High School, to give an update of new parking procedures.
## <u>Chief Financial Officer’s Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Robyn Keene presented the financial report for June. **Mrs. Franklin made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mr. Barger and passed unanimously at 6:39 p.m.**
## <u>Information Items</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Duncan presented the academic update on PD Palooza ’24 for staff on July 17
th and 18 th.

- Early Childhood Program Update – Assistant Superintendent Duncan invited Director Jefferson to demonstrate a Fueling Brains activity with the Board. <u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Mrs. Franklin, the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 6:49 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**
- Resignations
- Elections
## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 6:51 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session to consider a personnel matter. The Board exited Executive Session at 8:20 p.m.

<u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at** **8:20 p.m. on a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Mr. Leach.**
