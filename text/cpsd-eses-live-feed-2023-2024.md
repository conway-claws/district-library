Dear CPSD families, 

CPSD is currently monitoring the weather, roads, and bus routes for today.

Decisions to alter the school schedule will be made based on the ability of CPSD to safely transport students to and from school.  Any decisions made will be based on the most accurate information and forecasts available.  As always, our top priority is the safety of our students, staff and parents.

As we approach inclement weather season, we would like to inform each of you of the process and methods Conway Public School District will disseminate information regarding possible school closings. Please review the following.

Mornings of possible inclement weather, CPSD will begin evaluating road conditions within Faulkner County by 4:00 a.m. while monitoring weather projections for the remainder of the day. Possible actions could include closures, delayed start times, or in certain scenarios an early release. School closings or late start determinations will occur by 6:00 a.m. before morning bus routes begin. 

CPSD will utilize an automated calling and texting system to notify students, parents and staff of possible closings or delays.  A pre-recorded message will be sent to all phone numbers in the student and staff directory within 10 minutes.  Please make sure the numbers that you listed on the student information sheet are current.

If school is to continue as normal, CPSD will not make an announcement. 

Our social media outlets will be continuously updated to keep parents and community members informed of school closing status. To ensure everyone is informed, television stations will also be contacted.

Please review the CPSD Bus Routes - Snow/Inclement Weather Routes.

Visit our website at conwayschools.org for more information. CPSD Inclement Weather

Thank you for your support and cooperation!
