## <u>1: Jointly Developed</u>

Describe how the School works with parents and families to develop and review relevant plans, policies, and strategies related to engagement.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***1.1:** How does the School-in collaboration with parents-establish an engagement plan that* *reflects the specific academic improvement needs of the School, and that includes programs* *and practices that enhance engagement and address the specific engagement needs of* *students and their families?*
[*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(a)*]

- ***1.2:** What efforts have been made to ensure adequate representation of parents and families* *of participating children in the process (Title I families)?*
<u>[ESSA § 1116(c)(3)]</u>

Parent feedback will be solicited, collected, and analyzed throughout the school year. A committee of parents meets quarterly to provide specific input on the parent engagement plan. Further, parent conferences and meetings are conducted at the request of staff and stakeholders and are timed to accommodate parent schedules. Meeting purposes may include communication, assisting parents in understanding how to enhance their child’s education, adjust/adapt to a changing school environment, and how to communicate with the school and staff more effectively. Teachers will be surveyed and some will serve on committees to inform the parent engagement plan.

The school will hold an orientation for parents at each grade level to inform them about the school's participation in their child's future. Further, teachers will hold conferences individually with parents of children in their classrooms. Parents will be asked to engage in a discussion of their dreams for their students, how the school and parents can work together to support academic efforts, and offer feedback to the teacher and school. The school will offer parents workshop(s) each year to provide an explanation of the statewide assessment system, school communication system (Apptegy), standards, other accountability measures, graduation requirements, and college information. Parent feedback is solicited in the planning of these events.

The school will encourage parents in the following types of roles and activities to increase

their involvement and support for student learning: Volunteering College/Career Fair Awards day presentation Parent education workshops Hispanic outreach Orientation presentations Open House Parent-school organization-CLAWS-Community Leaders At Work for Students Red Ribbon week Fine arts events Various committees-Handbook, Curriculum, School Health and Safety, Diversity

*Guiding Questions*

- ***2.1:** How does the School distribute an informational packet appropriate for the age and grade* *of each child annually, ensuring to include:*

- *recommended roles for parents, students, teacher, and the School*

- ***2.2:** How will the School ensure information related to school and parent programs, meetings,* *and other activities is provided to parents in a format and in a language that parents can* *understand (to the extent practicable)?*

- ***2.3:** How does the School offer flexible opportunities for meetings with families?*
<u>[ESSA § 1116(c)(2)]</u>

The school will regularly distribute electronic communication to parents through email, the school website, and district-approved social media platforms. Opportunities for participation in meetings, feedback, college/career information, and the school calendar will be communicated. At open house and during PTC, representatives from CLAWS will be present to distribute information and speak with stakeholders.

The teachers will utilize Schoology, the school website, and Apptegy to disseminate information to parents and students. Parents may use email or Schoology messaging to communicate with members of the school staff. Parents will be issued a Schoology parent login for academic monitoring and have access to Home Access Center to monitor attendance.

Teachers will contact parents on an individual basis using the Apptegy platform, email, and/or phone to communicate about their child's progress as needed.

The school will provide parents reports every four and a half weeks with information regarding their child's academic progress.

The school will use the student handbook, school Website, signage at the school entrance, and parent orientation meetings.
