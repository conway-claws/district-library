# Courtway Middle School School Improvement Plan

# 2025-2026

## <u>GOAL 1 - Reading</u>

## Area of Need/Concern

- ■ Literacy- Student reading and writing on grade level
**Data**

- **■ In Spring 2024, only 35% of students in grades 5–7 at Courtway Middle School** **scored at or above grade level in literacy on the ATLAS exam.**
## Measurable Goal

- ■ Increase the percentage of students from not achieving to achieving in each grade level by 5% by the end of the 2025/26 school year.
## Strategies

1. Administrators and the instructional coach will analyze data from the spring administration of the 2025 ATLAS assessment to determine individual student needs and build a data wall to track student progress towards goal.
2. Continue the implementation of the Science of Reading Initiative (RISE). ☐Include an increased focus on literacy across all classes, with planned
access to reading in all disciplines.

3. For the 2025/26 school year, we will continue implementing Into Reading by HMH for 5th and 6th Grade and Into Literature by HMH for 7th Grade, focusing on using learning ladders for essential learning goals. ☐Utilize close reading strategies, as they are embedded through this
curricular resource.

- ☐Standardize the use of learning ladders across all reading and ELA courses, placing learning goals in the hands of students for a better understanding of skills and need for mastery. ☐Track mastery of essential standards using CFA data and learning ladders.

4. Implement RTI practices for students needing Tier 2 and Tier 3 interventions using WIN time to remediate and extend for student growth.
5. Use of Sonday, SPIRE, and Take Flight interventions as Tier III interventions.
6. Build a more robust culture of reading by incentivizing and celebrating reading growth.

## <u>GOAL 2 - BEHAVIORAL INTERVENTION</u>

## Area of Need/Concern

- **■** Student behavioral concerns, causing missed instruction.
## Data

- ■ During the 2024–2025 school year, Courtway Middle School experienced a high number of office discipline referrals, particularly those resulting in in-school suspension (ISS) and out-of-school suspension (OSS).
## Measurable Goal

- ■ By May 2026, Courtway Middle School will reduce the total number of student ISS and OSS days by 25% compared to the 2024–2025 school year, by implementing positive behavior supports and restorative discipline practices.
## Strategies

1. Continued implementation of School-Wide Positive Behavior Supports (SWPBS).
a. A collaborative effort to establish values and expectations across the campus
b. Behavior Matrix-C-WAY(Choose kindness; Wise Choices; Act Safely; Yes, I Can!)
c. Tier 1 behavioral instruction to implement throughout the different school settings
2. Establish a restorative practices model to address conflict and repair harm.
3. Formation of CMS Behavioral Intervention Team, a school-wide behavior team to address consistency and solutions for staff members.
4. Provide Tier 2 behavior interventions for students with recurring disciplinary infractions.
5. Analyze referral data monthly to identify patterns and respond with targeted interventions.
6. Staff members will implement Tier II positive behavioral interventions as developed in conjunction with the behavioral RTI team.

7. The assistant principals will monitor the effectiveness of the Tier II behavioral interventions in increasing positive behavior and decreasing negative behaviors.
8. The counselor and administrators will conduct Functional Behavior Assessments (FBAs) when it is deemed necessary based on data collected and analyzed by the behavioral leadership team.
## Professional Development

- ■ The administrators and counselor will attend professional development focusing on a multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support system hosted by the district. (June 2-3, 2025) ■ The administrators and counselor will attend the Arkansas School Behavior Conference focusing on strategies and resources for school behavior teams. (July 23, 2025) ■ All staff members will participate in professional development on the implementation of a multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support system. As part of this professional development, the staff will revisit the consistent schoolwide expectations for behavior across the campus (the behavior matrix). (August 12, 2025) ■ Monthly behavior strategy sessions with admin and teachers collaborating on interventions for student behaviors.
## Implementation Plan

- ■ Admin and staff will attend multiple professional development sessions leading up to the 2025/26 school year to learn about successful behavioral teaching and intervention practices. ■ Continue to teach the Tier 1 behavior matrix and school-wide behavioral procedures. Focus on ways to positively correct misbehavior using de-escalation and questions. ■ Weekly focused SEL lesson during WIN time for all students on Mondays built by CMS staff members. ■ Formation of a Behavioral Intervention and Leadership team. The team will continue to learn about behavioral RTI and serve our school staff in this role when students need greater support. ☐The team will provide grade-level teams with strategies and interventions
for students needing further behavior support.

- ☐Assistant principals and counselors will continue to hold monthly behavioral meetings with each team (5, 6, & 7).

■ The administrators and counselor will analyze data from eSchool each quarter

- to determine the effectiveness of this plan in regard to decreasing negative behaviors that result in discipline referrals to the office, resulting in ISS or OSS.
■ The assistant principals will review discipline incident data for specific students

- with Tier II behavioral interventions to determine the impact of the Tier II behavior plan on decreasing the students’ negative behaviors.

## Measurable Goal

- ■ Increase the percentage of students from not achieving to achieving in each grade level by 10% by the end of the 2025/26 school year.
## Strategies

1. Administrators and the instructional coach will analyze data from the spring administration of the 2025 ATLAS assessment to determine individual student needs and build a data wall to track student progress towards goal.
2. Implement RTI practices for students needing Tier 2 and Tier 3 interventions using WIN time to remediate and extend for student growth.
3. Technology (iPads and TVs) to enhance instruction

- make curriculum more vibrant and interactive
- easily allow students to share their thinking with the class
4. Integration of Proficiency scales (learning ladders)
- Use Proficiency scales to clearly define learning expectations for students
- Use Proficiency scales and learn checks to help students monitor their own progress towards learning goals.
- Use Proficiency scales as centerpiece when planning lessons and writing assessments.
- Use elements on Proficiency scales when planning intervention (remediation and extensions) for students.

5. Student data and targeted intervention
- Use ATLAS data, common formative assessments, and short assessments (Learn Checks) aligned with learning ladders to assess student progress
- Create targeted intervention session for WIN time to address specific student needs
