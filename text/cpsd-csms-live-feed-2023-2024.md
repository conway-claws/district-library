The Arkansas State Board of Health Rules and Regulations Pertaining to Immunization Requirements states that every child attending a public school in the state shall receive a series of immunizations in order to enter school. Religious, Medical, and Philosophical exemptions can be obtained through the Arkansas Department of Health. However, proof of exemption letters must be obtained each year and provided to the school for their records. (AR Code Annotated 20-7-109, 6-18-702, 6-60-504, 20-78-206).

If your child is already 11 years old or will turn 11 yrs. before September 1, 2023, he/she will need the Tdap and the MCV4 vaccines before returning to school this year. If your child is entering 7th grade and has not received the MCV4 vaccine, he/she must have this before returning to school this year. 

The deadline for receiving these immunizations is September 1, 2023. If your child does not have the required immunizations by this date, he/she will be sent home until immunizations are updated. There will be NO exceptions! 

All updated records must be turned in to Nurse Siegel. 
Any questions or concerns should be sent to Nurse Siegel at siegelh@conwayschools.info. Or, please call 501-329-2782 with any questions. Thank you!

What is eWallet?
No more bills stuffed in backpacks! At the Scholastic Book Fair, kids shop without cash thanks to eWallet, a digital payment account for purchases at the Fair. Family and friends contribute funds. Then kids choose their own books. It’s convenient, simple, and secure.

How does it work? 
It starts on your school’s Book Fair homepage. A child’s family follows a link to create a free eWallet account. There are three ways to fund an account: Add money with a credit card, use funds from a previous eWallet, or invite friends and extended family to contribute. 

Because funds are available immediately, a child can shop the Fair with eWallet right away. Families can use a leftover balance to fund an eWallet for a future Fair.  #ChampionsForKids #celebratecsms

### 2023-11-15 · Carl Stuart Middle School (id 4988993)

The Veterans Day Assembly took place on Friday, November 10, and it was a special event honoring our Veterans. The school's band, orchestra, and choir all performed during the assembly, adding a meaningful touch to the event.

The assembly also highlighted the importance of family and pride in service. Students had the opportunity to introduce their family members who had served in the military and share which branch they belonged to. This created a personal connection and allowed the student body to better understand the sacrifices made by these individuals.

The Student Council played a significant role in hosting a reception for both the veterans and their family members in the media center. This gathering provided a chance for everyone to connect and appreciate each other's company. Additionally, the Student Council shared valuable information about each branch of service, educating the attendees about the different roles within the military.

Guest speakers were Veterans Paul Tull, Jason  Tooke, Angela Campagna, and Dr. Phillip Fletcher.  They spoke about the importance of Veterans Day and the significance of joining the military. The Veterans emphasized how being part of the military helps individuals find their sense of belonging and purpose. They also touched upon the sacrifices that service members make, but highlighted how the military gives back in numerous ways.  

During the assembly, the veterans had the privilege of sitting beside their students.  This served as a reminder of the importance of supporting and acknowledging the sacrifices made by these servicemen and servicewomen.

Conway Public Schools will not be in session, as planned, on February 19, 2024 in observance of President's day.  
The District will be seeking waiver approval from the Division of Elementary and Secondary Education to transition the remainder of the 2023-2024 school year to serve minutes versus days. Any future inclement weather days will be evaluated.  More information will be shared after the waiver has been approved or denied.
