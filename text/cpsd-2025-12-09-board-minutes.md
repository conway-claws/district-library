Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Champion Student Recognition</u>

Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, introduced Kimber King, Champion Student of the Month from Woodrow Cummins Elementary School, and presented a video featuring Kimber. Interim Superintendent Black presented Kimber with a certificate. Kimber led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.
