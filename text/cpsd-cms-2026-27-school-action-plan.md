# Courtway Middle School School Improvement Plan

# 2026-2027

## <u>GOAL 1 - Reading</u>

## Area of Need/Concern

- ■ Literacy- Student reading and writing on grade level
## Data

- ■ In Fall 2025, 29% of students in grades 5–7 at Courtway Middle School demonstrated proficient or advanced understanding of required ELA skills on the ATLAS Interim I exam. ■ In Winter 2026, 32% of students in grades 5–7 at Courtway Middle School demonstrated proficient or advanced understanding of required ELA skills on the ATLAS Interim II exam.
## Goal

- ■ Increase the percentage of students from not achieving to achieving to 37% by the end of the 2026-27 school year as measured by the ATLAS 2027 Summative Exam.
## Strategies

1. School personnel will analyze data from the spring administration of the 2026 ATLAS assessment to determine individual student needs and build a digital intervention wall to track student progress towards goal.
2. School personnel will place students in Tier III interventions based on dyslexia screening criteria and IEP/504 requirements, and place remaining students in grades 5-6 into new “Open Box” courses based on Tier II and enrichment criteria. The creation of these courses align with the district goal of providing growth opportunities for all students and is essential for meeting the growth needs of moderate to highly achieving students.

## <u>GOAL 2 - BEHAVIORAL INTERVENTION</u>

## Area of Need/Concern

- **■** Significant student behaviors, either in frequency and/or magnitude, resulting in missed instruction.

## Data

- ■ During the 2025-2026 school year, Courtway Middle School saw a marked reduction in the number of students with discipline referrals resulting in in-school suspension (ISS) and out-of-school suspension (OSS). ■ During the 2025-2026 school year, Courtway Middle School saw a marked reduction in the number of ISS and OSS days assigned to students with disabilities.
## Measurable Goal

- ■ By May 2027, Courtway Middle School will continue to reduce the total number of student ISS and OSS days by 10% compared to the 2025-2026 school year, by implementing positive behavior supports and restorative discipline practices.
## Strategies

1. Continued implementation of School-Wide Positive Behavior Supports (SWPBS).
a. A collaborative effort to establish values and expectations across the campus
b. Behavior Matrix-C-WAY(Choose kindness; Wise Choices; Act Safely; Yes, I Can!)
c. Tier 1 behavioral instruction to implement throughout the different school settings
2. Establish a restorative practices model to address conflict and repair harm.
3. Formation of CMS Behavioral Intervention Team, a school-wide behavior team to address consistency and solutions for staff members.
4. Provide Tier 2 behavior interventions for students with recurring disciplinary infractions.
5. Analyze referral data monthly from Incidents+ software to identify patterns and respond with targeted interventions.
6. Staff members will implement Tier II positive behavioral interventions as developed in conjunction with the behavioral RTI team.
7. The assistant principals will monitor the effectiveness of the Tier II behavioral interventions in increasing positive behavior and decreasing negative behaviors.
8. The counselor and administrators will conduct Functional Behavior Assessments (FBAs) when it is deemed necessary based on data collected and analyzed by the behavioral leadership team.
## Professional Development

- ■ The administrators and counselor will attend professional development focusing on a multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support system hosted by the district. (Summer 2026)

■ All staff members will participate in professional development on the

- implementation of a multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support system. As part of this professional development, the staff will revisit the consistent schoolwide expectations for behavior across the campus (the behavior matrix). (August 2026)
■ In-depth training and expectation communication for Incidents+ and SmartPass

- software. (August 2026)
■ Monthly behavior strategy sessions with admin and teachers collaborating on

- interventions for student behaviors.
## Implementation Plan

- ■ Admin and staff will attend multiple professional development sessions leading up to the 2026-2027 school year to learn about successful behavioral teaching and intervention practices. ■ Continue to teach the Tier 1 behavior matrix and school-wide behavioral procedures. Focus on ways to positively correct misbehavior using de-escalation and questions. ■ Weekly focused Mentoring Monday during WIN time for all students to mentor and encourage small groups of students (max of 15 students per teacher). ■ Formation of a Behavioral Intervention and Leadership team. The team will continue to learn about behavioral RTI and serve our school staff in this role when students need greater support. ☐The team will provide grade-level teams with strategies and interventions
for students needing further behavior support.

- ☐Assistant principals and counselors will continue to hold monthly behavioral meetings with each team (5, 6, & 7).
## Evaluation

- ■ The administrators and counselor will analyze data from Incidents + and eSchool each quarter to determine the effectiveness of this plan in regard to decreasing negative behaviors that result in discipline referrals to the office, resulting in ISS or OSS. ■ The assistant principals will review discipline incident data for specific students with Tier II behavioral interventions to determine the impact of the Tier II behavior plan on decreasing the students’ negative behaviors.
## <u>GOAL 3 - Math</u>

## Area of Need/Concern

- ■ Number of students scoring at gradle level in math on the ATLAS test.

## Data

- ■ In Fall 2025, 15% of students in grades 5–7 at Courtway Middle School demonstrated proficient or advanced understanding of required mathematics skills on the ATLAS Interim I exam. ■ In Winter 2026, 23% of students in grades 5–7 at Courtway Middle School demonstrated proficient or advanced understanding of required mathematics skills on the ATLAS Interim II exam.
## Measurable Goal

- ■ Increase the percentage of students from not achieving to achieving in each grade level by 10% by the end of the 2026-2027 school year.
## Strategies

1. Administrators and the instructional coach will analyze data from the spring administration of the 2026 ATLAS summative assessment to determine individual student needs and build a data wall to track student progress towards goal.
2. Implement RTI practices for students needing Tier 2 and Tier 3 interventions using WIN time to remediate and extend for student growth.
3. Implement new Open Box courses for remedial, Tier II and enrichment in mathematics.
4. Technology (iPads and TVs) to enhance instruction
- better proximity to monitor students and address student needs
- make curriculum more vibrant and interactive
- easily allow students to share their thinking with the class
5. Integration of Proficiency scales (learning ladders)
- Use Proficiency scales to clearly define learning expectations for students
- Use Proficiency scales and learn checks to help students monitor their own progress towards learning goals.
- Use Proficiency scales as centerpiece when planning lessons and writing assessments.
- Use elements on Proficiency scales when planning intervention (remediation and extensions) for students.
6. Student data and targeted intervention
- Use ATLAS data, common formative assessments, and short assessments (Learn Checks) aligned with learning ladders to assess student progress
- Create targeted intervention session for WIN time to address specific student needs

- Continue to use Mentoring Mondays along with classroom teachers for student conferences around math achievement.
