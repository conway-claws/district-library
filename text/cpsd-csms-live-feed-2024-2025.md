Give your child a safe, cashless way to shop at the Book Fair.  What is eWallet? No more bills stuffed in backpacks! At the Scholastic Book Fair, kids shop without cash thanks to eWallet, a digital payment account for purchases at the Fair. Family and friends contribute funds. Then kids choose their books. Because funds are available immediately, a child can shop the Fair with eWallet right away. ### 2024-10-29 · Carl Stuart Middle School (id 7350281)

Exciting News for Our Middle Schools! We will begin implementing Yondr pouches on November 4, 2024. Students will receive their own Yondr pouch to take home on that morning.

What does this mean for students? To create a focused and distraction-free environment, all cell phones, smart watches, and AirPods will be secured in these special bags during school hours. Students are required to bring their Yondr pouches every morning and place their devices inside before entering the school. The bags will remain with them, but all devices must be turned off during school hours. At the end of the day, students will unlock their Yondr pouches as they exit the school and will keep their pouches with them. This initiative will help foster learning, connection, and engagement—without the screens!

Important Reminder: Violating district policy regarding the possession of cell phones can lead to disciplinary action. Students who tamper with the Yondr pouch, repeatedly forget to bring their pouch, damage or take another student's Yondr pouch, or use their phone without authorization during the school day may face consequences ranging from warnings and phone confiscation to detentions, Saturday school, or in-school suspension. Additionally, restitution may be required to replace a Yondr pouch.

We will also be participating in a survey of students as part of a program supported by Governor Sarah Huckabee Sanders and the Office of Education Policy. This survey aims to gather valuable insights into the effectiveness of our new policies and the overall school experience. Consent forms will be sent home soon, so please return them to your student's third period teacher by November 8, 2024.

Let’s work together to enhance our students’ educational experience!

🏆 We are thrilled to share that two incredible teachers from Carl Stuart Middle School have been selected as 2025 Mental Health Champion Award recipients by Arisa Health! 💙

Congratulations to Jonathan Gunsolus and Charles “Chuck” German, who were chosen out of more than 110 educators nominated across dozens of Arkansas counties for their unwavering dedication to student well-being.

Mr. Gunsolus, "Mr. G", was recognized for his deep connections with students, his leadership in the D3 (Discipline, Determination, Dreams) program, and the way he encourages and supports students daily—whether it’s mentoring, playing soccer, gardening, or simply being a listening ear.

Mr. German was honored for his years of mentorship work, founding both the D3 and G3 programs that now impact nearly 200 students with the help of 10 staff mentors. His focus on character development, community service, and leadership has made a powerful and lasting difference on campus.

We are incredibly proud of these two Wampus Cat educators for being Champions for Kids in every sense of the word! 👏

SPECIAL RECORDS TO BE DESTROYED
On July 7, 2025, the Conway Public School District will destroy Special Education records of students who graduated, moved or were dismissed from Special Education services in the district between January 1, 2019 and June 30, 2019. This process is carried out in compliance with federal and state laws. Students, parents and/or guardians may obtain any data from folders prior to July 7, 2024, by calling the Special Education office at (501) 450-6634, extension 0303 or emailing at records@conwayschools.info.
