Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street March 11, 2025

<u>Members Present</u>: Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Andre’ Acklin, Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Carl Barger, Mr. Tom Kennedy

## <u>Members Absent</u>: Mr. Trip Leach

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Ben Darley, Kelli Gordon, Greg Hughes, Sarah Jerry, Eric King; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; Business Manager Stacy Rimmer; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Naylor called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video featuring Kedren White, Ascending Student of the Month from Raymond and Phyllis Simon Middle School. Kedren led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Conway High School Girls and Boys Bowling State Champions – Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, introduced the 6A Girls and Boys Bowling State Champion teams.
- Counselor Sarah Franklin – Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized Counselor Sarah Franklin, Carl Stuart Middle School, for receiving the 2025 Mental Health Champion Award from Arisa Health.
- National Merit Scholars/Chamber Partnership – Superintendent Collum updated the Board on the Chamber Partnership with the National Merit Scholars. He thanked Brad Lacy, Conway Area Chamber of Commerce President; and congratulated the National Merit Finalists: Reese Taylor, Gavin Law, Octavio Menon, Annalise Montgomery, and Timothy Garrison.
- Office for Education Policy (OEP) Awards – Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized the following schools: Carolyn Lewis Elementary School, for High ELA Growth; Ida Burns Elementary School for High ELA Growth; Preston and Florence Mattison Elementary for High ELA Growth; Marguerite Vann Elementary School for High ELA Growth; Bob and Betty Courtway Middle School for High ELA Growth; and Conway Junior High School for High Math Growth. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:15 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mr. Barger and seconded by Mrs. Hargis.**
## Items on the Consent Agenda were:

- Approval of Minutes – February 11, 2025, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in February 2025
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of School Choice Applications
- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline. Dr. Collum, Dr. Duncan, and Board members addressed patrons concerns about the District Conversion Charter application letters of intent that were previously taken off the agenda for approval.
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Finance Update – Superintendent Collum invited Scott Beardsley, First Security Beardsley, to speak about millage timelines and legal requirements.
- Professional Learning Grant Funding – Superintendent Collum invited Director Sarah Jerry to speak about the district being approved for a Professional Learning grant in the amount of $342,914.57.
- District Awarded Safety Grant – Superintendent Collum invited Director Eric King to speak about the School Safety Grant that was awarded to the District in the amount of $563,765.00.
- School Election Update – Superintendent Collum gave an update on the school election in May. Three Board positions will be on the ballot. For Zone 1, Position 4, Jason Sandefer and Barrett Petty are seeking election. For Zone 5, Position 3, Tom Kennedy is seeking reelection and Leona Walton is seeking election. For At Large, Position 1, Leo Cummings III, Tyler Moses, and Tami Marsh are seeking election.
## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Stacy Rimmer, Business Manager, presented the financial report for February. **Mr. Kennedy made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mrs. Franklin and passed unanimously at 6:48 p.m.** [Mr. Acklin left the meeting at 6:55 p.m. and returned at 6:56 p.m.]
- 2023-2024 Audit Report – Dr. Ashley invited Mark Glover, HCJ CPAs & Advisors, PLLC, to present the 2023-2024 Audit Report. **Mr. Barger made the motion to approve the 2023-2024 Audit Report, as presented. The** **motion was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 7:05 p.m.**
## <u>Information Item</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Dr. Kate Worley, Conway High School Principal; and Karey Smith, Career Center Supervisor, presented the academic report on

Integration of Career and Technical Education.

<u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mr. Kennedy and seconded by Mrs. Hargis, the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 7:22 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**

- Resignations
- Elections
## <u>Patron Comments</u>

- Audie Alumbaugh – Audie Alumbaugh spoke to the Board about concerns with teaching students to read, the reading program, and training of teachers.
- Tyler Moses – Tyler Moses spoke to the Board about concerns with transparency from the Board and policy 1.14 Meeting Agenda & Public Participation.
## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 7:36 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session to consider a personnel matter. The Board exited Executive Session at 9:14 p.m.

<u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at** **9:14 p.m. on a motion made by Mrs. Franklin and seconded by Mrs. Hargis.**
