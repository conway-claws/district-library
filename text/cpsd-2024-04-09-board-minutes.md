Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street April 9, 2024

<u>Members Present</u>: Mr. André Acklin, Mr. Jason Sandefer, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Bill Milburn

## <u>Members Absent</u>: Mr. Trip Leach

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Ben Darley, Kelli Gordon, Keisha Jefferson, Sarah Jerry, Eric King, Jason Lawrence, Tammy Woosley; Chief Financial Officer Robyn Keene; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Acklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- 2023 Arkansas Foreign Language Teacher of the Year-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, introduced Martha Reynolds, 2023 Arkansas Foreign Language Teacher of the Year.
## <u>Champion Student Recognition</u>

- Karl Johnson, Jr. - Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video featuring Karl Johnson, Jr., Champion Student of the Month from Conway Junior High School. She welcomed Principal Preston Echols, who introduced Karl. Karl led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance. Superintendent Collum presented him with a certificate. <u>Reorganization of the Board</u> **A motion to appoint Mr. Acklin as Board President was made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by** **Dr. Naylor, Jr. The motion passed Unanimously at 6:16 p.m.** **A motion to appoint Dr. Naylor, Jr. as Board Vice President was made by Mr. Sandefer and** **seconded by Mrs. Hargis. The motion was passed unanimously at 6:17 p.m.** **A motion to appoint Mrs. Hargis as Board Secretary was made by Dr. Naylor, Jr. and seconded** **by Mr. Milburn. The motion passed unanimously at 6:18 p.m.** <u>Resolution Naming Primary Board Disbursing Officer</u> **A motion to appoint Mrs. Linda Hargis as the Primary Board Disbursing Officer was made by** **Mr. Milburn and seconded by Mr. Sandefer. The motion carried unanimously at 6:19 p.m.**
## <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u>

## The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:31 p.m. on a motion made by

## Mr. Sandefer and seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr.

Items on the Consent Agenda were:

- Approval of Minutes – March 12, 2024, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in March 2024
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of School Choice Applications
- Approval of Policy Revisions
- Approval of Act 1599 Resolution
- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline.
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Facility Planning Update – Deputy Superintendent Black presented an update on facility planning.
- District Enrollment Update – Superintendent Collum presented an update on enrollment.
- Graduation Plans – Superintendent Collum gave an update on graduation plans for the Class of 2024.
## <u>Chief Financial Officer’s Report</u>

- Future Budget Planning Overview – Robyn Keene presented an overview of future budget planning.
- Board Financial Report – Robyn Keene presented the financial report for March. **Mr. Sandefer made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mrs. Franklin and passed unanimously at 6:58 p.m.**
## <u>Information Items</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Duncan, Director Sarah Jerry, Principal Carise Echols, and Librarian Amanda Yates presented the academic update on the Community Volunteer Reading Program.
- CTE Updates and Communication – Assistant Superintendent Duncan gave an update on CTE and Communication.
- Future Academic Program Updates – Assistant Superintendent Duncan gave an update on future academic programs. <u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Mr. Sandefer, the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 7:25 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**
- Resignations
- Elections
- Contract Renewal
