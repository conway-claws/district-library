Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street February 10, 2026

<u>Members Present</u>: Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty

## <u>Members Absent</u>: Mr. Leo Cummings III

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jason Black; Interim Deputy Superintendent Dr. Karen Lasker; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Bryce Bennett; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Preston Echols, Kelli Gordon, Shawn Higginbotham, Eric King, Shanda New; General Counsel Shasta Wagner; Strategic Planning Consultant Dr. Jeff Standridge; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Patrons Comments</u>

- Jimmie Cavin – Mr. Cavin spoke to the Board about the recent FOIA lawsuit against the Board and District.
- Essau Corona – Mr. Corona spoke to the Board about the recent false information about ICE being at the high school.
- Tyler Moses – Mr. Moses spoke to the Board about transparency and the FOIA violation the District received. Board members asked Mr. Jay Bequette and Mrs. Shasta Wagner questions about the FOIA lawsuit.
## <u>Champion Student Recognition</u>

Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, introduced Hudsyn Besancon, Champion Student of the Month from Preston and Florence Mattison Elementary School, and presented a video featuring Hudsyn. Superintendent Black presented her with a certificate. Hudsyn led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Beating the Odds – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized Ida Burns Elementary for receiving awards from the Office for Education Policy for Statewide High Overall Growth: Elementary Level; and Statewide High ELA Growth: Elementary Level.
- District Recognition of Black History Month – February is Black History Month. Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented the Board with a presentation of students and staff celebrating Black History Month and highlighted events that will take place throughout the District this month.
## <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u>

## The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:31 p.m. on a motion made by Mrs.

## Hargis and seconded by Ms. Walton.

Items on the Consent Agenda were:

- Approval of Minutes – January 13, 2026, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in January 2026
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of Resolution for Purchasing Card
- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline.
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Winter Weather – Superintendent Black spoke about the recent winter weather that caused the District to close. He thanked the Maintenance Department, county and city road crews, and partners that helped to clear streets, sidewalks, and parking lots for the District. He also thanked Dr. Ashley, Mr. King, Mr. McMahan, and Mrs. Ryals for their work during this time to get the District ready to open.
- Project Conway4Ward – Superintendent Black presented the Project Conway4Ward for Academics, Arts, Activities, and Athletics.
- Strategic Planning Committee – Superintendent Black invited Dr. Standridge, Strategic Advisor, to present an update on the Strategic Planning Committee.
## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Shawn Higginbotham, Director of Finance, presented the financial report for January. **Ms. Walton made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr. and passed unanimously at 6:45 p.m.**
## <u>Information Items</u>

- CPS Career Fair – Interim Deputy Superintendent Dr. Lasker spoke about the upcoming Career Fair on February 28
th in the Buzz Bolding Arena.

- HR Update – Interim Deputy Superintendent Dr. Lasker invited Director Gynger Campbell to give a HR update.
- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Bennett invited Adam Stroman, Technology Supervisor, and Jake Stroman, Technology Integration Facilitator, to present an instructional technology update.
- New Business Update – Shasta Wagner, General Counsel, gave the Board an update on Board Member Hargis’ request during January’s Board meeting about the District’s procedures for complaints filed against staff. Mrs. Hargis requests a process to be added that states that the parent will receive notification after a complaint has been filed on personnel and documentation kept in the personnel file about the complaint.
## <u>Personnel Approval</u>

**Upon a motion made by Ms. Walton and seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr., the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 7:12 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**

- Resignations
- Elections
## <u>New Business</u>

- Mrs. Hargis wants to start discussions on behavior problems in classrooms where students with special needs are being placed. Board Member Dr. Naylor, Jr. thanked his family, community members, past and present Board members, and staff who have helped him served the District during his time on the Board. He stated that he would like the district to move forward and wants everyone to support Superintendent Black. He wants the District to continue working on discipline issues. He also would like the city to put a moratorium on new apartment buildings. <u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at** **7:28 p.m. on a motion made by Ms. Walton and seconded by Mr. Leach.** Respectfully submitted,
