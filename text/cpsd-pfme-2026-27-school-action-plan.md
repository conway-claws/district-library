# Assessment Context

Mattison Elementary students are assessed through ATLAS (Arkansas Teaching, Learning, and Assessment System), the statewide system designed to measure student learning, growth, and readiness aligned to Arkansas academic standards. ATLAS places students into four performance levels:

- Level 1 – Below Expectations: Student is not yet demonstrating sufficient understanding and will likely need significant support.
- Level 2 – Approaching Expectations: Student shows partial understanding but needs additional support to meet grade-level expectations.
- Level 3 – Meets Expectations: Student demonstrates solid understanding of grade-level standards and is on track for success.
- Level 4 – Exceeds Expectations: Student shows advanced understanding and can apply skills beyond grade-level expectations.
This data directly informs all instructional decisions, intervention placements, and professional learning priorities at Mattison.

## Reading Strategies

##### 1. Tier I Core Instruction — HQIM with Fidelity (Pillars I & II)

All K–4 classrooms will fully implement two district-adopted HQIM programs aligned to the Science of Reading:

- 95% Group Phonics: Provides explicit, systematic phonics instruction addressing phonemic awareness, phonics, fluency, vocabulary, and comprehension — the five essential components emphasized by the district reading plan.
- Arts & Letters Curriculum: Delivers structured literacy instruction that builds language comprehension, background knowledge, and academic vocabulary across content areas.
These programs directly address the district's Year 1 foundation work and extend full implementation into Year 2. Teachers will internalize HQIMs through weekly PLCs alongside instructional facilitators and administrators.

##### 2. Tier II & III Targeted Intervention (Pillar III — Early Literacy Intervention)

The master schedule includes protected, grade-level Tier II/III intervention time. Intervention programs are aligned to district Scottish Rite transition priorities:

- BUILD (Scottish Rite)
- Rite Flight (Scottish Rite)
- Take Flight (Scottish Rite)
- Sonday System
Interventionists and teachers will use the above programs to provide intensive, individualized support.

##### 3. Data-Driven Instruction & Progress Monitoring (Pillars VIII & IX)

- BOY ATLAS K–3 screeners and Amplify Benchmark measures will identify gaps in phonological awareness, phonics, comprehension, vocabulary, and fluency at the beginning of year.
- Individual Reading Plans (IRPs) will be developed for all students based on screener data, with monthly progress monitoring updated in the online ATLAS portal, accessible to families — directly aligned to the district's Year 2 IRP/Quarterly Progress Report goal.
- MOY Interim data (January 2027) will trigger IRP revisions and instructional adjustments.
- Teachers will participate in weekly PLCs to collaboratively analyze data, plan targeted Tier II small groups, and reflect on HQIM implementation.
- RTI meetings will be held at regular intervals to assess all students not performing on grade level and adjust support accordingly.
##### 4. Family & Community Engagement (Pillar VII)

- Literacy reports (IRPs) will be communicated to families through the online ATLAS portal.
- Information about school literacy programs, resources, and progress will be shared through school communication channels.

Additionally, CPSD's district-level analysis identified five cross-grade skill deficit strands from the 2025 ATLAS that are directly relevant to Mattison's K–4 population:

- Fractions → Ratios → Proportional Reasoning (the #1 cross-grade challenge, Grades 3–4)
- Data Literacy Across Grades (scaling and data representation, Grades 3–4)
- Geometry Vocabulary & Spatial Reasoning (Grades 3–4 weaknesses persist into high school)
- Translating Real-World Problems to Mathematical Models (affects early operations)
- Equation Solving Fluency (foundational gaps emerge in early grades)
## Alignment to District Math Plan

The CPSD Math Plan's 2025–2026 and 2026–2027 priorities directly shape Mattison's building strategies:

- Math Intervention Plans (MIPs) for all K–8 students scoring at ATLAS Level 1 — required by Arkansas Code Annotated § 6-17-431
- Strengthen Tier I instruction aligned to HQIM (Eureka Math²) with embedded formative assessment
- Identify and target skill deficits through formative data analysis and small group instruction
- Reflection and refinement of intervention period structures
- Ongoing professional development emphasizing conceptual understanding, vertical alignment, and effective instructional strategies
## Math Goal

**By the end of the 2026–2027 school year, Mattison Elementary will increase the** **number of students scoring at ATLAS Levels 2–4 by advancing at least 10% of** **students from each performance level to the next higher level.**

## Math Strategies

##### 1. Tier I Core Instruction — Eureka Math² with Fidelity

All K–4 classrooms will fully implement Eureka Math² (EM²), the district-adopted HQIM for mathematics. Eureka Math² provides a coherent, research-based approach to building both conceptual understanding and procedural fluency — directly aligned to the district's plan to strengthen Tier I instruction before pursuing acceleration.

- Teachers will internalize EM² curriculum units through weekly PLCs alongside the instructional facilitator (IF) and administrators.
- A focus will be placed on addressing the five identified skill deficit strands — particularly fractions, data literacy, and geometry vocabulary — within Tier I instruction.
- Teachers will use Eureka Math Equip diagnostic data to identify individual student learning gaps and tailor instruction to prerequisite skills.
##### 2. Tier II Targeted Small Group Intervention

The master schedule contains dedicated, protected grade-level Tier II time aligned to the district's commitment to strengthening intervention structures:

- Classroom teachers will pull targeted small groups of students based on skill deficit data.
- BOY ATLAS screeners will identify deficit areas and guide group composition.
- Math Intervention Plans (MIPs) will be developed for all students scoring at Level 1 on ATLAS, in compliance with state requirements, housed in the online ATLAS portal, accessible to families. MIPs will be updated as progress monitoring data is reviewed.
##### 3. Data-Driven PLCs & Instructional Reflection

- Teachers will meet weekly in PLCs to internalize HQIMs, analyze classroom and Tier I small group data, reflect on student progress, and adjust Tier II groups accordingly.
- RTI meetings will be held at regular intervals to assess all students performing below grade level and determine appropriate interventions and supports.
- Building leaders will monitor intervention period structures and refine as needed — consistent with the district's 2025–2026 priority of reflecting on and improving intervention block efficiency.
##### 4. Vertical Alignment & Math Vocabulary

Consistent with the district Math Plan's emphasis on standardizing vocabulary and practices across grade levels:

- Mattison will participate in vertical alignment team collaborations to ensure consistency of mathematical language and instructional expectations from Kindergarten through Grade 4.
- Teachers will prioritize math discourse routines including turn-and-talks, error analysis, and number talks to build conceptual understanding and mathematical communication.

# Shared School-Wide Supports

The following structures support both reading and math goals and reflect the cross-cutting priorities of the district's strategic plans:

## Professional Learning Communities (PLCs)

Mattison teachers will meet weekly in grade-level PLCs that include the instructional facilitator and campus administrators. PLCs will focus on:

- Internalizing HQIMs for both ELA and math
- Analyzing student data to identify learning gaps and adjust instruction
- Collaborative planning for Tier I small groups and Tier II intervention groups
- Reflecting on implementation fidelity and professional development needs

## RTI / Multi-Tiered System of Supports

Mattison's tiered support system is structured to provide all students with the level of support they need:

- Tier I: High-quality core instruction for all students using district HQIMs with embedded differentiation
- Tier II: Targeted small group instruction during protected intervention time blocks in the master schedule; informed by screener and progress monitoring data
- Tier III: Intensive, individualized intervention for students significantly below grade level, delivered by trained interventionists using Scottish Rite programs (Build, Take Flight, Rite Flight) and Sonday as appropriate
## Child Find & Dyslexia Compliance

Mattison will actively monitor and support district compliance with Child Find mandates and state dyslexia laws, in alignment with the district's Pillar X (Sustainability & Continuous Improvement) obligations. Students showing characteristics of dyslexia will be identified and served through appropriate Scottish Rite Tier III programming.
