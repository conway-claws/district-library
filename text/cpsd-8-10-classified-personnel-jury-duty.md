# 8.10—CLASSIFIED PERSONNEL – JURY DUTY

Employees are not subject to discharge, loss of sick leave, loss of vacation time or any other penalty due to absence when called for jury duty or when subpoenaed to appear in any type of court upon giving reasonable notice to the District through the employee’s immediate supervisor.

The employee must present the original (not a copy) summons to jury duty to his supervisor in order to confirm the reason for the requested absence.
