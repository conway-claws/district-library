# Jim Stone Elementary School

# School Improvement Plan

# 2025-2026 Area of Concern

1) Jim Stone Elementary School's data shows opportunities for improvement in literacy and science achievement, with 54.76% of students achieving proficiency in ELA and only
34.92% in science. Analysis reveals limitations in academic vocabulary proficiency across content areas, particularly impacting reading comprehension, written expression, and content knowledge acquisition. With only 29.51% of students meeting science growth targets and only 43.75% of the lowest 25% meeting science growth targets, there's a clear need for integrated academic language development. The lack of systematic vocabulary instruction and limited opportunities for structured writing are contributing to these gaps, particularly affecting students in the lowest 25% growth category.
2) Building on the 2024-2025 behavior initiative, Jim Stone Elementary has made progress with the Mighty Stallion program. While behavioral referrals decreased during the 2024-2025 school year, there remains a need to deepen and expand social-emotional learning (SEL) components to address root causes of behavioral issues and promote positive school culture.
# Goal #1: Building Academic Language Through Structured Writing and Vocabulary Instruction

Increase student academic language proficiency as measured by a 6% improvement in ELA proficiency rates (ATLAS from 54.76% to 60.76% and NWEA 58.42% to 64.42%), a 10%

improvement in science proficiency rates (ATLAS from 34.92% to 44.92%), and increase the percentage of students meeting science growth targets from 29.51% to 45% through systematic implementation of structured writing and science-focused vocabulary instruction across all grade levels.

# Strategies to Meet the Goal

- Continue to implement the HQIM curriculum for Tier 1 and Tier 2 instruction and interventions
- Create interactive word walls with student-generated examples and visual supports
- Create explicit connections between ELA vocabulary instruction and science terminology
- Implement writing conferences focused on vocabulary precision and academic language use
- Implement science vocabulary notebooks with cross-references to ELA, math, and social studies
- Develop science literacy stations incorporating reading, writing, and vocabulary development
- Create authentic opportunities to apply scientific language in multiple contexts
# Professional Development

- Professional development for teachers in the area of vocabulary acquisition research and implementing engaging vocabulary strategies.
- Continuous support in vocabulary instruction and differentiation strategies for vocabulary usage.
- Develop during PLCs a writing curriculum that matches the HQIM and AR Standards.
- Provide teachers with co-teaching opportunities for structured vocabulary and writing lessons.
- Utilize PLCs to analyze CFA and CSA data to tailor Tier 2 instruction and curriculum to meet student needs.

# Goal #2: Continue Mighty Stallion Program to target behavioral and social-emotional needs

For the 2025-2026 school year, Jim Stone Elementary will strengthen and expand the Mighty Stallion program to create a comprehensive approach to student behavior and social-emotional development with the following measurable objectives:

1. Decrease overall behavior incidents by 15% from the 2024-2025 school year baseline
2. Reduce repeat referrals for the same students by 20%
3. Increase positive behavior acknowledgments by 25%
4. Implement morning meetings across all classrooms
5. Enhance staff capacity to address diverse student needs through targeted professional development
# Strategies to Meet the Goal

- Continue Mighty Stallion Leadership Team focused on: Tier 1 schoolwide supports, Tier 2/3 interventions, data analysis, and professional development
- Establish student representatives (3rd-4th grades) to provide student voice and representation.
- Dedicate 10 minutes daily for explicit SEL instruction during morning meetings
- Provide professional development on grade-level appropriate emotional regulation strategies
- Our school counselor will continue to conduct monthly classes and provide small group and individual counseling as needed.
- Continue our 4th grade D3 and Lunch Bunch (boys and girls group) to continue a mentoring program, strengthening our school character word, and targeting at-risk students.
- Develop small group interventions targeting specific skill deficits (impulse control, conflict resolution, etc.)
- Follow district protocol for classroom-level data tracking for incidents to identify patterns
- Continue monthly character award assemblies, celebrating both behavioral and SEL achievements
- Continue the Stallion Strong Cards for daily recognition of positive behavior
- Create a Mighty Stallion showcase displaying student achievements
# Professional Development Plan

- Provide professional development on trauma-informed practices for all certified staff
- Quarterly data analysis workshops during PLC meetings
- Peer observation schedule focused on SEL implementation and behavior management
