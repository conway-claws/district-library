CLE will work collaboratively with families and staff to establish an engagement plan that reflects the specific academic needs of the school and targets the specific engagement needs of the students and families. To ensure adequate and diverse representation of families during this process, a request for input will be sent to all families electronically and on paper. This request will allow families to share thoughts, concerns, and/or ideas for the betterment of CLE. With the feedback received, CLE will recruit alumni, parents, community members, faculty and staff to all be part of an advisory committee to review and plan accordingly. Throughout the year, CLE will offer multiple opportunities for parents and families to be involved in the family and school improvement committee discussions and planning. (1.1, 1.2)

## <u>2: Communication</u>

Describe how the School will communicate with and distribute information to parents and families.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***2.1:** How does the School distribute an informational packet appropriate for the age and* *grade of each child annually, ensuring to include:*
- *description of the engagement program*
- *recommended roles for parents, students, teacher, and the School*
- *ways for a family to get involved*
- *survey regarding volunteer interests*
- *schedule of activities planned throughout the school year*
- *regular, two-way, and meaningful system for parents/teachers to communicate* [*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(b)(3)(B)(i)*]
- ***2.2:** How will the School ensure information related to school and parent programs,* *meetings, and other activities is provided to parents in a format and in a language that* *parents can understand (to the extent practicable)?*
- *How is relevant information provided in a variety of ways? (For example, paper*
*copies made available, as well as social media posts, website links, parent apps, etc.?* [*ESSA § 1116(e)(5)*]

- ***2.3:** How does the School offer flexible opportunities for meetings with*
*families?* [*ESSA § 1116(c)(2)*]

CLE will distribute an informational brochure to each family at the Annual Report to the Public on August 15, 2024. The following information will be included: CLE overview, mission/vision/core values of CLE, contact information for CLE admin, CLE enrollment, CLE free & reduced statistics, how Title I funds assist CLE, standardized state test results, and ethnicity breakdowns of CLE. Additionally, families will be provided with a description of the parental/family engagement plan which includes a recommended role for parents, students, and the school, as well as ways families can get involved, a survey regarding volunteer interests, and how to successfully maintain communication with CLE teachers and staff throughout the school year. (2.1) CLE will use paper, digital, and face-to-face methods to communicate with parents. CLE will use multiple methods for communicating meaningful information including but not limited to the district and school website and Facebook page, Rooms, the campus marquee, the front office digital signage, grade level newsletters, texts, push notifications, and email. Families can access all forms of digital communication in their native language. CLE will utilize the district’s translator to translate printed communication. Teachers will regularly communicate with families through a variety of the above mentioned methods. Teachers will discuss regularly with parents about their students’ progress. CLE will provide interim reports to parents of students who are performing below grade level every five weeks. Report cards will be provided every nine weeks informing families of their students’ academic progress. Teachers will provide feedback and suggestions on how the student can continue their learning at home. Two way meaningful communication will be encouraged in order to assist every child’s success. (2.2) Parent/Teacher conferences will be held twice per year at flexible times during the morning, afternoon, and evening. Translators will be scheduled to attend conferences when the families’ native language is one other than English. In the event that families are not able to attend conferences in person, teachers and staff members will arrange conferences to be held virtually or via phone. PTO meetings will be scheduled at various times and days during the year to accommodate more parental and familial involvement. (2.3)

## <u>5: Coordination</u>

*Guiding Questions*

- ***5.1:** How does the School investigate and utilize community resources in the* *instructional program?*
[*ADE Rules Governing Parental Involvement Section 5.06*]

- ***5.2:** How does the School coordinate and integrate programs and activities with other* *Federal, State, and local programs? Some examples include:*
- *public preschool programs such as Head Start*
- *organizations/activities to help students transition to elementary, middle, high,* *and postsecondary schools or careers*
- *wraparound services that allow families to send their children to school ready and* *able to focus on learning* [*ESSA §1116(e)(4)*]

-CLE will utilize community resources in the instructional program through forming partnerships with organizations, businesses, and community members who coordinate with CLE to provide additional support and resources to families in various ways. This includes but is not limited to Central Baptist Church, Homeward Realty, Conway’s First Baptist Church, Conway High School, Centennial Bank, First Community Bank, Tio’s, Conway Fire Department, Conway Police Department, and various other community groups. CLE staff will work collaboratively to work with these partnerships as they provide resources and support for families to help assist in the child’s academic life and foster its success. Ashley Silliman, school counselor, will be in constant contact with these involved parties when needs arise in the building. Yvonne Sturdivant and Lindsey Jones, CLE Admin, will coordinate communication and work alongside community resources to enrich the students’ academics. Charity Tracker, a community tracking system, will be used to make sure the resources are being provided in an equitable manner. CLE fosters family and community involvement within the school through volunteer readers, VIP lunches, backpack program, library assistants, Open House/Report to the Public, parent trainings, and math and literacy parent workshops. (5.1) -CLE will hold preschool transition conferences for families of incoming kindergarten students who have special education support to promote a smoother transition to kindergarten. CLE administrators alongside Carl Stuart Middle School administrators will coordinate a visit for outgoing fourth grade students to visit Stuart Middle School Spring 2025. During this visit, the administrators and counselors at Stuart Middle School will provide students with an overview of fifth grade and tour the campus. The district will partner with mental health providers in order for counseling agencies to provide services to students and families that are in need and qualify during the school day. These local businesses and community organizations will provide support and resources to families. (5.2) -CLE extends an invitation for all families to join the PTO at no charge via social media, district app, email, and printed notes sent home with students regularly throughout the school year. The CLE PTO is active and will lead in the recruitment of additional volunteers to participate in appropriate decision making. Meetings will be held anywhere from three to five times per semester. (5.3)
