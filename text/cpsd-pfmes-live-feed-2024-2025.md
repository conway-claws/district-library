At the conclusion of Wampus Kitty Kinder Camp, your child will bring home a scratch off card revealing their assigned Kindergarten teacher for the school year!

August 26 - Welcome to Class!
Your child will report to their classroom to begin a wonderful year of learning with their assigned teacher.  We welcome you to walk your child to class the first day!
