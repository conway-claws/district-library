🚗 Today's Topic: Parking Lot Drop-Offs

For the safety of all our students, we do not allow parking lot drop-offs. Students walking through moving traffic without staff supervision creates a significant safety concern during our busy arrival time.

Our car rider line is designed to provide a safe, supervised location where students can exit their vehicles and begin their day. Thank you for helping us keep every Little Wampus Cat safe by using the designated car rider line each morning! 💙🐾 #WeAreCLE
