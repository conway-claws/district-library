Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street April 8, 2025

<u>Members Present</u>: Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mr. Trip Leach, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Andre’ Acklin, Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Carl Barger, Mr. Tom Kennedy

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Bryce Bennett, Gynger Campbell, Ben Darley, Kelli Gordon, Paula Harris, Eric King; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; Business Manager Stacy Rimmer; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Naylor called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video featuring Jaxson Askew, Ascending Student of the Month from Theodore Jones Elementary School. Jaxson led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Conway High School Unified Basketball State Champions – Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, introduced the Unified Basketball team State Champion team.
- CPSD Transportation Department – Blue Hear Award – Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized the transportation department for their dedication and hard work for the District and awarded them the Blue Heart Award. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:12 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mr. Acklin and seconded by Mr. Barger.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – March 11, 2025, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in March 2025
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of School Choice Applications
- Approval of E-Rate Resolution
- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline.
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- School Election Update – Superintendent Collum gave an update on the school election in May.
- Parent Night With The Superintendent – Superintendent Collum invited parents and students to Parent Night with the Superintendent on April 17 from 6:30 p.m. to 8:30 p.m.
- Graduation Plan Preview – Superintendent Collum invited Conway High School Principal Dr. Worley to give an overview of the graduation plans for the Class of 2025. Dr. Worley presented the 2025 yearbook to the Board.
- Academic Highlight Video – Superintendent Collum presented an academic video highlighting elementary science education.
## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Stacy Rimmer, Business Manager, presented the financial report for March. **Mr. Kennedy made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mr. Leach and passed unanimously at 6:40 p.m.**
- Salary Schedule – Dr. Ashley presented the revised salary schedule to the Board for approval. **Mrs. Hargis made the motion to approve the Salary Schedule, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mrs. Franklin and passed unanimously at 6:41 p.m.**
## <u>Information Items</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Dr. Andy Ashley, Assistant Superintendent, invited Amanda Townsend, School Nurse Coordinator, to present the academic report on Student Health and Wellness.
- Proposed Policy Revisions – Shasta Wagner, Chief Legal Officer, presented the proposed changes to policy 3.6 Licensed Personnel Employee Training and adopt policies 3.68 Licensed Personnel Use of Artificial Intelligence and 8.53 Classified Personnel Use of Artificial Intelligence. These policies are to be considered and voted upon at the May Board meeting.
## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 6:49 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session to consider a personnel matter. The Board exited Executive Session at 8:23 p.m.

<u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mr. Kennedy and seconded by Mr. Barger, the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 8:24 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**

- Resignations
- Elections
- Contract List

|Adjournment Respectfully submitted,|No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at 8:24 p.m. on a motion made by Mrs. Franklin and seconded by Mrs. Hargis.|
