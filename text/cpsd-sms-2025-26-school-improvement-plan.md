## 2025-2026 School Action Plan Raymond & Phyllis Simon Middle School

## <u>Area of Concern</u> (#1)

In Spring 2024, only 44% of students in grades 5–7 at Simon Middle School scored at or above grade level in reading on the ATLAS exam. This indicates a significant need for improvement in reading comprehension and fluency across all grade levels.

## <u>Goal</u> (#1)

By May 2026, the percentage of students in grades 5–7 reading at or above grade level on the ATLAS exam will increase from 44% to 50% through the implementation of evidence-based reading instruction aligned to the Science of Reading.

## <u>Strategies/Action Steps</u>

The instructional coach will participate in district-level planning and professional development sessions aligned to the SoR and the implementation of district-adopted HQIMs. Teachers will attend professional development offered by the district on curriculum and instruction aligned to the SoR and implementation of HQIM aligned to SoR. The media specialist will purchase and distribute books to students for summer reading. Administrators and the instructional coach will analyze data from the spring administration of the 2025 ATLAS assessment to determine areas of greatest strength and need. Teachers will participate in regular grade-level collaborative meetings with the instructional coach and administrators to plan and internalize instruction aligned to the SoR utilizing the Benchmark Reading and 95% Group HQIMs. The administrators and instructional coach will conduct classroom walkthroughs using DESE’s *key levers for instructional leaders* to gather evidence of implementation as it relates to the five impact areas in literacy. Administrators and instructional facilitators will review classroom walkthrough data with the teachers in order to improve instruction. Teachers will monitor and assess students’ growth in reading using CFAs and

interim assessments. In collaboration with the instructional coach and administrators, teachers will collaboratively analyze students’ performance and growth to inform instructional decisions and provide timely interventions to promote student growth. Embed reading strategies across content areas to support literacy development school-wide.

## <u>Area of Concern</u> (#2)

During the 2023–2024 school year, Simon Middle School experienced a high number of office discipline referrals, particularly those resulting in in-school suspension (ISS) and out-of-school suspension (OSS). This negatively impacts student learning time and school climate.

## <u>Goal</u> (#2)

By May 2026, Simon Middle School will reduce the total number of student ISS and OSS days by 25% compared to the 2023–2024 school year, by implementing positive behavior supports and restorative discipline practices.

## <u>Strategies/Action Steps</u>

Implement Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports (PBIS) framework with clearly defined expectations and consistent responses to behavior. Establish a restorative practices model to address conflict and repair harm. Provide Tier 2 behavior interventions for students with recurring disciplinary infractions. Analyze referral data monthly to identify patterns and respond with targeted interventions.

The administrators and counselor will attend professional development focusing on a multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support system hosted by the district. The administrators and counselor will attend the *Arkansas School Behavior* *Conference* focusing on strategies and resources for school behavior teams. The administrators will establish a behavioral leadership team to lead the processes related to the multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support system. All staff members will participate in professional development on the implementation of a multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support system. As part of this professional development, the staff will revisit the consistent schoolwide expectations for behavior across the campus (the behavior matrix). Staff members will refer students to the behavioral RTI team if they are struggling to meet behavioral expectations. Staff members will implement Tier II positive behavioral interventions as developed in conjunction with the behavioral RTI team. The assistant principal will monitor the effectiveness of the Tier II behavioral interventions in increasing positive behavior and decreasing negative behaviors. The counselor and administrators will conduct Functional Behavior Assessments (FBAs) when it is deemed necessary based on data collected and analyzed by the behavioral leadership team.
