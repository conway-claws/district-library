### 2023-11-09 · Laurel Breashears (id 4946512)

This year at Jim Stone Elementary School, all students take part in a daily 40 minute block of time that is called WIN time. WIN is an acronym that stands for "What I Need," and this time is built into their daily master schedule. It is a time set aside for teachers and staff to deliver specific instruction targeting the individual needs of each student. All students in each grade level go to an assigned classroom during their WIN time sessions to receive direct, skill-based instruction based on their individual needs. This classroom may/may not be their classroom teacher and may/may not be in their grade level. We have worked extremely hard to get WIN going at Jim Stone and that causes for celebration. Student’s hard work during WIN the 1st nine weeks is celebrated with Popsicles on the Playground. Each student received a popsicle, a scratch and sniff bookmark, and little extra time on the playground. We are so excited to see the academic results from our implementation of WIN this spring.
