If you do not use the school app:

Scroll to "Notifications" and adjust the settings for Messages and Announcements.

### 2026-02-23 · Jenna Havlik (id 11520040)

We are writing to clarify information shared in a previous email regarding BUILD: Literacy. We apologize for any confusion it may have caused.
BUILD: Literacy is not a replacement for our current reading curriculum. ### 2026-05-15 · Woodrow Cummins Elementary (id 12352340)

If your student has been receiving K-3 Literacy Tutoring this message is for you!
The Arkansas Department of Education is providing updated guidance regarding Arkansas Literacy Tutoring Grant invoicing deadlines and the proposed program closeout timeline.

Key Updates:
Students must have at least one tutoring invoice submitted by June 30, 2026, to remain active for continued tutoring services beyond June 30, 2026
Tutoring services for active students may continue throughout the summer months
The final program closeout date is September 30, 2026
Eligible invoices for active students and newly approved students may continue being submitted through September 30, 2026

Additional clarification:
Tutoring services must occur before invoices are submitted
Providers should continue submitting invoices regularly throughout the closeout period
Families and providers should not wait until September to submit invoices for completed services

Education Service Cooperatives may continue receiving questions regarding these updated timelines and expectations. We appreciate your assistance in helping reinforce accurate and consistent communication across your service areas.

If you have questions regarding Arkansas Literacy Tutoring Grant guidance, deadlines, or program procedures, please contact the Arkansas Department of Education at ade.tutoring@ade.arkansas.gov.

Thank you, 
Arkansas Department of Education Literacy Tutoring Grant Team
