MUTUAL AGREEMENT & STATEMENT OF WORK
Between Conway Public Schools and

Startup Junkie Consulting, d/b/a Innovation Junkie

The School Board of Conway Public Schools (“Client”) and Startup Junkie Consulting, LLC d/b/a
Innovation Junkie (“Consultant”) do hereby enter into this Agreement on this 8" day of July 2025. This
Statement of Work (“SOW”) shall constitute the full Agreement between the Client and Consultant.
Unless otherwise defined in this SOW, terms which are set forth in initial uppercase letters have the
meaning established for such terms as set forth in this Agreement. Consultant agrees to complete the
following project in accordance with this SOW and in accordance with the following terms:

Project Overview

This scope of this engagement will include assisting the Client in an Organizational Transformation
Program focused on the improvement of critical learning outcomes, resolving impediments to the
improvement of those learning outcomes, leadership effectiveness, organizational effectiveness,
financial performance, and other high-value outcomes.

Wrapped around this engagement will be regular Advisory Services designed to assist the Board, the
Superintendent, and other members of the School District's Central Administration and Campus
Leadership in the cultivation of a Culture of Excellence, leading to the generation of improved results for
the School System. In addition to the items listed above, Consultant will assist the School District in the
creation of a multi-year, stakeholder-driven Strategic Plan to position Conway Public Schools as a
nationally competitive school district using widely accepted measures and metrics. Consultant will also
be available for coaching, consulting, and guidance to address “just in time” issues throughout the
engagement. The effective dates of this engagement shall be for a 12 month period beginning 8 July
2025.

Deliverables by Consultant

1. Work with the Superintendent to reset expectations among the Central Administration and
Campus Leadership that CPSD will adopt and pursue a Culture of Excellence across the entire
district, with specific focus on Strong Leadership, Clarity and Focus, 100% Accountability, among
other elements.

2. Develop and execute a short-term, medium-term, and long-term Organizational Transformation
Program in partnership and collaboration with the Board and Superintendent, to drive targeted
improvements in critical learning outcomes and other high-value areas as described above.

3. Conduct a Leadership Assessment of Central Administration and other District Leaders to assist
in putting the right people over the right targets to drive rapid improvement in multiple
expected outcomes.

4. Provide targeted Leadership Development for members of the Central Administration and
Campus Leadership and other designees, as needed, during the engagement period.

Fees/Payment Structure:

Payments for this Statement of Work shall be in the form of 6 monthly installments, at $9,000 each, with
the first installment being due upon execution of this agreement (on or about July 10, 2025).
Subsequent payments shall be due by the 10" of each subsequent month, with the last installment due
on December 10" for work ending 12/31/25. If the Board wishes to extend this engagement for an
additional six months, at the same scope, the monthly retainer will be extended for the revised term.

All payments shall be made by electronic ACH deposits, direct to the Startup Junkie Consulting, LLC
d/b/a Innovation Junkie account. Upon execution of this Mutual Agreement and SOW, a single invoice
for the total engagement amount will be sent, including appropriate banking information for monthly
ACH payments.

If it is determined that the Client shall require additional effort or output by Consultant beyond this
statement of work, Consultant shall communicate clearly to Client any additional fees that might apply
and gain approval from Client before engaging in such additional effort.
