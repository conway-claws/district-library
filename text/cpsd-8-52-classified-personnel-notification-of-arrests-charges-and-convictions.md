## 8.52 - CLASSIFIED PERSONNEL NOTIFICATION OF ARRESTS, CHARGES, AND CONVICTIONS

The purpose of this policy is to set the expectation that all employees must report arrests, charges, and convictions to the Conway School District (“District”) in order to promote safety and security, mitigate potential risks, and to maintain the integrity of the District.

Unless circumstances arise that would prevent notification, all employees of the District must disclose to the District any arrest, charge, or conviction that he or she receives while employed by the District. Minor traffic infractions do not need to be reported, unless the infraction occurs in a school owned vehicle.

Disclosure must be made in writing to the employee’s direct supervisor and the Office of Human Resources concerning the circumstances surrounding the arrest, charges, or conviction within three (3) calendar days of the arrest, charge, or conviction occurring. There may be circumstances that arise that would extend the notification timeline, and these circumstances will be approved on a case-by-case basis. Disclosure alone will not automatically result in disciplinary action. Disclosure may result in an investigation into the conduct that gave rise to an arrest, charge, or conviction. If an investigation occurs, then upon the completion of the investigation the District will determine if any disciplinary action is warranted.

Failure to disclose an arrest, charge, or conviction will result in disciplinary action up to and including a recommendation of termination to the Superintendent.

This policy will not be construed or applied in a manner that would violate federal or state law.

This policy will not discharge an employee’s obligation to comply with Policy 8.28 - Drug Free Workplace-Classified Personnel.
