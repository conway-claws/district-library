Exciting News for Our Middle Schools! We will begin implementing Yondr pouches on November 4, 2024. Students will receive their own Yondr pouch to take home on that morning.

What does this mean for students? To create a focused and distraction-free environment, all cell phones, smart watches, and AirPods will be secured in these special bags during school hours. Students are required to bring their Yondr pouches every morning and place their devices inside before entering the school. The bags will remain with them, but all devices must be turned off during school hours. At the end of the day, students will unlock their Yondr pouches as they exit the school and will keep their pouches with them. This initiative will help foster learning, connection, and engagement—without the screens!

Important Reminder: Violating district policy regarding the possession of cell phones can lead to disciplinary action. Students who tamper with the Yondr pouch, repeatedly forget to bring their pouch, damage or take another student's Yondr pouch, or use their phone without authorization during the school day may face consequences ranging from warnings and phone confiscation to detentions, Saturday school, or in-school suspension. Additionally, restitution may be required to replace a Yondr pouch.

SPECIAL RECORDS TO BE DESTROYED
On July 7, 2025, the Conway Public School District will destroy Special Education records of students who graduated, moved or were dismissed from Special Education services in the district between January 1, 2019 and June 30, 2019.
