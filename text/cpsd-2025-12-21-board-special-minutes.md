Minutes of Board of Education Special Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street December 21, 2025

<u>Members Present</u>: Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Assistant Superintendent Bryce Bennett; Directors Gynger Campbell, Preston Echols, Kelli Gordon, Eric King, Shanda New; General Counsel Shasta Wagner; Strategic Planning Consultant Jeff Standridge; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m.

Board President Franklin led the audience in the Pledge of allegiance.

Dr. Jeff Standridge, Strategic Planning Consultant, presented a review of the candidate search process.

## <u>Executive Session</u>

The Board entered Executive Session at 5:07 p.m. for the purpose of employment.

The Board exited Executive Session at 7:35 p.m.

**Mr. Cummings III made the motion to appoint Mr. Jason Black as the Superintendent of Conway** **School District effective January 1, 2026 and authorize Dr. Standridge to work with President Franklin** **and Mr. Bequette to finalize the contract. The motion was seconded by Ms. Walton and approved at** **7:36 p.m. on a 4-3 vote. Voting for the motion were Mrs. Franklin, Mr. Cummings III, Ms. Walton, and** **Mr. Leach. Voting against the motion were Dr. Naylor Jr., Mrs. Hargis, and Dr. Petty.**

<u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at 7:38 p.m.** **on a motion made by Dr. Petty and seconded by Mr. Cummings.**

Respectfully submitted,
