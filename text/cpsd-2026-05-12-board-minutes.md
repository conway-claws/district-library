Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street May 12, 2026

<u>Members Present</u>: Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty, Mrs. Ashley Lyon

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jason Black; Deputy Superintendent Dr. Karen Lasker; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Bryce Bennett; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Preston Echols, Kelli Gordon, Shawn Higginbotham, Eric King, Shanda New; General Counsel Shasta Wagner; Strategic Planning Consultant Dr. Jeff Standridge; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board Vice President Walton called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Champion Student Recognition</u>

Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, introduced Cole Britt, Champion Student of the Month and Valedictorian from Conway High School. Superintendent Black presented him with a certificate. Cole led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Purple Star Campuses-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Director Preston Echols to introduce John Kaminar, Arkansas Department of Education, to recognize the following schools as Purple Star Schools: Conway High School, Conway Junior High School, and Raymond and Phyllis Simon Middle School.
- AY Magazine Best of Schools-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Michelle Daugherty, AY Magazine representative, to recognize the district for being named one of the top three Best of 2026 School Districts from AY Magazine and informed the District that we were the overall winner.
- Jacob Smith Recognition-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized Jacob Smith, Maintenance Department, for his accomplishment of winning a vehicle during the “Hands on a Hyundai” Toad Suck Daze competition.
- Conway Educational Aspiring Leadership-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Director Kelli Gordon to introduce the following CPSD employees that graduated from the Conway Educational Aspiring Leadership program for the 2025-2026 academic year: Melissa Authement, Ben Broyles, Todd Dooley, Konley Dye, Kristen Ellis, Melinda Francis, Melinda Fraser, Natalie Horton, Deisha Jolivette, Zachary Noland, Aja Ramsay, Kim Raup, Kendra Rimlinger, Nicole Rogoff, Erica Russell, Brook Sanchez, Macaela Seabolt, Stacey Spearmon, Kristy Stroman, Amanda Townsend, Cady Vaughn, Cory Walker, and Erin Wesbecher.

Superintendent Black read Board member Mr. Trip Leach’s resignation letter to the public. Board member Mrs. Sheila Franklin resigned Monday, May 11, 2026. Superintendent Black thanked them for their dedication and service to the District.

## <u>Patrons Comments</u>

- Jimmie Cavin – Mr. Cavin spoke to the Board about accountability and Freedom of Information Act laws.
- Audie Alumbaugh – Ms. Alumbaugh spoke to the Board about her concerns with funding for students traveling expenses and the district needing to be child focused.
- Julie Hill – Mrs. Hill addressed her concerns that she feels is the demise of the district to the Board.
- Tyler Moses – Mr. Moses spoke to the Board about his concerns that he feels the doors are not open to patrons when asking for clarity, transparency, and FOIA requests.
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Board Appointment Process – Superintendent Black invited Mrs. Wagner, General Counsel, to explain the Board member appointment process. **Mrs. Hargis made the motion to change the June Board meeting to July 6, 2026 to hold** **Board member position interviews. The motion was seconded by Dr. Petty and passed unanimously at 6:52 p.m.**
- Teacher Appreciation – Superintendent Black thanked the community for their outpouring of food, treats, and support to the teachers during Teacher Appreciation week. He thanked the district’s teachers for all they do for the students and district.
- CPSD Appreciation – Superintendent Black thanked all staff and Board members for their dedication and hard work for the students and District.
- Strategic Advisor – Superintendent Black recommended consideration of continuing Dr. Jeff Standridge’s consultant contract work for another six months and gave the Board a thirty- day read to vote at the July meeting. **Mrs. Hargis made the motion to have a thirty-day read on the extension of Dr.** **Standridge’s consultant contract and vote on it at the next meeting in July. The motion** **was seconded by Dr. Petty and passed unanimously at 7:06 p.m.** <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 7:09 p.m. on a motion made by Mr.** **Cummings III and seconded by Mrs. Hargis.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – April 14, 2026, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in April 2026
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petition for Transfer of Students
- Approval of School Action Plans
- Approval of Policy Revisions

- Approval of Section 4579b) Eligible Deferred Compensation Plan Board Resolution
## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Shawn Higginbotham, Director of Finance, presented the financial report for April. **Dr. Petty made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion was** **seconded by Mr. Cummings III and passed unanimously at 7:13 p.m.**
## <u>Information Items</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Dr. Bryce Bennett and Director Shanda New presented updates on the Virtual Pathway and end of year data.
- Bob and Betty Courtway Middle School Renovation Update – Assistant Superintendent Dr. Andy Ashley presented a renovation update.
- Proposed Policy Revisions – Shasta Wagner, General Counsel, presented the proposed changes to Policy Section 4. These policies are to be considered and voted upon at the July Board meeting.
- 2026-2027 Student Handbook for Review-Shasta Wagner, General Counsel, presented the revisions that were made to the student handbook for the Board to consider and vote upon at the July Board meeting.
- 2026-2027 Athletic Handbook for Review-Shasta Wagner, General Counsel, presented the revisions that were made to the athletic handbook for the Board to consider and vote upon at the July Board meeting.
## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 7:35 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session for the purpose of employment and litigation purposes. The Board exited Executive Session at 8:04 p.m.

<u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mr. Cummings III and seconded by Mrs. Hargis, the Board approved** **the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent on a 4-1 vote at 8:05 p.m.,** **as presented in the agenda. Voting for the motion were Ms. Walton, Mr. Cummings III, Mrs.** **Hargis, and Mrs. Lyon. Voting against the motion was Dr. Petty.**

- Resignations
- Elections <u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at** **8:05 p.m. on a motion made by Dr. Petty and seconded by Mr. Cummings, III.** Respectfully submitted,
