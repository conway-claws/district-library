Jace Talent Agency.

This is the 2nd year for Addison Choate to be named a Star Attorney. The tournament awards the top 5 witnesses, and Annabel Champlin and Naomi Brooks brought home 2 of the Star Witness Awards. 

The CHS team will be the team to represent Arkansas at the National High School Mock Trial Championships in May. They will travel to Des Moines, IA, to compete against the top team from each state, along with South Korea, Guam, and Northern Mariana Islands. 

All members of the CHS Mock Trial Team are members of the CHS Speech & Debate Team and are coached by Mrs. Casey Griffith. The team is also coached by volunteer attorney coach, Mr. Matt Brown.
