Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street June 11, 2024

<u>Members Present</u>: Mr. André Acklin, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Trip Leach, Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Bill Milburn

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Ben Darley, Kelli Gordon, Greg Hughes, Eric King; Chief Financial Officer Robyn Keene; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Acklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Conway High School Boys Soccer Team-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Athletic Director Hughes to introduce the Conway High School Boys Soccer Team and recognize them for winning the state championship for the third time.
- AY’s Best of 2024 for Public School – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented to Board AY magazine’s award of being the Best of 2024 for Public School.
- U.S. News & World Report Best High Schools-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a plaque to the Board for receiving Best High Schools recognition for Conway High School. Superintendent Collum led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:10 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Mrs. Franklin.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – May 14, 2024, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in April 2024
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of 2024-2025 Statement of Assurances
- Approval of Policy Revisions
- Approval of Act 1599 Resolutions o Ericka French/Central Arkansas Professional Surveying (CAPS) o Carrie Nabholz/A & B Dirt Movers
- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline.

## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Board Member Vacancy Process Review – Superintendent Collum invited Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner to brief the Board on the Board member vacancy process to fill the position of Zone 1, Position 4 due to Board member Jason Sandefer resigning May 24, 2024.
- District Goals Workshop Reminder – Superintendent Collum reminded the Board of the upcoming work session on August 7, 2024.
## <u>Chief Financial Officer’s Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Robyn Keene presented the financial report for May. **Dr. Naylor, Jr. made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mr. Leach and passed unanimously at 6:20 p.m.**
## <u>Information Items</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Duncan gave an update on the early childhood program, Fueling Brains.
- 2024-2025 Student Handbook for Review – Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner presented the revisions that were made to the student handbook for the Board to consider and vote upon at the July 2024 Board meeting.
- 2024-2025 Athletic Handbook for Review – Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner presented the revisions that were made to the handbook for the Board to consider and vote upon at the July 2024 Board meeting.
- Athletics End-of-Year Report – Athletic Director Greg Hughes recapped the accomplishments of the sports teams during the 2023-2024 school year. <u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mr. Milburn and seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr., the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 6:30 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**
- Resignations
- Elections Board President Acklin asked for a brief recess at 6:34 p.m.
## The Board reconvened at 6:41 p.m.

## <u>School Board Vacancy Zone 1, Position 4 Candidate Interviews in Open Meeting</u>

Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner led the Board in the interview process to fulfill the vacant position. Mr. Carl Barger was interviewed for the position.

Board President Acklin asked for a brief recess at 7:18 p.m.

## The Board reconvened at 7:23 p.m.

The Board interviewed Ms. Millie Goins and Dr. Barrett Petty.

## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 8:01 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session to consider candidates for the vacant Board Zone 1 position. The Board exited Executive Session at 8:50 p.m.

**Mrs. Hargis made the motion to appoint Carl Barger as Board member for Zone 1, Position 4** **to serve until the annual school election following the appointment at which time a member** **will be elected in the usual manner to serve the remainder of the unexpired term. The motion** **was seconded by Mr. Milburn and passed at 8:51 p.m. on a 5-1 vote. Voting for the motion** **were Mr. Acklin, Dr. Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Hargis, Mr. Leach, and Mr. Milburn. Voting against the motion was Mrs. Franklin.**

<u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at** **8:53 p.m. on a motion made by Dr. Naylor, Jr and seconded by Mrs. Hargis.**

Respectfully submitted,
