## Target Goal:

Mattison Elementary will increase the percentage of students meeting and/or exceeding grade level standards in reading in each grade level by five percentage points.

## Strategies:

Mattison Elementary will continue to use a variety of supports and systems that have been acquired for use through district leadership. Specific vision will be promoted in the following areas.

- Tier II Targeted Small Groups
- The school master schedule contains a grade level Tier II time for teachers to work with small groups of students using the Amplify curriculum which is targeted towards the student’s learning goal in reading. This block of time will be 50 min in length. All Interventionists will pull their Tier III groups during those times.
- Teachers will use BOY screeners, as well as, Amplify benchmark measure and progress monitoring data to target their instruction. These will identify the gaps in phonological awareness, phonics, comprehension, vocabulary, and fluency in order to provide the best explicit individualized instruction.
- HQIM 95% Group Phonics-Grades K-4 will fully implement the 95% Group curriculum.
- HQIM Benchmark Reading-Grades K-4 fully implement the Benchmark Reading curriculum.
- Teachers will meet weekly for PLC:
- Mattison teachers will work collaboratively within their grade level team including the IF and administrators to internalize HQIM lessons in 95% Group and Benchmark.
- A focus will be placed on the continuous improvement/growth for collaborative teams to implement strong tier one instruction utilizing our HQIM’s, reflecting on data, and adjusting Tier I small groups accordingly.
- Teachers will analyze progress monitoring data and lessons to adjust Tier II small groups utilizing Amplify data.

## <u>Area of Concern:</u>

Mattison students have shown an increase in behavioral issues that include a lack of problem solving skills and social skills.

<u>Target Goal:</u> Student discipline referrals will decrease by 10% from the previous year.

## Strategies:

- Teachers will implement a school wide behavior plan.
- Teachers will make positive phone calls home.
- Classes will receive counseling lessons monthly.
- The school counselor will utilize our UCA counseling students to support staff parents and students.
- The school counselor will pull small groups to target behavior needs.
- The school counselor will implement the Teacher Empowerment and Mentorship (T.E.A.M) to serve students that need extra support.
- Administrators and school counselor will consult with our campus school psych to support teachers, parents, and students.
- Staff will collaborate with our Pinnacle Point and Counseling Associate team members.
- Back to school behavioral professional development
## <u>Implementation Plan:</u>

- RtI meetings are conducted to discuss students who need further support with behavior.
- Campus X-tra mile behavior program will be implemented.
- Nine week rewards will be conducted with a behavioral motivation component.
- Teacher Empowerment and Mentorship (T.E.A.M) will be implemented to serve students that need extra support.
## <u>Professional Development:</u>

K-4 teachers will receive training on behavior supports in August 2024 to understand the impact it has on behavior/learning in our schools.

## <u>Evaluation:</u>

- Behavior data will be reviewed to determine areas of concern or patterns of behaviors.
- Follow up meetings with classroom teachers, counselor, and administrators for progress monitoring
## <u>Area of Concern:</u>

Mattison Elementary School has been on the state special education monitoring list. Through partnerships with ADE and district leadership we have been able to receive 1003 grant money for staff professional development. Our data shows that student achievement among our special education students is lacking in consistent growth.

## <u>Target Goal:</u>

Mattison Elementary will show growth in our special education population

## <u>Strategies:</u>

- Special education teachers and general education teachers will collaborate to incorporate best practices for all students.
- A focus will be placed on the continuous improvement for collaborative teams to implement strong tier one instruction, provide common formative assessments, reflect on data, and pre-planning instructional time to respond to the learning.
