Minutes of Board of Education Special Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street August 26, 2025

<u>Members Present</u>: Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Interim Superintendent Jason Black; Interim Deputy Superintendent Dr. Karen Lasker; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Bryce Bennett; Directors Gynger Campbell, Preston Echols, Eric King; General Counsel Shasta Wagner; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m.

Interim Superintendent Black led the audience in the Pledge of allegiance.

## <u>Patrons Comments</u>

- Jimmie Cavin – Mr. Cavin spoke to the Board about the Legislative Audit that was filed over the maintenance department investigation.
- Tami Marsh – Ms. Marsh spoke to the Board about the maintenance department investigation and the superintendent search. Interim Superintendent Black addressed the Board and public about misinformation circulating in the public. He stated that it is important that we operate with facts, transparency, and unity so that our community has an understanding of the situation. The investigation was performed by a third party investigator and our private auditor. Once our general counsel consulted with Legislative Audit about the process, she acted decisively and expeditiously. The District has submitted all required documents and is fully meeting it’s obligations. He asked Mrs. Wagner to present a timeline on the investigation process. Mrs. Wagner, General Counsel, presented a timeline on the investigation process and the submission of the investigation to Legislative Audit. Interim Superintendent Black stated that the Board unanimously, in open session, approved personnel at the June 10, 2025 meeting. He also stated neither Mrs. Wagner or myself were present in executive session when personnel discussions took place prior to that vote. To imply or otherwise state that General Counsel or myself have in any way attempted to cover up, mislead, or lie to the public, the Board, or anyone else is simply wrong. CPSD’s teachers, students, and families deserve leadership that is focused on the future. Board members discussed the investigation timeline and stated that the Board’s decision was not carried out by the administration. Board members asked Mrs. Wagner questions about

their concerns. Board President Franklin reminded the Board that was the prior administration and a vote was never taken in public as is required.

Board Member Dr. Naylor, Jr. stated he had reached out to Representative Mary Bentley to ask for guidance in the process of Legislative Audit. He stated he was told the Board doesn’t have to vote to get a Legislative Audit and that Representative Bentley would take this forward on September 11, 2025 to the Legislative Audit Committee. The Committee will make a ruling as to whether it goes forward.

## <u>Superintendent Search Discussion</u>

Board President Franklin invited Dr. Jeff Standridge to discuss the superintendent search and asked for a motion to use him as the search consultant.

**Mrs. Hargis made a motion to use Dr. Jeff Standridge as the search consultant for the** **superintendent’s position. The motion was seconded by Ms. Walton and passed at 6:55 p.m.** **Dr. Petty abstained from voting.**

Dr. Jeff Standridge presented to the Board a proposed Superintendent Search process and timeline.

Board Members and Dr. Standridge discussed position description, salary range, committee structure, charter, selection range, timeline, and overall process.

## Mrs. Hargis made a motion to approve the position description and salary with total

**compensation range between $275,000 - $300,000. The motion was seconded by Ms. Walton and passed unanimously at 7:28 p.m.**

## Dr. Petty made a motion to approve the committee structure, charter, and selection process

## as amended. The motion was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 7:32 p.m.

## Dr. Petty made a motion to approve the timeline, overall process, and recruitment plan as

## amended. The motion was seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr. and passed unanimously at 7:42 p.m.
