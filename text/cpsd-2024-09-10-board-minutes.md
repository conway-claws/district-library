Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street September 10, 2024

<u>Members Present</u>: Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mr. Trip Leach, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Andre’ Acklin, Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Carl Barger

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Ben Darley, Kelli Gordon, Eric King; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Naylor called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video featuring Brayden Williams, Ascending Student of the Month from Ruth Doyle Middle School. She welcomed Principal Matt Kelly, who introduced Brayden. Superintendent Collum presented Brayden with a certificate. Brayden led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Office for Education Policy (OEP) Awards – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized the following: Principal Yvonne Sturdivant, Carolyn Lewis Elementary School, for Best Growth Scores in ELA Central Region and Best Overall Growth Scores Statewide Elementary School Level; Principal Carise Echols, Theodore Jones Elementary School, for Beating The Odds in ELA Statewide Elementary Level; Principal Robert Yates, Bob and Betty Courtway Middle School, for Beating The Odds in Math Central Region Middle Level; Principal Marquis Rogers, Raymond and Phyllis Simon Middle School, for Best Growth Scores Overall Statewide Middle School Level; and Principal Preston Echols, Conway Junior High School, for Best Growth Scores in Math Central Region High School Level and Best Growth Scores Overall Statewide High School Level.
- NICHE Award – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized the District for being one of the top 10 best school districts in Arkansas by NICHE.
- Board Member Recognition – Superintendent Collum recognized Mr. Andre’ Acklin for serving as Board President from 2022-2024. Superintendent Collum and Board members thanked Mr. Acklin for his service.
- Honoring of Board Member Bill Milburn – Superintendent Collum presented Mrs. Milburn with a plaque honoring Board Member Bill Milburn after his passing last month. Board members offered condolences to Mrs. Milburn. Superintendent Collum asked for a moment of silence to honor Mr. Milburn.
