Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street July 8, 2025

<u>Members Present</u>: Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Interim Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Bryce Bennett, and Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Preston Echols, Kelli Gordon, Greg Hughes, and Eric King; General Counsel Shasta Wagner; Business Manager Stacy Rimmer; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Board President Franklin called for a moment of silence for the victims of the flood in Texas.

Interim Superintendent Black led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Board Member Recognition – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized Dr. Naylor, Jr. for serving as Board President from 2024-2025. Interim Superintendent Black thanked him for his service to the District.
- Video Series – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented the next video in the series, Novice Teachers.
## <u>Patrons Comments</u>

- Tyler Moses – Mr. Moses spoke to the Board about process, transparency, and Arkansas law.
- Alyce Hardee – Ms. Hardee spoke to the Board about the Deputy Superintendent position and asked the Board not to approve the position tonight until the position has been publicly posted. Interim Superintendent Black and General Counsel Mrs. Wagner answered questions from Board members about items on the Consent Agenda. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:33 p.m. on a motion made by** **Ms. Walton and seconded by Dr. Petty.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – June 10, 2025, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of Minutes – June 25, 2025, Special Board Meeting

- Approval of Minutes – June 29, 2025, Special Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in June 2025
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of Bus Lease
- Approval of Arch Ford Coop Addendum
- Approval of 2025-2026 Student Handbook
- Approval of 2025-2026 Athletic Handbook
- Approval of Policy Revisions
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

Interim Superintendent Black addressed the Board and public. He thanked the team for their hard work and dedication and the Board for their communication, leadership, and trust. He is confident that the District can be unified. He thanked Dr. Collum for his leadership and service to the District. He talked about how special and great the District is because of all the employees that make it work every day and thanked them all. To the students, staff, parents, and guardians, you are our priority!

- Back to School Convocation – Interim Superintendent Black shared Convocation plans on August 14
th.

- Back to School – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations shared plans for back to school.
## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- District Financial Report – Dr. Andy Ashley presented the financial report for June. **Dr. Naylor, Jr. made the motion to approve the financial report for June, as presented.** **The motion was seconded by Ms. Walton and passed unanimously at 7:00 p.m.**
## <u>Action Item</u>

- Adoption of Resolution for School Board/Chamber Steering Committee Process and Structure – Interim Superintendent Black presented to the Board the Resolution to adopt the School Board/Chamber Steering Committee Process and Structure Resolution and invited Brad Lacy, Conway Area Chamber of Commerce, to speak about the process. **Mrs. Hargis made a motion to adopt the Resolution for the School Board/Chamber** **Steering Committee Process and Structure, as presented. The motion was seconded by Dr.** **Naylor, Jr. and passed unanimously at 7:05 p.m.**
## <u>Information Items</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Bennett presented the academic report on Strategic Reading Plan Development.
- CPSD Organizational Chart – Assistant Superintendent Lasker presented the Organizational Chart.

- Central Office Staffing – Assistant Superintendent Lasker presented an update on Central Office staffing and Human Resources.
- Approval of Policy 1.19 Board Member Length of Term and Holdovers – Mrs. Shasta Wagner, General Counsel, presented Policy 1.19 Board Member Length of Term and Holdovers to the Board and explained the new law that states Districts have to choose either four-year or six-year terms. **Dr. Petty made a motion to adopt six-year terms. The motion was seconded by Ms.** **Walton and passed at 7:56 p.m. on a 4-3 vote. Voting for the motion were Mrs. Franklin,** **Ms. Walton, Mr. Cummings III, and Dr. Petty. Voting against the motion were Mr. Leach,** **Dr. Naylor, Jr. and Mrs. Hargis** Board President Franklin asked for a brief recess at 7:57 p.m.
## The Board reconvened at 8:03 p.m.

- E. Conway Junior High Facility Update – Assistant Superintendent Ashley gave an update on the water damage at the junior high school that occurred on July 1, 2025 from a broken pipe.
- Proposed Policies 1.25 Creation of FTE’s and 7.5 Purchases And Procurement – General Counsel Shasta Wagner presented the proposed Policies 1.25 Creation of FTE’s and 7.5 Purchases and Procurement to the Board to consider and vote upon. After discussion, Mrs. Wagner made the proposed changes to Policy 1.25 that the Board requested. **Dr. Petty made a motion to approve policies 1.25 and 7.5 with waving of the 30 day read,** **as presented. The motion was seconded by Ms. Walton and passed unanimously at 8:15 p.m.**
- Strategic Planning Consultant – Interim Superintendent Black invited Dr. Jeff Standridge to present his proposed engagement agreement to the Board. Dr. Standridge explained his plan for consulting services to help the Superintendent and the Board. **Mrs. Hargis made a motion to approve the District Strategic Planning Consultant, Dr. Jeff** **Standridge. The motion was seconded by Ms. Walton and passed unanimously at 8:36 p.m.** <u>Executive Session</u> **Dr. Petty made a motion to enter Executive Session to discuss a personnel matter. The** **motion was seconded by Mr. Cummings III and passed unanimously at 8:36 p.m.** The Board exited Executive Session at 9:58 p.m.
