Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street October 8, 2024

<u>Members Present</u>: Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mr. Trip Leach, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Andre’ Acklin, Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Carl Barger

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Ben Darley, Eric King; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Naylor called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video featuring Kamila Matute, Ascending Student of the Month from Ida Burns Elementary School. She welcomed Principal Amy Howell, who introduced Kamila. Superintendent Collum presented Kamila with a certificate. Kamila led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- 2024 National Blue Ribbon School – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized Woodrow Cummins Elementary School as a 2024 National Blue Ribbon School. Dr. Collum thanked Dr. Lewis and her teaching staff for their hard work.

- Approval of Minutes – September 10, 2024, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of Minutes – September 16, 2024, Special Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in September 2024
- Approval of Purchase Orders

## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Dr. Andy Ashley presented the financial report for September. **Mrs. Hargis made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mr. Leach and passed unanimously at 6:21 p.m.**
## <u>Information Item</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Duncan presented the academic report on effective classroom management strategies for student success.
## <u>Action Item</u>

The motion was seconded by Mr. Barger and passed unanimously at 6:29 p.m.** <u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mr. Leach and seconded by Mrs. Franklin, the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 6:29 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**

## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 6:56 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session to review Board member candidate applications for interviews. The Board exited Executive Session at 7:32 p.m.

## <u>School Board Vacancy Zone 5, Position 3 Candidate Interviews</u>

Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner led the Board in the interview process to fulfill the vacant position. The Board interviewed Trey Geier, Wesley Scroggin, Thomas Kennedy, Leona Walton, and Jerry Rye.

## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 8:45 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session to consider candidates for the vacant Board Zone 5 position. The Board exited Executive Session at 9:26 p.m.

**Mr. Barger made the motion to appoint Thomas Kennedy as Board member for Zone 5,** **Position 3 to serve until the annual school election following the appointment at which time a** **member will be elected in the usual manner to serve the remainder of the unexpired term.** **The motion was seconded by Mr. Leach and passed at 9:28 p.m. on a 5-1 vote. Voting for the** **motion were Dr. Naylor, Jr., Mr. Leach, Mrs. Hargis, Mr. Acklin, and Mr. Barger. Voting against** **the motion was Mrs. Franklin.**

<u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at** **9:29 p.m. on a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Mr. Barger.**
