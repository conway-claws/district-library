## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

Board President Naylor called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

- Board Member Recognitions – Deputy Superintendent Jason Black recognized Dr. David Naylor, Jr. for receiving the Master Board Member award from ASBA for earning at least 50 hours of Board development training. Mr. Acklin was recognized for serving on the Board for the last fifteen years. Mr. Barger was recognized for serving on the Board for this past year.

2025.
