Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street September 12, 2023

<u>Members Present</u>: Mr. André Acklin, Mr. Jason Sandefer, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Trey Geier

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Clint Ashcraft, Ben Darley, Kelli Gordon, Paula Harris, Keisha Jefferson, Eric King, Jason Lawrence, Dr. Tammy Woosley; Chief Financial Officer Robyn Keene; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Acklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Superintendent Collum led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Superintendent Collum asked for a moment of silence for the loss of Rebecca Kelly, Conway High School.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- The Pine Street Backpack Program – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized The Pine Street Backpack Program for serving the community for years in distributing backpacks and supplies to students.
- CPSD Board Members and Employees – Community Awards and Recognitions – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized the following for their recognitions and awards in the community: Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Physician Of The Year; Sheila Franklin, Person Of The Year; Miranda Sligh, Educator Of The Year; Dr. Karen Lasker, Person Of The Month; and Dayna and Mark Lewis, Couple Of The Month.
- U.S. Education NICHE Award – Superintendent Collum presented the Board with a plaque from NICHE for recognition of being one of the best school districts in America. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:12 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:

- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in August 2023

## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Enrollment Update – Superintendent Collum presented an update on student enrollment.
## <u>Information Items</u>

- City Wide Safety Drill – Deputy Superintendent Black presented a review of the City Wide Safety Drill preformed and thank everyone from the community and district that participated.
- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Dr. Megan Duncan continued with Mr. Black’s safety drill update.
- Junior High Athletic Teams Update – Athletic Director Ashcraft gave the Board an update on discussions of combining the Junior High Athletic Teams.
- K12 Insight Update – Assistant Superintendent Dr. Karen Lasker presented the Board with an update from the K12 Insight stakeholder groups.
## <u>Action Items</u>

- Solar Eclipse Day Calendar Adjustment April 8, 2024 – Assistant Superintendent Dr. Megan Duncan presented a revision to the school calendar to change the planned professional development flex day from April 29 to April 8 to accommodate the festivities in town concerning the Solar Eclipse. Students will not be in session on April 8
th with this change.

**Mr. Leach made the motion to approve the adjustment to the calendar for April 8** **th** **Solar** **Eclipse Day. The motion was seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr. and passed unanimously at 7:08 p.m.**

- Board Financial Report – Chief Financial Officer Robyn Keene presented the financial report for August. **Mrs. Franklin made the motion to approve the financial report for August, as presented.** **The motion was seconded by Mr. Leach and passed unanimously at 7:10 p.m.**
- Review and Approval of 2023-2024 Operating Budget – Chief Financial Officer Robyn Keene presented the 2023-2024 Operating Budget to the Board. **Mr. Sandefer made the motion to approve the 2023-2024 Operating Budget, as** **presented. The motion was seconded by Mr. Geier and passed unanimously at 7:22 p.m.**
- Approval of the 2022-2023 Annual Financial Report – Chief Financial Official Robyn Keene presented the 2022-2023 Annual Financial Report to the Board. **Mr. Leach made the motion to approve the 2022-2023 Annual Financial Report. The** **motion was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 7:24 p.m.**

<u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mr. Sandefer and seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr., the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 7:29 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**

- Resignations
- Elections
## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 7:33 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session to consider a personnel matter. The Board exited Executive Session at 8:58 p.m.

<u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at** **8:58 p.m. on a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Dr. David Naylor, Jr.**
