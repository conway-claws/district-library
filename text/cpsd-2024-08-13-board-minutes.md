Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street August 13, 2024

<u>Members Present</u>: Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mr. Trip Leach, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Andre’ Acklin, Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Carl Barger

## <u>Members Absent</u>: Mr. Bill Milburn

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Ben Darley, Paula Harris, Eric King; Chief Financial Officer Robyn Keene; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Naylor called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- 2024 Safe Schools Educational Professional of the Year – Superintendent Collum recognized Deputy Superintendent Jason Black for receiving the 2024 Safe Schools Educational Professional of the Year award.
- State Finalist for Teacher of the Year – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized Abby Roberts as one of the four state finalists for Teacher of the Year.
- Board Member Recognition – Superintendent Collum recognized Mr. Andre’ Acklin for serving as Board President from 2022-2024. Superintendent Collum and Board members thanked Mr. Acklin for his service. Superintendent Collum led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:10 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mr. Barger and seconded by Mrs. Hargis.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – July 9, 2024, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in July 2024
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of Act 1599 Resolutions o Angela Rogers/Angela Rogers o Reed Kelley/Reed Kelley
- Approval of Governor Sanders Cell Phone Pilot Program and DESE Grant
