# 3.12—LICENSED PERSONNEL VACATIONS AND HOLIDAYS

1. All full-time twelve month employees shall be allowed vacation time with full pay at the rate of one (1) day per contracted month. Vacation eligibility shall begin with the first day of employment. The maximum number of days accrued shall be ten (10).
2. The vacation time earned for a school year must be taken by August 15th of the following calendar year (which would include two (2) summers). There shall be no accumulation of vacation time beyond this date.
3. Permission for vacation shall be obtained by submitting a request through the employer’s immediate supervisor to the superintendent.
4. The superintendent may deny an employee’s request for a vacation day if such absence would, in the superintendent’s opinion, be disruptive to the educational process.
5. In the event of extenuating circumstances, the superintendent may grant an exception to any of these policies.
Date Adopted: May 12, 1987 Last Revised: June 12, 2007
