### 2024-10-28 · Ruth Doyle Middle School (id 7339470)

Exciting News for Our Middle Schools! We will begin implementing Yondr pouches on November 4, 2024. Students will receive their own Yondr pouch to take home on that morning.

What does this mean for students? To create a focused and distraction-free environment, all cell phones, smart watches, and other wearable tech will be secured in these special bags during school hours. Students are required to bring their Yondr pouches every morning and place their devices inside before entering the school. The bags will remain with them, but all devices must be turned off during school hours. At the end of the day, students will unlock their Yondr pouches as they exit the school and will keep their pouches with them. This initiative will help foster learning, connection, and engagement—without the screens! Students will be able to access their devices at the conclusion of the school day. 

Important Reminder: Violating district policy regarding the possession of cell phones can lead to disciplinary action. Students who tamper with the Yondr pouch, repeatedly forget to bring their pouch, damage or take another student's Yondr pouch, or use their phone without authorization during the school day may face consequences ranging from warnings and phone confiscation to detentions, Saturday school, or in-school suspension. Additionally, restitution may be required to replace a Yondr pouch.

We will also be participating in a survey of students as part of a program supported by Governor Sarah Huckabee Sanders and the Office of Education Policy. This survey aims to gather valuable insights into the effectiveness of our new policies and the overall school experience. Consent forms will be sent home soon, so please return them to your student's third period teacher by November 8, 2024.

Let’s work together to enhance our students’ educational experience!

### 2025-06-23 · Conway Public Schools (id 9409538)

SPECIAL RECORDS TO BE DESTROYED
On July 7, 2025, the Conway Public School District will destroy Special Education records of students who graduated, moved or were dismissed from Special Education services in the district between January 1, 2019 and June 30, 2019. This process is carried out in compliance with federal and state laws. Students, parents and/or guardians may obtain any data from folders prior to July 7, 2024, by calling the Special Education office at (501) 450-6634, extension 0303 or emailing at records@conwayschools.info.
