## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Special Award to the District-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Megan Williams, Associated Builders and Contractors of Arkansas to present an Award of Excellence for the Sallie Cone Pre-K Renovation by Nabholz Construction. Mr. Cunningham also introduced Drum Majors Keri Brewington and Garrett Butler.
- Band Alumni Spotlight – Conway High School Band Director Nathan Cunningham highlighted Alumni student William Rowe, who is currently a Drum Major at the University of Arkansas.

## Action Item

- Approval of 2024-2025 School Calendar – Assistant Superintendent Karen Lasker presented the proposed 2024-2025 School Calendar. Two calendar options had been approved by the Personnel Committees. Both options followed all rules and regulations required by statute. The options were presented to the staff for a vote. Out of 893 responses, Option A received
95.9 % of the votes while Option B received 4.1% of the votes. **Dr. Naylor, Jr. made the motion to approve Option A, as presented, as the official calendar** **for the 2024-2025 school year. The motion was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 6:53 p.m.**
