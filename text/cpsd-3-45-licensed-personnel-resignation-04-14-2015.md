# 3.45—LICENSED PERSONNEL RESIGNATION

A licensed employee has the privilege of resigning from the Conway Public School District upon thirty (30) days’ notice.

When a licensed employee resigns from the school district before the close of the year, the immediate supervisor shall be responsible for seeing that all records and other necessary items are turned in to him/her following the same procedure that is required of all other licensed employees at the end of the year.

Date Adopted: June 12, 1979 Last Revised: April 14, 2015
