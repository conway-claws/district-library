## <u>1: Jointly Developed</u>

Describe how the School works with parents and families to develop and review relevant plans, policies, and strategies related to engagement.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***1.1:** How does the School-in collaboration with parents-establish an engagement plan that* *reflects the specific academic improvement needs of the School, and that includes programs* *and practices that enhance engagement and address the specific engagement needs of* *students and their families?*
[*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(a)*]

- ***1.2:** What efforts have been made to ensure adequate representation of parents and families* *of participating children in the process (Title I families)?*
[*ESSA § 1116(c)(3)*]

- Woodrow Cummins’ Parent and Family Engagement Coordinator meets with school administrators, PTO representatives, parents, and teachers in the fall to review the current parent and family engagement plan, and address new needs of our current students and families. This meeting will take place on September 13, 2024. During this meeting, the stakeholders will discuss last year’s successes and what changes need to be made for the upcoming school year to better accommodate our changing student and family population. New goals to enhance family and student engagement are suggested, discussed, and added to the current parent and family engagement plan. At the conclusion of the 2024-2025 academic year, the plan will be reviewed in order to suggest deletions/additions/suggestions for the 2025-2026 year.
- The Parent/Family Engagement Plans are posted online on our school website. Parent and Family Engagement meetings are advertised in the school newsletter, teacher classroom newsletters, and social media outlets to encourage attendance. Meetings are open to any school stakeholder who wants to attend. Parents are able to make comments and suggestions pertaining to planning and improvement of the Parent/Family Engagement Plan.
## <u>2: Communication</u>

Describe how the School will communicate with and distribute information to parents and families.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***2.1:** How does the School distribute an informational packet appropriate for the age and grade* *of each child annually, ensuring to include:*
- *description of the engagement program*
- *recommended roles for parents, students, teacher, and the School*
- *ways for a family to get involved*
- *survey regarding volunteer interests*
- *schedule of activities planned throughout the school year*
- *regular, two-way, and meaningful system for parents/teachers to communicate* [*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(b)(3)(B)(i)*]
- ***2.2:** How will the School ensure information related to school and parent programs, meetings,* *and other activities is provided to parents in a format and in a language that parents can* *understand (to the extent practicable)?*
- *How is relevant information provided in a variety of ways? (For example, paper copies* *made available, as well as social media posts, website links, parent apps, etc.?* [*ESSA § 1116(e)(5)*]
- ***2.3:** How does the School offer flexible opportunities for meetings with families?*
[*ESSA § 1116(c)(2)*]

- WCE will distribute the Lion's Den weekly newsletter to parents that is developed with participation of the parent school organization, principal, and staff. It includes school news, a calendar of school activities, and parenting tips related to school achievement such as homework tips, organizational skills, and study skills.
- Every fall, a parent guide will be provided to our families via email and a copy will be added to Woodrow Cummins Elementary’s school website. A paper copy can be provided if needed. The parent guide will also be translated into Spanish. The parent guide will include:
- description of the engagement program
- recommended roles for parents, students, teacher, and the school
- ways for a family to get involved
- survey regarding volunteer interests
- schedule of activities planned throughout the school year
- regular, two-way, and meaningful system for parents/teachers to communicate
- Conway School District has a district website to house school websites for each school. Information about homework assignments and pertinent school/classroom information will be available on the school website.
- Parents may use email to communicate with members of the school staff.
- Each teacher will send home a folder containing student papers and work samples each week. Parents will be asked to sign the folder and send it back to school.
- Teachers will routinely contact parents on an individual basis to communicate about their child's progress.
- WCE PTO's will provide a volunteer sign-up sheet regarding volunteer interests.

- WCE will provide to parents reports/report cards every nine weeks with information regarding their child's academic progress and upcoming classroom and school events.
- WCE will send parents a parent friendly letter in the first report card that explains their child's test results and standardized test scores.
- WCE will offer parents a special workshop each year on the school's approach to school improvement.
- WCE will send brochures home with students, post notices in school facilities and public buildings.
- WCE will use the student handbook, school website, signage at the school entrance, and parent orientation meetings to give parents school information
- Teachers will strive to meet with parents face-to-face but will offer phone conferences in order to accommodate parents’ schedules. If needed, teachers will schedule a translator to ensure a clear understanding on how to meet the needs of the whole child.
- WCE Staff will encourage parents to use the CPSD app and Rooms to obtain current information about the school and/or district.

WCE is committed to providing ongoing academic and emotional support to the families of our students.

- Teachers will hold conferences individually with parents of children in their classrooms. Parents will be given a summary of the student's test scores and an explanation of the interventions teachers are using to assist the child in reaching achievement goals. Parents will be asked to engage in discussion of how they can support these efforts. Parents will also be given suggestions for coordinating school and parent efforts and explanations of homework and grading procedures.
- WCE will offer parents a "Report to the Public" each year to provide an explanation of the statewide assessment system, standards, and other accountability measures.
- WCE PTO will provide volunteer opportunities to parents throughout the academic year. WCE will encourage parents to participate in the following activities to increase their involvement and support for student learning: Watch Dog Dads, Monthly Parent/Family Lunches, Grandparents Days, Book Fairs, Running Club, Mighty Lion 5K, Field Days, Family Nights, Grade Level Orientations, Open House, PTO, Red Ribbon week, Choir concerts, Summer Reading Programs, and various PTO committees.
