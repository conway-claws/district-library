Minutes of Board of Education Special Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street June 29, 2025

<u>Members Present</u>: Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty <u>Members Absent</u>: None <u>Others Present</u>: Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Directors Preston Echols and Eric King; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. Board President Franklin led the audience in the Pledge of allegiance.

# Ms. Walton made a motion to enter Executive Session to discuss a personnel matter. The

# motion was seconded by Dr. Petty and passed unanimously at 6:01 p.m.

[Mr. Cummings III entered meeting at 6:19] The Board exited Executive Session at 8:44 p.m.

# Dr. Naylor, Jr. made a motion to approve the Separation Agreement between the District and

# Superintendent Dr. Collum in the amount of $461,000. The motion was seconded by Mr.

**Cummings III on a 5-2 vote at 8:45 p.m. Voting for the motion were Mrs. Franklin, Ms.** **Walton, Mr. Cummings III, Dr. Naylor, Jr., and Mrs. Hargis. Voting against the motion were Mr. Leach and Dr. Petty.**

Board President Franklin read a statement to the public about the Voluntary Separation Agreement between the School Board and Superintendent Dr. Collum. Board Members spoke about the separation agreement.

# Ms. Walton made a motion to approve and appoint Deputy Superintendent Jason Black as

# Interim Superintendent effective immediately. The motion was seconded by Mr. Cummings III

# and passed at 8:49 p.m. Voting for the motion were Mrs. Franklin, Ms. Walton, Mr.

# Cummings III, Mr. Leach, Dr. Naylor, Jr., and Mrs. Hargis. Dr. Petty abstained from voting.

Board President Franklin stated that the Board intends to engage a consultant to advise the Board on leadership of the district and the consultant will work with Mr. Black. The job of Superintendent will be posted and a formal search will be conducted in the future. Board members thanked the public and staff for their input on concerns of the District. <u>Adjournment</u>

|Respectfully submitted,|No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at 8:58 p.m. on a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Mr. Leach.|
