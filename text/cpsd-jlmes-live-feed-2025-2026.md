Dear Families,

Over the next few weeks, your second grader will participate in a districtwide assessment. Each spring, Conway School District screens all second grade students using a nationally normed measure that evaluates students’ reasoning abilities in verbal, quantitative, and nonverbal areas.

This assessment helps us better understand each student’s learning strengths and academic potential. The results are one of several data points we use to determine whether a student may benefit from further evaluation for gifted and talented services. There is nothing you need to do to prepare, and testing will take place during the regular school day.

If you have any questions, please contact the Gifted and Talented teacher at your child’s school. There is also more information about GT services on the district website.

Thank you for your continued support.
