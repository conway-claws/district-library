## Goal

Based on the STAR Assessment, currently, 51% of students in third grade are reading below grade level. Fifty-five percent of students in fourth grade are performing below grade level on the STAR Assessment. In Kindergarten, 35% of students are below benchmark on NWEA. Forty-four percent of first grade students are below benchmark on NWEA. In second grade 66% of students were below benchmark on NWEA. We will look at the Benchmarks of our new State Assessment for K-2 to determine what our goal will be for the 2024-2025 school year. By the end of the 2024-2025 school year, 75% of students in Third and Fourth grade will grow on their STAR assessment by 8 months or more.

## <u>Strategies</u>

- All grade level teachers will implement 95% group Phonics with fidelity. Teachers will track student progress through weekly phonics assessments.
- Teachers will use screeners, as well as, informal assessments to target their instruction. They will be able to know exactly what gaps in phonological awareness and phonics students have in order to provide the best explicit individualized instruction.
- These assessments will be analyzed on a regular basis and adjustments in instruction will be made according to what the data shows. Teachers will keep Data notebooks with student anecdotal notes, checklists, accuracy checks, and other informal assessments along with screeners and formal assessment measures.
- Teachers in all grade levels will provide Tier II intervention for Literacy during RTI time daily, as well as pull out intervention available with school Interventionists.
- Teachers will teach the essential lessons from Benchmark Reading as outlined in unit plans.
- Teachers will communicate with families to support reading instruction at home.
- RISE-Grades K-4 will fully implement and follow RISE, including the use of decodable texts to target specific reading levels.
- Teachers will meet weekly during PLC to collaborate within grade level to implement strong Tier I instruction, provide common formative assessments, reflect on data, and pre-plan for instruction.

## Professional Development

Our teachers will attend Curriculum Planning days facilitated by our District instructional Facilitators. During this planning time they will be gaining knowledge about our district Tier I curriculum for Literacy (Benchmark Reading, 95% Phonics, and Writing). This allows for our teachers to understand the pacing of our curriculum and to understand how to teach it with fidelity. It also allows for planning among our district grade level teams.

Our RTI Team analyzes data on struggling students every 4-6 weeks in order to make sure that we are serving all students to the best of our ability. This allows us to see if a student is not making progress, or if another intervention is needed in order to help them move to the next part of their learning.

Based on the needs of students, Admin and the Instructional Facilitator will determine if additional learning is needed for the teacher, in order to best meet the student needs.

## <u>Area of Concern</u>

Students at Ida Burns have shown an increase in behavioral issues while at school. We have seen a diverse range of needs, and a lack of problem solving skills.

## <u>Goal</u>

We would like to see the number of referrals sent to the office decrease 10% from the previous year. We would also like to see the number of referrals as a repeated offense decrease by 10%.

## <u>Strategies</u>

- Teachers will implement a school wide behavior plan.
- Teachers will make positive phone calls home quarterly.
- Teachers will participate in professional development focused on trauma.
- Teachers will implement a “Calm Down corner.”
- A discipline committee will meet quarterly to discuss school wide behavior.
- A positive behavior reward system will be implemented.
- Classes receive counseling lessons monthly.
- Ida Burns Staff will plan and conduct two parents nights to include our families in a social environment.
- Monthly awards ceremonies.
- Behavior flowchart.
- VIP Lunch for Students with good behavior
- Collaboration with Pinnacle Point and Counseling Associates team members
## <u>Professional Development</u>

Teachers will receive updated training in ACT 1084 during summer PD 2024. In this professional development, teachers will use understanding of the past year to help improve upon building wide strategies, as well as classroom strategies they can use for behavior management.
