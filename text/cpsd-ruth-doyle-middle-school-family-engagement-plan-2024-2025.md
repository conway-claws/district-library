## <u>1: Jointly Developed</u>

Describe how the School works with parents and families to develop and review relevant plans, policies, and strategies related to engagement.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***1.1:** How does the School-in collaboration with parents-establish an engagement plan that* *reflects the specific academic improvement needs of the School, and that includes programs* *and practices that enhance engagement and address the specific engagement needs of* *students and their families?*
[*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(a)*]

- ***1.2:** What efforts have been made to ensure adequate representation of parents and families* *of participating children in the process (Title I families)?*
[*ESSA § 1116(c)(3)*]

In May and June of each year, a committee of stakeholders will continue to establish and improve upon the family engagement plan for the following school year. The stakeholders will include parents representation from each grade level. A needs assessment and survey are developed during this May-June time period. The survey will be directed and communicated to parents of students attending RDMS during the 2024-2025 school year. This committee will review past practices and develop new opportunities to foster effective parental involvement to improve student engagement, belonging, and achievement. Regular meetings throughout the 2024-2025 school year with members of our school’s parent-teacher association and school faculty will take place to plan, review, and improve parent and family engagement programs. A needs assessment is developed

- Needs assessment utilizing feedback from parents and families.
- Annual parent and family feedback data will be collected and reviewed by committee members to gauge, reflect, and improve our parent and family engagement plan.
