# 3.14.1—LICENSED PERSONNEL LEAVE ― COURT SUBPOENAED

Licensed personnel subpoenaed to appear in any type of court shall be allowed to serve without loss of pay or without being required to use a personal leave day. The employee must present the original (not a copy) subpoena to his/her supervisor in order to confirm.

Date Adopted: June 12, 1979 Last Revised: June 12, 2007
