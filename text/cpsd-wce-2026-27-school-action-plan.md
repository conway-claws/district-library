## Woodrow Cummins Elementary

## School Improvement Plan

## 2026-2027

## <u>Area of Concern</u> 7/SA

Literacy is an area of need at Woodrow Cummins Elementary. Student achievement data from the Spring 2025 administration of the ATLAS assessment indicate 47% of third-grade students scored at a proficient level and 51% of fourth-grade students scored at a proficient level.

## <u>Goal 7/SA</u>

By May 2027, Woodrow Cummins Elementary will increase 3rd grade ELA proficiency from 47% to 57% and 4th grade ELA proficiency from 51% to 61% on the ATLAS summative assessment, through full implementation of the newly adopted district Tier 1 HQIM and a strengthened, data-driven multi-tiered intervention system aligned to the Science of Reading.

## <u>Strategies/Action Steps</u>

- Implement the newly adopted district Tier 1 HQIM with fidelity district-wide, supported by professional development and instructional coaching aligned to the Science of Reading.
- Use Atlas universal screener data (3x/year) to identify students for Tier 2 and Tier 3 intervention and ensure timely, proactive placement.
- Implement district-aligned Tier 2 interventions: Build for K-2 and Rite Flight for grades 3-4, with Take Flight for Tier 3 students with dyslexia or intensive reading needs.
- Facilitate monthly grade-level data PLCs in which teachers collaboratively review progress monitoring data and co-plan targeted instruction.
- Conduct regular classroom walkthroughs to monitor fidelity of Tier 1 HQIM implementation and provide feedback to teachers.
- Strengthen family engagement through a Family Literacy Night, parent-friendly Atlas reports, and provide Individualized Reading Progress (IRP) reports.
- Embed cross-curricular literacy strategies in Science and Social Studies to reinforce reading skills across content areas.
- The instructional coach will participate in district-level C&I planning and Science of Reading professional learning to stay aligned with the district strategic plan.
## <u>Professional Development</u>

- August 2026: District-led professional development for all teachers and administrators on the newly adopted Tier 1 HQIM, including lesson internalization, Science of Reading alignment, and differentiation strategies.
- Ongoing: Grade-level team planning sessions led by the instructional coach focused on internalization of daily HQIM lessons.
- Ongoing: Reading interventionist continue professional development in Take Flight and Rite Flight implementation.

- Ongoing: Instructional coach participates in district C&I planning sessions and external Science of Reading professional learning opportunities.

## <u>Area of Concern</u> 7/TA

Math is another area of need at Woodrow Cummins Elementary. Student achievement data from the Spring 2025 administration of the ATLAS assessment indicate 60% of third-grade students scored at a proficient level and 58% of fourth-grade students scored at a proficient level.

## Goal 7/TA

By May 2027, Woodrow Cummins Elementary will increase 3rd grade math proficiency from 60% to 68% and 58% to 66% on the ATLAS summative assessment, through consistent implementation of Eureka Math squared, target Math Intervention Plans for Level 1 students, and strengthened data-driven Tier 2 support structures.

## <u>Strategies/Action Steps</u>

- Implement Eureka Math² in grades K-4 with fidelity, using aligned pacing guides and instructional coaching to support high-quality Tier 1 instruction for all students.
- Develop and implement individualized Math Intervention Plans (MIPs) for all students who scored at Level 1 on the 2025-2026 ATLAS, in accordance with Arkansas Code Annotated § 6-17-431.
- Use Eureka Math Equip diagnostic data to identify specific skill gaps and tailor Tier 2 small-group instruction to target prerequisite skills.
- Implement learning ladders (proficiency scales) in grades 3-4 to clarify learning progressions for teachers, students, and families.
- Facilitate monthly math data PLCs in which teachers collaboratively review formative and interim assessment data and adjust instruction accordingly.
- Conduct regular walkthroughs to monitor fidelity of Tier 1 math instruction and support teachers in implementing math discourse routines (number talks, turn-and-talks, error analysis).
- Specifically target the five identified district skill deficit strands — especially fractions, geometry vocabulary, and data literacy — within Grades 3 and 4 instruction and intervention.

- Begin identifying 4th grade students who demonstrate readiness for the Honors Math pathway (launching in Grade 5 in 2026-2027) and communicate with families accordingly.
- Communicate learning progress to families through learning ladders published on the district website and parent-friendly math resource materials.
## Professional Development

- August 2026: District-led professional development emphasizing conceptual understanding, vertical alignment, and effective instructional strategies within Eureka Math².
- Ongoing: Instructional coaches participate in district Math Coalition meetings and relevant professional learning opportunities focused on math discourse, intervention, and content knowledge.
- Ongoing: Grade-level PLCs focused on lesson planning, data analysis, and skill deficit targeting aligned to the five district math strands.
- Spring 2027: Teachers in 4th grade participate in preparation for Honors Math pathway alignment ahead of the 2027-2028 implementation.
