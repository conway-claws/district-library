# 3.05—LICENSED PERSONNEL CONTRACT RETURN

An employee shall have ten (10) days from the date of the receipt of his/her contract for the following school year in which to return the signed contract to the office of the Superintendent. The date of receipt of the contract shall be presumed to be the date of a cover memo or receipt of the employment contract via electronic mail.

Failure of an employee to return the signed contract to the office of the superintendent within ten (10) days of the receipt of the contract shall operate as a rejection of the offer of employment by the employee. No further action on the part of the employee, the Superintendent, or the Conway Public Schools Board of Education shall be required in order to make the employee’s rejection of the offer of employment final.
