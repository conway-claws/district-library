## 2024-2025 School Action Plan Julia Lee Moore Elementary School

## I. <u>Area of Concern</u>

Writing in response to reading is one area of need at Julia Lee Moore. Perceptual data from teachers and other staff members indicate they need to improve their ability to plan and provide explicit writing instruction in response to a variety of texts. Student writing samples reveal lack of mastery as it relates to writing in response to reading.

## II. <u>Goal</u>

For the 2024-2025 school year, the staff at Julia Lee Moore Elementary will focus on improving students' performance in writing, specifically focusing on writing in response to reading. All teachers will provide students with effective instruction in the areas of planning, revising, and editing writing in response to reading. Additional support will be provided through paraprofessionals, classroom teachers, support staff, and interventionists.

## III. <u>Strategies</u>

The instructional facilitator will participate in district-level planning and professional development targeting writing in response to reading. Two teachers from Julia Lee Moore will attend teacher-leader training for Benchmark Education for planning writing instruction in response to the texts from the Benchmark reading curriculum. The teacher leaders who attend Benchmark Education training will provide support to all staff on how to plan instruction focusing on writing in response to reading using texts from the Benchmark reading curriculum. Teachers will attend curriculum and instruction professional development during the summer which will include unit planning for writing.

Administrators and the instructional facilitator will analyze data from the spring administration of the ATLAS assessment to determine areas of greatest need. In collaboration with administrators and the instructional facilitator, teachers will collaboratively and individually analyze data from the ATLAS assessment to inform instructional decisions. Teachers will participate in weekly grade-level collaborative planning meetings with the instructional facilitator to plan writing lessons. Teachers will plan and implement lessons across the curriculum targeting writing in response to various texts. The administrators and instructional facilitator will visit classrooms (to conduct walkthroughs) to gather observational data during writing instruction. Administrators and instructional facilitators will review classroom walkthrough data with the teachers in order to make further plans or adjustments. Teachers will assess students’ abilities to write in response to reading. In collaboration with the instructional facilitator and administrators, teachers will collaboratively score students’ writing samples to identify overall patterns and trends to address through classroom instruction as well as individual students’ needs.

## I. <u>Area of Concern</u>

The implementation of high-quality instructional materials (HQIM) with fidelity is another need at Julia Lee Moore. Teachers and interventionists have expressed the need to deepen their understanding of how to implement HQIM with fidelity. Teachers have indicated they feel they need further training to successfully use HQIM to plan and implement effective instruction using Benchmark Reading (reading), 95% Group Core Phonics (phonics), Eureka Math Squared (math), and Sonday (dyslexia intervention).

## II. <u>Goal</u>

For the 2024-2025 school year, the staff at Julia Lee Moore Elementary will engage in focused efforts to improve student growth and achievement in the areas of reading, phonics, and math using HQIM. Teachers will plan and implement lessons using the following HQIM: Benchmark Reading, 95% Group Core Phonics, and Eureka Math Squared. Interventionists will plan and implement tier III dyslexia instruction using the Sonday System.

## III. <u>Strategies</u>

The instructional facilitator will participate in district-level planning and professional development training on using Eureka Math Squared with fidelity. Administrators will attend professional development on how to support the

implementation of HQIM, Eureka Math Squared. Two teachers from Julia Lee Moore will attend teacher-leader training for Benchmark Education. The teacher leaders who attend Benchmark Education training will provide support to all staff on how to plan instruction in reading and writing using the HQIM, Benchmark reading curriculum. Teachers will attend professional development sessions at Arch Ford co-op on how to implement HQIM with fidelity including 95% Group Core Phonics, Benchmark Reading, and Eureka Math Squared. K-4 teachers will also attend curriculum and instruction professional development during the summer which will include using HQIM with fidelity. Intervention teachers will attend collaborative planning meetings during summer PD to collaborate with other dyslexia intervention teachers to refine their knowledge and practices of implementing the Sonday System with fidelity. Administrators and the instructional facilitator will analyze data from the spring administration of the ATLAS assessment and NWEA to determine areas of greatest need in reading, ELA, and math. In collaboration with administrators and the instructional facilitator, teachers will collaboratively and individually analyze data from the ATLAS assessment and the NWEA assessment to inform instructional decisions. Teachers will participate in weekly grade-level collaborative planning meetings with the instructional facilitator to plan phonics, reading/writing, and math lessons using HQIM including 95% Group Core Phonics, Benchmark Reading, and Eureka Math Squared. Teachers will implement phonics, reading/writing, and math lessons using HQIM (95% Group Core Phonics, Benchmark Reading, and Eureka Math Squared). Intervention teachers will implement the Sonday System with fidelity to serve Tier III dyslexia intervention. The administrators and instructional facilitator will visit classrooms (to conduct walkthroughs) to gather observational data regarding the implementation of HQIM with fidelity. Administrators and instructional facilitators will review classroom walkthrough data with the teachers in order to determine next steps and/or make adjustments. Teachers will assess students’ abilities in phonics, reading, writing, and math to monitor students’ achievement and progress. The instructional facilitator will maintain an electronic data wall to monitor each student’s progress in the areas of reading and math.
