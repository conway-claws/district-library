## 2025-2026 School Action Plan Julia Lee Moore Elementary School

## <u>Area of Concern</u> (#1)

Literacy is one area of need at Julia Lee Moore. Student achievement data from the Spring 2024 administration of the ATLAS assessment indicate 59% of third-grade students scored at a proficient level and 63% of fourth-grade students scored at a proficient level.

## <u>Goal</u> (#1)

The staff members at Julia Lee Moore Elementary will implement effective instruction aligned to the Science of Reading (SoR) utilizing high-quality instructional materials (HQIM) with integrity to increase the number of students performing at grade-level proficiency in ELA by 3% as evidenced by summative, interim, and common formative assessments (CFAs) by the end of the 2025-2026 school year.

## <u>Strategies/Action Steps</u>

The instructional coach will participate in district-level planning and professional development sessions aligned to the SoR and the implementation of district-adopted HQIMs. Teachers will attend professional development offered by the district on curriculum and instruction aligned to the SoR and implementation of HQIM aligned to SoR. The media specialist will purchase and distribute books to students for summer reading. Administrators and the instructional coach will analyze data from the spring administration of the 2025 ATLAS assessment to determine areas of greatest strength and need. The media specialist and instructional coach will plan and host a family/grandparent night highlighting literacy. Teachers will participate in regular grade-level collaborative meetings with the instructional coach and administrators to plan and internalize instruction aligned to the SoR utilizing the Benchmark Reading and 95% Group HQIMs.

The administrators and instructional coach will conduct classroom walkthroughs using DESE’s *key levers for instructional leaders* to gather evidence of implementation as it relates to the five impact areas in literacy. Administrators and instructional facilitators will review classroom walkthrough data with the teachers in order to improve instruction. Teachers will monitor and assess students’ growth in reading using CFAs, DIBELS/Amplify, and interim assessments. In collaboration with the instructional coach and administrators, teachers will collaboratively analyze students’ performance and growth to inform instructional decisions and provide timely interventions to promote student growth. The media specialist and literacy interventionist will organize and host a guest reading day for community members and families to share stories with students.

## <u>Area of Concern</u> (#2)

A multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support system is another area of concern at Julia Lee Moore Elementary. Perceptual data from staff indicate the need for further training on how to consistently implement a multi-tiered system to promote positive behavior. eSchool data from the 2024-2025 school year reveal 38 discipline incidents during the first quarter, 14 discipline incidents during the second quarter, and 16 incidents during the third quarter.

## <u>Goal</u> (#2)

The staff members at Julia Lee Moore will implement a multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support system to decrease the number of behavior referrals as measured by quarterly discipline incident data by 3% by the end of the 2025-2026 school year.

## <u>Strategies/Action Steps</u>

The administrators and counselor will attend professional development focusing on a multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support system hosted by the district. The administrators and counselor will attend the *Arkansas School Behavior* *Conference* focusing on strategies and resources for school behavior teams. The administrators will establish a behavioral leadership team to lead the processes related to the multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support system. All staff members will participate in professional development on the implementation of a multi-tiered positive behavior intervention and support system. As part of this professional development, the staff will revisit the consistent schoolwide expectations for behavior across the campus (the behavior matrix). All staff members will explicitly explain, teach, role play, and reinforce schoolwide and classroom expectations as outlined on the behavior matrix as part of the Tier I component of the multi-tiered positive behavior and intervention support system. Classroom teachers and the counselor will provide instruction on prosocial skills to all students as part of the Tier I component of the multi-tiered positive behavior and intervention support system. Staff members will refer students to the behavioral RTI team if they are struggling to meet behavioral expectations.

Staff members will implement Tier II positive behavioral interventions as developed in conjunction with the behavioral RTI team. The assistant principal will monitor the effectiveness of the Tier II behavioral interventions in increasing positive behavior and decreasing negative behaviors. The counselor and administrators will conduct Functional Behavior Assessments (FBAs) when it is deemed necessary based on data collected and analyzed by the behavioral leadership team. The administrators will collaborate with the PTO to host JLM Jam Sessions as positive reinforcement activities for students who meet expectations.
