# <u>Ellen Smith Elementary School Improvement Plan: 2025–2026</u>

Goal #1: Literacy Improvement

**S.M.A.R.T. Goal:** *By May 2026, Ellen Smith Elementary will increase the percentage of students reading on grade level by 5%* *as measured by ACT Aspire summative assessments, Amplify benchmarks, Accuracy Checks, Progress* *Monitoring, ATLAS Interims, and classroom common formative assessments (CFAs).* **Area of Concern:** Reading at grade level is the weakest component of our ESSA score. Amplify and interim data show stagnation in 2nd and 3rd grade, and inconsistent Tier II interventions in the classroom.
## Strategies:

1. Continue implementation of High-Quality Instructional Materials (HQIM) aligned to the Science of Reading (SoR), with a focus on lesson internalization and instructional fidelity.
2. Deliver explicit phonics (K–3) and morphology (3-4) instruction aligned with Amplify and 95%.
3. Provide data-driven small group instruction targeting foundational skills, fluency, comprehension, and vocabulary.
4. Incorporate Sustained Volume Reading and Familiar Reading Book Boxes in all classrooms.
5. Require written responses to reading to strengthen comprehension and writing fluency.
6. Embed, explicit vocabulary and reading comprehension strategies across content areas.
7. Strengthen fluency through modeling, repeated readings, and goal setting.
8. Collaboration with the library/media specialist to:
- Distribute free summer reading books
- Organize guest reader events, reading challenges and book fair
- Curate engaging texts aligned with science and social studies topics
- Promote library use and independent reading habits through Read-A-Thon, Caught You Reading Awards, Page Turners (home reading incentive) and Blind Date with a Book Challenge
9. Expand family literacy engagement through:
- Biweekly home literacy tips (<u>English</u>/<u>Spanish</u>)
- Take-home summer books for every student
- Family literacy nights and classroom reading routines

10.Invite guest readers into classrooms each school year to promote the joy of reading and community connection.
11.Deliver strategic summer professional development on foundational reading instruction, small-group design, lesson internalization and SoR-aligned practices.
## Implementation Timeline:

- **Summer 2025** – PD on HQIM, internalization, and small groups
- **August 2025** – Classroom routines and book boxes in place
- **September 2025**- Explicit small groups begin
- **Sept–May** – Implementation, data monitoring, and coaching cycles
- **Quarterly** – Family literacy events, benchmark data reviews

Goal #2: Behavior and Student Engagement

**S.M.A.R.T. Goal:** *By May 2026, Ellen Smith Elementary will reduce office discipline referrals by 10% and increase student* *engagement scores on climate surveys by 10%, as measured by discipline data reports and student feedback.* **Area of Concern:** Behavioral referrals and disengagement are reducing instructional time and creating inconsistent learning environments, especially in classrooms with high-need students.

<u>Top 4 High-Impact Engagement Strategies</u>:

1. Student Goal-Setting & Data Tracking
2. Instructional Engagement Protocols (Turn & Talk, response boards, collaboration)
3. Teacher-Student Check-Ins (2x10 strategy)
4. Classroom Jobs & Leadership Roles
## Additional Strategies:

- Brain breaks & movement-based learning
- High-interest, culturally relevant content
- Clear, consistent classroom routines
- Engagement-focused recognition systems
## Protecting Instructional Time:

- Minimize transitions and class removals
- Use reflection tools and calming resets
- Monitor disruptions using a time loss tracker
- Apply Tier II supports and follow behavior matrix before removal
#### Comprehensive Counselor-Led Behavior & Engagement Support

- **Tier II/III Intervention Coordination:**
- Counselor will collaborate with teachers and the RTIb team to ensure individualized plans are developed, monitored, and adjusted for students with repeated behavioral or attendance concerns.
- Maintain a tracker of Tier II and Tier III student interventions, check-ins, and outcomes to be reviewed at biweekly RTIb meetings.
- **Counselor-Led Class Visits:**

- Implement proactive classroom SEL mini-lessons or check-ins targeting key skills such as emotion regulation, coping strategies, kindness, and empathy.
- Schedule grade-level rotation with built-in time for feedback from teachers on class climate and specific student needs.
- **Mentorship Program:**
- Develop a counselor-led mentorship system connecting high-risk students with a consistent adult mentor (teacher, staff member, or administrator).
- Mentorship logs and student feedback collected.
- **Family Outreach & Intervention Planning:**
- Counselor will lead family meetings/phone conferences for Tier II/III behavior or attendance concerns to co-design support strategies, share progress, and build home-school partnerships.
- **Behavior and Culture Data Analyst:**
- Counselor will prepare summary reports disaggregating referral and attendance data by subgroup, grade level, and trend area to guide planning and interventions.
- Present findings in PLCs, RTI(b) and leadership team meetings.

## Implementation Details:

## Professional Learning & Teacher Leadership Rollout

- **What**: Selected teacher leaders, administrators, and the school counselor will attend summer professional learning on school wide behavior systems, SEL supports, Tier II and III supports, benefits of brain breaks and engagement strategies. They will return to provide building-wide training focused on engagement, de-escalation strategies, and trauma-informed classroom practices.

- **Who**: Teacher Leaders, Admin, Counselor (summer cohort); all certified staff participate in building-level PD.
- **When**:
- **Summer 2025** – Off-site training for selected staff; Administrator led book study on engagement, <u>Cultivating a Classroom of Calm</u>
- **August 13, 2025** – Full staff training on engagement, de-escalation, and trauma-informed classrooms
- **Ongoing** – Follow-up learning during PLCs and staff meetings; Teacher leaders to share learning in PLC
## Consistent Classroom Routines

- **What:** Each class will implement clear procedures for transitions, attention signals, expectations, and consequences. Staff will provide families a copy and turn in rules, routines and procedures along with rewards and consequences to administrators.
- **Who:** Teachers; Administrator and Counselor monitor during observations.
- **When:** Taught explicitly during the first two weeks of school and revisited monthly. **Instructional Engagement Protocols**
- **What**: Teachers will implement daily engagement protocols such as Turn & Talk, response boards, and collaborative learning tasks to ensure active participation.
- **Who**: All teachers; modeled and supported by Instructional Coach. **Classroom Jobs & Leadership Roles**
- **What**: Students are assigned consistent classroom jobs and rotating leadership roles to promote ownership, belonging, and responsibility.
- **Who**: Teachers assign and monitor roles; students apply or rotate weekly.
- **When**: Roles established by end of August 2025 **Brain Breaks & Movement Integration**
- **What**: Teachers embed short, structured brain breaks and movement-based learning to improve focus and reduce disruptive behavior.
- **Who**: Teachers select appropriate activities; Coach provides resources.
- **When**: Embedded daily starting August 2025. **High-Interest, Culturally Relevant Content**

- **What**: Classroom content and library materials will reflect students’ backgrounds and interests to increase motivation and relevance.
- **Who**: Teachers and Media Specialists collaborate to select texts and activities.
- **When**: Incorporated into planning **Minimize Transitions and Class Removals**
- **What**: Use in-class supports, follow behavior matrix and/or behavior plans, and reflection tools before considering removal.
- **Who**: All staff, supported by behavior intervention team and Counselor.
- **When:** Implement beginning August 2025, monitored by behavior logs/systems **Tier Supports Before Removal**
- **What**: Provide supports in the students plan such as calming resets, movement breaks, and individual check-ins before removing a student from instruction.
- **Who**: Teachers initiate; Counselor provide back-up supports.
- **When**: Applied as needed throughout the year; monitored bi-weekly by Counselor. **Targeted SEL Groups**
- **What**: Counselor leads small groups for students needing support in self-regulation, motivation, attendance, peer conflict, grief, etc.
- **Who**: Counselor leads groups; students selected via data and teacher referral.
- **When**: Groups begin September 2025, running in 6-week cycles.

- **When**: Twice monthly from September 2025-May 2026 and/or as needed

## Stakeholder Collaboration

This plan was developed collaboratively with internal stakeholders including:

- **Lead Teachers –** Support design of instructional routines, engagement strategies, and grade-level supports
- **School Counselor –** Design SEL and behavior intervention structures, including small groups
- **School Counselor/Assistant Principal –** Lead PBIS planning and discipline data reviews
- **Literacy Coach –** Facilitate lesson internalization and HQIM coaching cycles
- **Classroom Teachers –** Provide input on literacy and engagement needs
- **Family Liaison & Bilingual Support Staff –** Support equity in outreach and communication
- **Librarian/Media Specialist –** Support summer reading distribution, guest reader coordination, and cross-content literacy through curated text sets and library collaboration ●
- **Parents/Guardians** are recognized as essential stakeholders in the success of our SIP. They contribute in the following ways:
- **Literacy Support at Home** Participate in take-home reading programs, attend family literacy events, and reinforce reading habits through tools like Book Boxes and reading logs.
- **Engagement in Behavior & Culture** Partner in reinforcing PBIS expectations at home, participate in feedback surveys, and engage in goal-setting conferences.
- **Attendance Support** Collaborate with staff to problem-solve chronic absenteeism and build routines that support on-time, daily attendance.
- **Feedback & Communication** Provide input through school climate surveys, family nights, parent-teacher conferences, and community roundtables.
- **Cultural Representation** Participate in and help lead culturally responsive events that reflect and celebrate the school’s diversity.
