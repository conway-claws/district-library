## Conway High School School Action Plan 2024-2025

## Area of Concern:

Conway High School teachers have worked to implement high-yield instructional strategies based on the research of Robert Marzano, as well as the teaching of literacy according to Kylene Beers and Robert Probst. While Conway High School has made strides in this goal, it is recognized that we need to now respond to student achievement and assessment data in order to isolate gaps in learning as well as bridge those gaps.

## Goal:

- Content area teams will meet at least twice a month and evaluate student assessment data and respond instructionally.
## Strategies:

Teachers will implement the following:

- Common formative assessments based on identified power standards.
- Structured PLC model that involves:
- evaluating student growth by isolating gaps in learning.
- adjust instructional strategies to support students in bridging gaps in learning
## Professional Development

- Teachers who have not received training in the explicit teaching of literacy strategies according to Beers and Probst or Marzano elements will be provided professional development. These will be facilitated by department instructional facilitators.
- Teachers will receive professional development in:
- Standards-based assessments with Edulastic
- Examples of common formative assessments
- Professional learning communities
- Responding to student data
## Implementation Plan

Core-area teachers will:

- Assist in facilitating PLCs.
- Develop monthly department goals.
- Develop standards-based assessments.
- Meet collaboratively to analyze student assessment data.
- Adjust curriculum and instruction in order to respond to assessment data.
- Catalog adjustments to curriculum and instruction through minutes.
- Schedule students into courses using RTI Scheduler to provide intervention and support.
- Track student proficiencies.
Instructional facilitators will:

- Assist in facilitating conversations regarding student assessment data.
- Provide support in integrating instructional strategies in order to bridge learning gaps.
- Observe and provide feedback to teachers in order to improve instruction.

- Assist in facilitating conversations regarding student assessment data.

Determination of progress will be based on the following:

- Minutes from PLCs
- Classroom walkthroughs

## 2024-2025

## Area of Concern

Conway High School continues to emphasize the importance of attendance as a predictor of academic success. Although processes were implemented in the 2023-2024 school year, Conway High School wishes to build on those processes for the 2024-2025 school year in order to reduce preventable absenteeism which continues to impact student graduation rate and credit pulls.

Number of students who were not awarded credit due to nonattendance.

|School Year|Semester 1|Semester 2|
|---|---|---|
|2020-2021|No credit pulled due to Covid-19||
|2021-2022* (2,125 Students)|45 (2.12%)|48 (2.26%)|
|2022-2023 (2,181 Students) 2023-2024 (2,188 Students)|56 (2.57%) 99 (4.52%)|119 (5.46%)|

**2021-2022 COVID-19 quarantines continued into February of 2022.

## Goal

Reduce the number of students who are not receiving credit due to nonattendance to under 4%.

## Strategies

- Increase parent awareness and involvement in Home Access Center (HAC).
- Letters to families (5 and 10 day letters)
- Family in Need of Support (FINS) letter filed with Juvenile Court
- One-on-one student meetings conducted by School Social Worker
- Attendance conversations through Deans of Students.
## Professional Development

Faculty will receive professional development on:

- School policies regarding attendance and truancy.
- How to properly code attendance in eSchool.
## Implementation Plan

Faculty will implement the following:

- Ensure the proper coding of student attendance information.
- Notify the appropriate dean of students, counselor, or assistant principal, when student attendance becomes erratic.
- Develop semester exams with an excusal policy based on grades and attendance.
Front Office Personnel will implement the following:

Determination of progress will be made based on the following:

- Attendance trends
- Quantity of credit not awarded by semester

## 2024-2025

## Area of Concern

Although Conway High School offers a variety of Advanced Placement and Technical Education courses, it recognizes that it should be responsive to student desired outcomes. As part of this recognition, Conway High School will incorporate “What I Need” Time (WIN) in order to not only provide academic intervention and support for students, but also enrichment and extension activities. As part of this, Conway High School will assess the intended post-graduate outcomes of its students in order to provide opportunities for them while they are in high school.

## Goal

Conway High School will revisit and revise all Student Success Plans to enact curricular and course offering decisions.

## Strategies

- Utilize “What I Need Time” (WIN) in order to support students in decision making.
- Survey students regarding their post-graduate outcomes in order to determine school-level decisions.
## Professional Development

- Faculty will receive professional development on Kuder and student success plans.
- Faculty will receive expectations for WIN Time.
- Faculty, staff, and administrators will receive professional development on course offerings at Conway High School.
## Implementation Plan

Faculty will:

- Work with students to create an awareness of course offerings and pathways.
- Revise and implement student success plans.
Counselors will:

- Hold one-on-one meetings with students to schedule them in appropriate classes dependent upon their post-graduate outcomes.
Administrators will:

- Support student success plan program.
## Evaluation

Determination of progress will be made based on the following:

- Successful revision of student success plans.
- Alignment of course offerings with student-desired outcomes.
