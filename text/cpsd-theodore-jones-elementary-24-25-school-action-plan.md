# THEODORE JONES ELEMENTARY SCHOOL ACTION PLAN

# 2024-2025

## <u>First Area of Concern</u>

Theodore Jones Elementary students show an overall weakness in reading, comprehension, and phonics.

## <u>Goal</u>

At Theodore Jones Elementary, our primary focus is on advancing literacy skills by comprehensively utilizing our district's HQIM materials. By prioritizing reading, comprehension, and phonics, we aim to foster student achievement and growth. Our overarching goal includes the implementation of dedicated 'What I Need' (WIN) time across all grade levels, ensuring that each student receives Tier 1 and personalized Tier 2 instruction within the classroom setting. Through these efforts, we aspire to elevate the percentage of students meeting or exceeding grade-level reading standards by three to five percent.

## <u>Strategies</u>

|●|Implement a solid RTI process to ensure interventions are working and appropriate for all|
|---|---|
||students.|
|●|Establish a building-wide grade-level focus on students who need extra support in reading.|
|●|Conduct weekly specific grade-level team meetings targeted towards academic growth in all students.|
|●|Organize monthly data-driven grade-level meetings to review student progress and adjust instruction accordingly.|
|●|Provide modeling of lessons by our instructional facilitator and teachers.|
|●|Ensure solid Tier 1 core instruction by classroom teachers using HQIM materials.|
|●|Offer Tier II classroom support to students not making adequate growth using district Tier 2 programs.|
|●|Provide Tier III instruction using the Sonday support curriculum for students not meeting state-mandated assessment/screener expectations.|

## <u>Professional Development</u>

|●|Conduct summer professional development at the district level to train teachers in HQIM|
|---|---|
||materials.|
|●|Facilitate cross-curricular grade-level meetings to provide teachers with valuable information regarding students' academic needs.|
|●|Hold monthly staff development meetings to share teaching strategies and techniques for student success.|
|●|Provide training and follow-up support for teachers to administer screeners and interpret progress monitoring guidelines properly.|

## <u>Implementation Plan</u>

- Review data from previous semester assessments to identify areas for improvement.
- Utilize formative assessments to guide instruction and monitor student progress.
- Conduct PLC meetings focused on monitoring the progress of all students and adjusting instruction accordingly.
- Utilize the RTI process to collect data and monitor the effectiveness of tiered instruction.

## <u>Evaluation</u>

- Ongoing evaluation will be conducted based on all assessment results.
- Formative assessments will be used to review student progress and revise plans for students not performing at grade level or meeting growth projections.
- Evaluation will include results of classroom progress monitoring, RTI data, ATLAS, NWEA, Acadience data, and spring 2023 standardized testing data.
## <u>Second Area of Concern</u>

Theodore Jones Elementary students exhibit challenges in social-emotional regulation and behavior management, impacting their overall well-being and academic performance.

## <u>Goal</u>

Theodore Jones will implement strategies to support social-emotional regulation and increase positive behavior among students. The goal is to continue to create a safe and nurturing environment where students can thrive socially, emotionally, and academically.

## <u>Strategies</u>

- Implement a school-wide Positive Behavior Intervention and Support (PBIS) framework to establish clear expectations for behavior and promote a positive school culture.
- Provide social-emotional learning (SEL) curriculum and activities to all grade levels to develop students' self-awareness, self-management, social awareness, relationship skills, and responsible decision-making.
- Establish a Student Support Team (SST) to identify students in need of additional social-emotional support and develop individualized intervention plans.
- Train teachers and staff in trauma-informed practices to better understand and support students who have experienced adverse childhood experiences (ACEs).
- Implement restorative practices to address conflicts and promote accountability while fostering empathy and understanding among students.
- Provide regular opportunities for students to participate in mindfulness exercises, relaxation techniques, and stress management activities to promote emotional well-being.
- Create a peer mentoring program where older students serve as mentors for younger students, fostering positive relationships and providing support and guidance.
- Establish a system for recognizing and celebrating positive behavior through incentives, such as awards, certificates, and special privileges.
## <u>Professional Development</u>

- Conduct training sessions for all staff on PBIS implementation, SEL strategies, trauma-informed practices, restorative practices, and mindfulness techniques.
- Provide ongoing professional development opportunities, including coaching sessions, to support staff in effectively implementing social-emotional support initiatives.
- Arrange for guest speakers, including behavioral specialists, counselors, and experts in social-emotional learning, to provide specialized training and workshops on behavior management strategies, de-escalation techniques, and creating a supportive classroom environment.

|●|Integrate SEL curriculum and activities into daily classroom instruction and school-wide initiatives.|

|●|Implement a system for tracking and rewarding positive behavior, and regularly communicate successes and achievements with the school community.|
