Superintendent Contract Addendum 2024-2025
Performance Targets

1. District Level Student Achievement Goal: Compare the 2024 ATLAS baseline assessment
outcome with the 2025 ATLAS assessment results for grade-level reading.

Actions:

e Comparison of Baseline: 2024 ATLAS Assessment data results as compared to
regional and state 2024 ATLAS Assessment data.

e Reading Programs: Implement and expand reading programs to increase the
percentage of students testing at or above grade level.

2. District Level Graduation Rate Goal: Improve the 4-year cohort graduation rate over three
years.

Actions:

e Graduation Initiatives: Develop and implement initiatives to support at-risk students
and improve graduation rates.

e Monitoring Progress: Regularly monitor graduation rate data and adjust strategies as
needed.

3. School Level Achievement for "C", "D", and "F" Schools Goal: Improve the letter grade
percentage for targeted schools over a three-year period.

Actions:

e Focus Schools: Prioritize interventions and support for schools who need additional
support as defined by the 2023-2025 Arkansas district report card.

e Improvement Plans: Develop and implement school-specific improvement plans with
clear targets and timelines.

*To Date: Final letter grade distribution has not been defined or determined by the
Division of Elementary and Secondary Education.

superintendent.
