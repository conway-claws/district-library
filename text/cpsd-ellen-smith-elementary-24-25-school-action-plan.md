Ellen Smith Elementary School Action Plan 2024-2025

## <u>Area #1</u>

Students and families have experienced social and emotional trauma. This trauma affects the learning process and environment for students and staff members.

- Evidence:
- Increased anxiety and emotional responses in the classroom
- Increase in social/emotional discipline referrals
- Increase in counseling referrals
- Increase in parent requests for mental health services/supports
- Increase in mental health support needed for students to be successful in the classroom
- Increase in Behavior RTI requests, Functional Behavior Assessments, suspensions and Saturday School
- Goal:
- Continue to Implement a prosocial behavior curriculum, Second Step and Guide for Life, that supports all students, kindergarten-fourth grade. This curriculum is in accordance with ACT 1084.
- Implement morning meeting to discuss topics that are current to each individual classroom.
- Strategies:
- Behavior Rubric ■ Ellen Smith’s behavior team will evaluate and update the school wide behavior
matrix based on the data from 2023-2024 school year.

- School Wide Expectations ■ Grade level teams will work together to evaluate and adjust, if needed, the
appropriate expectations for all areas of school (hallway, recess, cafeteria,etc). ■ Expectations will be implemented consistently throughout all grades and all activity classes ■ The behavior team will meet each 9 weeks to discuss any adjustments or reteaching that should occur

- Social Emotional Skills will be taught in all classrooms throughout the building ■ Problem solving ■ Emotional coping ■ Appropriate conflict resolution ■ Communication ■ Interpersonal skills
- Professional Development
- Counselor and administration will provide continuing professional development on implementation and pacing for Second Step, Guide for Life, and ACT 1084
- The Behavior team will revisit the Behavior RTI process and FBA process with teachers
- Professional Development will be ongoing through the school year through grade level collaborations and monthly staff meetings
- Implementation
- Teachers will implement Second Step and Guide for Life in classroom lessons
- ProSocial curriculum will be taught via morning meeting or oral language time
- Counselor will teach proSocial curriculum(Second Step) through counselor classes
- Counselor will hold small social/emotional groups for students that are teacher or parent referred
- The behavior team will watch data to determine additional needs.
- Evaluation
- Teachers and administrators will evaluate effectiveness through the following ■ Behavior data ■ Anecdotal notes

■ Informal documentation ■ Formal documentation
