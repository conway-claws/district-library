Minutes of Board of Education Special Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street March 1, 2024

<u>Members Present</u> Mr. André Acklin, Mr. Jason Sandefer, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Trip Leach, Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Trey Geier

## <u>Members Absent</u>: Dr. David Naylor, Jr.

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan, Dr. Karen Lasker; Chief Financial Officer Robyn Keene; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Acklin called the meeting to order at 11:00 a.m.

- Approval of Conway Project Resolution Superintendent Collum reported that the resolution is the next step in the project process. It will be sent to the Arkansas Department of Education for partnership funding. Board President Acklin called for a motion to approve a resolution stating that the District agrees to provide the portion of matching funds for the 2025-2027 funding biennium for the New JHS Addition construction project for which the District request state partnership funding. Mr. Leach so moved. Motion was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 11:03 a.m. <u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at** **11:04 a.m. on a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Mrs. Franklin.** Respectfully submitted, <u>/s/ Andre’ Acklin /s/ Linda Hargis</u> President Board Secretary APPROVED: <u>March 12, 2024</u>
