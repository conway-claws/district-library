## 2026-2027 School Improvement Plan Julia Lee Moore Elementary School

## <u>Area of Concern</u> (#1)

Literacy is an area of need at Julia Lee Moore. Student achievement data from the Spring 2025 administration of the ATLAS assessment indicate 57% of third-grade students scored at a proficient level and 54% of fourth-grade students scored at a proficient level.

## <u>Goal</u> (#1)

The staff members at Julia Lee Moore Elementary will implement effective instruction aligned to the Science of Reading (SoR) utilizing high-quality instructional materials (HQIM) with integrity to increase the number of students performing at grade-level proficiency in ELA by 3% as evidenced by summative, interim, and common formative assessments (CFAs) by the end of the 2026-2027 school year.

## <u>Strategies/Action Steps</u>

- Administrators and the instructional coach will participate in district-level planning and professional development sessions aligned to the SoR and the implementation of district-adopted HQIMs.
- Teachers will attend professional development offered by the district on curriculum and instruction aligned to the SoR and implementation of HQIM aligned to SoR.
- The media specialist will purchase and distribute books to students for summer reading.
- Administrators, the instructional coach, and teachers will analyze data from the spring administration of the 2026 ATLAS assessment to determine areas of greatest strength and need.
- The media specialist and instructional coach will plan and host a family/grandparent night and a school-wide Read-a-Thon to highlight literacy.
- Teachers will participate in regular grade-level collaborative meetings with the instructional coach and administrators to plan and internalize instruction aligned to the SoR utilizing Arts & Letters and 95% Phonics HQIMs.
- The administrators and instructional coach will conduct classroom walkthroughs using DESE’s *key levers for instructional leaders* to gather evidence of implementation as it relates to the five impact areas in literacy.

- Administrators and instructional facilitators will review CPSD Quarterly Classroom Walkthrough data with the teachers in order to improve instruction.
- Teachers will monitor and assess students’ growth in reading using CFAs, BUILD, Rite Flight, ULFI, Sound Sensible, SPIRE, Sonday, Take Flight, and interim assessments.
- In collaboration with the instructional coach and administrators, teachers will collaboratively analyze students’ performance and growth to inform instructional decisions and provide timely interventions to promote student growth.
- The media specialist and literacy interventionist will organize and host a guest reading day for community members and families to share stories with students.
## <u>Professional Development</u>

- Porscha Albert, instructional coach, will attend district-level professional development and planning sessions. These sessions will target the SoR and the implementation of HQIM aligned to SoR.
- Teachers and administrators will attend curriculum and instruction professional development sessions offered by the district on August 5 and 6, 2026. These sessions will focus and effective implementation of the district-adopted HQIM, Arts & Letters Curriculum, to support high quality instruction.
- Teachers will participate in regular grade-level collaborative meetings with the instructional coach and administrators to plan and internalize instruction aligned to the SoR utilizing the Arts & Letters Curriculum and 95% Group HQIMs.
- On August 10-13, 2026 - Teachers will be provided professional development opportunities at Julia Lee Moore.
