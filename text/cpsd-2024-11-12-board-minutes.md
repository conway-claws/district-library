Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street November 12, 2024

<u>Members Present</u>: Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Andre’ Acklin, Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Carl Barger, Mr. Tom Kennedy

## <u>Members Absent</u>: Mr. Trip Leach

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan; Directors: Bryce Bennett, Gynger Campbell, Ben Darley, Kelli Gordon, Greg Hughes, Sarah Jerry, Eric King; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Naylor called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video featuring Henry Gowin, Ascending Student of the Month from Julia Lee Moore Elementary School. She welcomed Principal Kenny Clark, who introduced Henry. Superintendent Collum presented Henry with a certificate. Henry led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Conway High School Volleyball State champions – Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Athletic Director Greg Hughes to introduce the Conway High School Volleyball team and recognize them for winning the state championship.
- Ryan Raup – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Ellen Smith Elementary School Principal Shannon Riner to introduce Ryan Raup, fourth grade teacher. Mr. Raup won the 2025 Arkansas Best Education Based School Garden award.
- Leslee Tell – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Conway High School Principal Dr. Worley to introduce Leslee Tell, Culinary Arts. Mrs. Tell won the 2025 Arkansas Best Overall Garden award. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:18 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mrs. Franklin and seconded by Mr. Acklin.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – October 8, 2024, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in October 2024
- Approval of Purchase Order
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of Annual Equity Compliance Report
- Approval of Transportation Sale

- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline.
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Annual Report to the Public – Superintendent Collum presented the 2024 Annual Report to the Board and to the public.
- Superintendent Goals – Superintendent Collum presented his Superintendent goals to the Board.
- School Election Timeline – Superintendent Collum presented the upcoming May school election timeline.
## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Dr. Andy Ashley presented the financial report for October. **Mr. Barger made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 7:00 p.m.**
## <u>Information Item</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Director Sarah Jerry presented the academic report on Professional Development for Educators: Lifelong Learning and Growth.
- Proposed Policy Revisions – Shasta Wagner, Chief Legal Officer, presented the proposed changes to Section 6 and 7 for the Board to consider and vote upon at the December 2024 Board meeting. <u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mr. Kennedy and seconded by Mrs. Franklin, the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 7:08 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**
- Resignations
- Elections
## <u>Patron Comments</u>

- Jimmie Cavin – Mr. Cavin spoke to the Board about his concern with sexually explicit books in schools.
## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 7:24 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session to consider a personnel matter. The Board exited Executive Session at 8:52 p.m.

## <u>Approval of Superintendent Goals and Contract Addendum</u>

The Board reviewed and approved Superintendent Collum’s goals and contract addendum based on Arkansas Code § 6-17-123.

## Mr. Barger made the motion to approve the Superintendent goals and contract addendum as

## presented. The motion was seconded by Mr. Acklin and passed unanimously at 8:52 p.m.
