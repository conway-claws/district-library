*This meeting was scheduled for both Certified and Classified Committees to vote on moving forward with the proposed CPSD employee monetary bonus for December of 2024.*

Option 1:

Each employee of Conway Public Schools will receive a bonus amount based on their overall years of experience. Bonuses will be awarded based on the following experience criteria:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Year of Experience | Bonus<br>Amount |
| 30+ | $3,000 |
| 20-29 | $2,500 |
| 10-19 | $1,500 |
| 0-9 | $1,000 |

Option 2:

Each employee of Conway Public Schools will receive a bonus amount of $500.
