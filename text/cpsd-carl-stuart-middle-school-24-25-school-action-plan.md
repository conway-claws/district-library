## Student Action Plan 2024-2025 Carl Stuart Middle School

## Area of Concern #1: Response to Intervention Processes and Practices for Literacy and Mathematics

During the 2023-24 academic year, Carl Stuart Middle School implemented our Cat Connections program. This program was a thirty minute time slot in our student’s schedule in which our students would receive both behavioral and academic support through the intervention process. This was our pilot year for this program. Our goal for the 2024-25 school year will be to increase the breadth and depth of this program.

Improvement strategies will include:

- The use of RtI Scheduler to ensure that each student has our Cat Connections time on their class schedule. This will drive home the importance of this time allotted in the student’s academic day.
- The use of the program will have a multi-faceted impact on student achievement.
- Students will be assigned an intervention time based on need each week during our Cat Connections time. Teachers will be able to pull students into their groups and focus on literacy, mathematics, and behavioral goals.
- Teacher will also be able to use the program to pull students from their enrichment times to attend additional instruction on needed content areas.
- We will incorporate Social Emotional Learning weekly for each student through our Cat Connections time
- Counselors will provide behavioral RtI to students in need of behavioal and emotional interventions.
- This time will also provide needed social interaction with peers and provide a collaborative time of discussion on learning and growing in the areas in which middle school students struggle.

Professional development to enhance our RtI program will include:

- Incorporation of the RtI Scheduler program
- Development of social emotional learning resources for teachers
- Work sessions to incorporate the tiers of intervention for both instuction and behavior
Evaluation:

- Academicially, students will show improvement and mastery of content standards. The evaluation process will be reviewing data on common assessments. Students should increase their initial scores due to daily intervention time.
- Behaviorally, the discipline of Carl Stuart Middle School will improve based on learning of coping skills, conflict resolution, and benefiting from the positive relationship from the teacher providing the intervention time.
## Area of Concern #2: Student Behavior

The faculty, staff, and administration of Carl Stuart Middle School will continue to foster and facilitate growth in positive behavior with all students. These behaviors will enrich all areas of student growth including academic, personal, and social. It is our goal that each student comes to the classroom to perform in an environment conducive to learning.

Improvement strategies will include:

- Building and classroom procedures to provide structure and expectations for our students.
- Continue incorporation of a behavior matrix which will outline expectations in all areas of the campus.
- A positive behavior intervention system to reinforce growth in the appropriate behaviors.
- Allow students a voice and choice in incentives earned due to met behavioral expectations.

- A tiered system of behavior support will be used by teachers and administration.
- A conflict resolution program will be used to facilitate improvement in relationships between students.
- Building a relationship and a partnership with the guardians of the students.
Professional development to address student behavior will include:

- Continuation of the Behavior RtI model
- More in depth study of Tiers II and III behavior interventions
- PBIS support and implementation for building-wide systems
Evaluation:

- Administration will track data from the 22-23 school year in which a consistent PBIS system was not fully established, the 23-24 school year with our current expectations and behavior interventions in place, and integrate the data at the end of the 24-25 school year. This will measure the reliability of our implementation over a period of time.
- Goal: There will be a viable increase in the number of students participating in PBIS incentives.
## Area of Concern #3: Literacy Performance

Carl Stuart Middle School will continue to focus on the reading achievement levels of our student population. Our goal is to continually grow our readers, yet we will have a radar focus on those students who are reading below grade level. During the academic year we will assess our students on an interim basis in literacy, with the STAR Reading Assessment reporting grade level growth among our student population. This assessment will provide teachers with a progression of reading and will provide teachers an overall skill set for each student. This assessment will be given at the beginning of each semester, typically in September and January. The data from these assessments is below.

The 2024 school year produced a new set of state standards in reading instruction. Furthermore, a new state assessment for the spring of 2024. The data from these assessments will also be included in our action plan once that data set is released.

Reading Data for 2023-2024 Academic Year: STAR Reading Assessment: The data reflects that we are showing consistent growth in grade level instructional reading levels. However, the data also reflects the importance of the continuance of development of reading skills in grade 7 where students do not have an independent reading class as a part of their instructional day.

|Subject|Fall Average 23-24|Spring Average 23-24|Growth 23-24|
|---|---|---|---|
|Reading 5|5.1|5.6|0.5|
|Reading 6|6.1|6.7|0.6|
|Reading 7|6.4|6.7|0.3|

Improvement Strategies will include:

- Review Arkansas State Standards and compare our curriculum to those standards that have been released.
- Using student achievement data, we will assess gaps in our student performance according to the curriculum standards.
- Provide PLC work with our teachers to focus on providing opportunities for RISE and implementation of reading in all classrooms.
- Focus on the RtI process to provide student intervention in literacy.
- Provide intervention time in reading in all literacy classrooms.
- Progress monitor to adjust instruction to meet the needs of our struggling learners.

Professional development for area of concern #2:

- Curriculum content area review at the beginning of the 202 sch4-25 school year. This will be accomplished through a district literacy model as well as providing planning time within our building.
- Continued implementation of a building RtI committee.
- Providing more opportunities for reading intervention within our school day. This will be accomplished during our RtI time called Cat Connections as well as provide classroom teachers a reading intervention time in an increased occurance within our master schedule.
- PLC training for teachers in order to make data-driven decisions for our students.
Evaluation:

- Teachers will be familiar with student data and be able to provide feedback to administrators.
- Data will show that our student population as a whole has grown in the area of literacy by assessing the fall and spring administration of the STAR Reading Assessment. ATLAS testing data will be used as a starting point of the student intervention plan.
