# CAROLYN LEWIS ELEMENTARY SCHOOL ACTION PLAN

# 2024-2025

# Area of Concern/Need

For the 2024-2025 school year, Carolyn Lewis Elementary will focus on academic learning loss in the areas of literacy and mathematics. We will provide students with high levels of academic and social-emotional support. Additional support will be provided through classroom teachers, support staff, and interventionists.

# Goal #1

Carolyn Lewis will focus on Literacy and Math achievement needs based upon gaps in student assessments. Carolyn Lewis Elementary will implement explicit learning opportunities to support student achievement during the 2024-2025 school year. Teachers and administrators will analyze results from Acadience and Amplify data to identify individual student needs and place students in an appropriate instructional group in order to directly address learning gaps.

# Strategies

- Utilization of data wall and monthly progress monitoring of all students. All students will receive intervention based on all assessment pieces. This includes students who are performing below, at, or above benchmark.
- Utilize the RTI process to ensure that students are making progress from their “at level” interventions. If a lack of progress is shown, adjustments will be made to the interventions provided.
- Implementation of WIN (What I Need) Teachers will ensure that daily interventions are taking place in the classroom as well as any available pull-out interventions that are needed with the support of interventionists and activity teachers.
- Teachers will review data and plan appropriately during weekly collaboration and scheduled RTI meetings. Additionally, teachers will participate in weekly PLC meetings.
- RISE-Grades K-4 fully implement and follow RISE, including the use of decodable texts to target specific reading levels
- Benchmark Reading-Grades K-4 fully implement Benchmark Reading instruction with fidelity as paced by the curriculum.
- 95% Group Phonics Instruction-Grades K-4 fully implement 95% Group Phonics instruction with fidelity as paced by the curriculum.
- Amplify-Grades K-4 and Activity Teachers will implement the Amplify Tier 2 Intervention Curriculum with fidelity.
- Eureka Math Squared-K-4 fully implement Eureka Math Squared Curriculum with fidelity.
# Professional Development

- Professional development for teachers in the area of analyzing data and determining instructional needs for students and intervention strategies.

- Continuous support for teachers once professional development has been delivered. The Instructional Facilitator and Interventionists will use a coaching cycle plan of support for teachers.
- Professional development on Intervention/RtI processes.
- Professional development on assessment administration and evaluation, as needed.
- Continuous professional development/coaching with Benchmark Reading, 95% Groups Phonics, and Amplify.
- Administrators, Teachers, and Instructional Facilitators will train on HQIM curriculum being implemented district wide.
# Implementation Plan

- Training on WIN time, Amplify, and district HQIM curriculum.
- Review data from formative assessments. Initially, the data team will analyze classroom/grade level data in order to assist teachers in determining groups and instructional focus.
- One hour of WIN Time (Literacy) and Math Groups will be added to the daily schedule.
- In addition to teacher monitoring of assessment data and progress monitoring, the data team will monitor as well. Initially, the data team will determine who is taken to the RtI committee.
# Evaluation

- Results of classroom progress monitoring
- Lexia data and reports
- Spring 2024 standardized testing data
# Goal #2

Carolyn Lewis Elementary will provide social emotional support to students delivered by our school counselor and classroom teachers per ACT 1084. The counselor will collaborate with classroom teachers to identify specific social emotional issues that could be addressed in both classroom and small group settings.

# Strategies

- Teachers will receive professional development to provide social emotional support
- Teachers will receive professional development for implementing positive behavioral supports as outlined in ACT 1084
- Our school counselor will conduct monthly classes and provide small group counseling. Individual counseling will also be provided, as needed
- Carolyn Lewis Elementary will implement strategies from the Guide For Life and Second Step curriculum programs
- CATS Kids program will be implemented.
# Professional Development

- Staff development in meeting the social/emotional needs of all students
- Tiered intervention strategies to deal with a variety of behavioral situations to address student behaviors

- Teacher expectations in regards to behavioral RTI protocols
- Utilization of arbss.org website for teacher support of behavioral supports
# Implementation Plan

- Administration will create an MTSS behavior team to support teachers and students needing additional interventions
- Scheduled RTI meetings for students needing Tier 2 and Tier 3 interventions
- Monthly recognition of CATS Kids
# Evaluation

- Data reports will be analyzed to review disciplinary trends through Educators’ Handbook
- Grade level and individual reports will be shared throughout the year to verify interventions are working
- We will disaggregate academic performance to social emotional learning.
- Follow up meetings with classroom teachers and behavior team for progress monitoring
