- A. Schedule K-Secretarial/Clerical Salary Schedule (Darkis/CLE) and (Cowings/SMS)

- The writer formally requests reconsideration of raises for Schedule K secretarial and clerical staff, noting that current pay scales prevent many secretaries from ever reaching $40,000 annually, even at the highest step with many years of service and relevant education.

- Current step progression for classified staff stops after 18 years, leaving employees at the same pay level for the next 10 years, which conflicts with the district’s stated commitment to rewarding longevity.

- The lack of step movement or salary increases means long-term classified employees may experience decreased take-home pay due to rising health premiums and cost of living.

- A bookkeeper raises concerns that salary structures for classified staff lack transparency, fairness, and alignment with the district’s treatment of certified staff, who have more step levels and received raises as a group.

- Meanwhile, the district is exploring raises for teachers and has approved increases for paraprofessionals, but secretaries have not received cost-of-living or salary increases in years, despite expanded duties and essential roles.

- The writer argues that reviewing and restructuring Schedule K is necessary to support retention, equity, and competitive compensation.

- Requests follow-up on:

1.Updates from district leadership after presenting this issue.

2.Guidance on the formal process for considering Schedule K raises and restructuring.

3.An explanation of why classified staff raises have become individualized while certified staff continue to receive groupwide raises.
