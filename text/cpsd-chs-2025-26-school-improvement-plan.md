# School Improvement Plan: Conway High School 2025-2026

# Goal 1: Improve Student Academic Achievement in Literacy

## Area(s) of Concern:

- Declining ELA scores on state assessments.
- Achievement gaps among subgroups (particularly African American and economically disadvantaged students).
- Lack of consistent instructional strategies across classrooms.
**Goal:** Increase the percentage of students scoring proficient or above in ELA on state assessments by 10% by the end of the 2025–2026 school year. **Strategies:**

- Implement a school-wide literacy initiative with targeted reading and writing instruction.
- Provide tiered intervention for struggling readers.
- Provide targeted feedback to teachers through classroom walk throughs on reading and writing instruction.
## Professional Development:

- Training on evidence-based reading strategies (e.g., close reading, reciprocal teaching).
- Begin process of creating learning ladders to set specific skill targeted achievements for students to demonstrate mastery of grade level skills.
- Collaborative planning sessions to align ELA strategies across content areas.
