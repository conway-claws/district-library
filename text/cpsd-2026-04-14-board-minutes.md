Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street April 14, 2026

<u>Members Present</u>: Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty, Mrs. Ashley Lyon

## <u>Members Absent</u>: Mr. Trip Leach

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jason Black; Interim Deputy Superintendent Dr. Karen Lasker; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Bryce Bennett; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Preston Echols, Kelli Gordon, Shawn Higginbotham, Eric King, Shanda New; General Counsel Shasta Wagner; Strategic Planning Consultant Dr. Jeff Standridge; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Champion Student Recognition</u>

Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, introduced Ty Cason, Champion Student of the Month from Carl Stuart Middle School, and presented a video featuring Ty. Superintendent Black presented him with a certificate. Ty led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

[Mrs. Hargis entered the meeting at 6:05 p.m.]

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- AP Honor Roll-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Principal Worley, Conway High School, to speak about College Board AP Honor Roll rating moving from Bronze to Silver. Dr. Worley recognized AP teachers for their accomplishments.
- Lisa’s Closet-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Director Preston Echols to speak about Lisa’s Closet and the partnership with the foundation that helps provide shoes for students in need.
- Mock Trial-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized the Conway High School Speech and Debate students for their Arkansas State Championship.
- Boys State-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized the Conway High School student Ian Lee-Kim for being one of two students from Arkansas selected to Boys Nation.
- Grant Recipients-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Assistant Superintendent Bryce Bennett to recognize the Conway Public Schools employees that have received grants and awarded funding for the 2025-2026 school year.
- State Champion Unified Basketball Team – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized the Conway High School Unified Basketball team for winning the state championship for the fourth year.
## <u>Patrons Comments</u>

- Alyce Hardee – Ms. Hardee spoke to the Board about her concerns with the Board’s conversation about transparency during the April 2
nd Board Workshop.

- Tyler Moses – Mr. Moses spoke to the Board about his concerns with the District’s disconnect with campuses and the Ten Commandments lawsuit legal fees.
- Jimmie Cavin – Mr. Cavin spoke to the Board about honesty, transparency, and trust.
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Strategic Plan/Project Conway4Ward – Superintendent Black presented a video and update on Project Conway4Ward and Dr. Jeff Standridge presented an update on the Strategic Plan.
- Spring C.A.T. Forum – Superintendent Black invited patrons to come to the C.A.T. Forum on April 21 in the Conway High School Lecture Hall from 6:00 – 7:00 p.m.
- Graduation Update – Superintendent Black invited Conway High School Principal Worley to give an overview of the graduation plans for the Class of 2026. Dr. Worley presented the 2026 yearbook to the Board. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 7:10 p.m. on a motion made by Mr.** **Cummings III and seconded by Ms. Walton.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – March 10, 2026, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in March 2026
- Approval of Purchase Orders

## <u>District Financial Reports</u>

- Board Financial Report – Shawn Higginbotham, Director of Finance, presented the financial report for March. **Ms. Walton made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mr. Cummings III and passed unanimously at 7:12 p.m.**
- Salary Schedules – Shawn Higginbotham, Director of Finance, presented the revised salary schedules to the Board for approval. **Ms. Walton made the motion to approve the Salary Schedules, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 7:14 p.m.**
## <u>Information Items</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Bryce Bennett presented the C & I Roles and Responsibilities, Virtual Update, Action Plans, and Summer PD report.
- Proposed Policy Revisions – General Counsel Shasta Wagner presented the proposed changes to Policy Sections 3, 7, and 8. These policies are to be considered and voted upon at the May Board meeting.
