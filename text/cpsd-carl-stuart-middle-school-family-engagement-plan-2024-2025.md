## <u>1: Jointly Developed</u>

Describe how the School works with parents and families to develop and review relevant plans, policies, and strategies related to engagement.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***1.1:** How does the School-in collaboration with parents-establish an engagement plan that* *reflects the specific academic improvement needs of the School, and that includes programs* *and practices that enhance engagement and address the specific engagement needs of* *students and their families?*
[*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(a)*]

- ***1.2:** What efforts have been made to ensure adequate representation of parents and families* *of participating children in the process (Title I families)?*
[*ESSA § 1116(c)(3)*]

CSMS has formed an advisory committee, which includes parents from our student population. This committee has worked to develop our family engagement plan for the 2024-25 school year. The committee began planning based on inquiries and comments from our patrons. This was the foundation of our plan so that multiple parents could have an input. CSMS will have ongoing family communication through periodic advisory meetings, family surveys, communication in our CSMS Thrillshare site and our Schoology pages in both English and Spanish, as well as having the plan available to families in our CSMS Parent Center.

## <u>2: Communication</u>

Describe how the School will communicate with and distribute information to parents and families.

## Guiding Questions

- ***2.1:** How does the School distribute an informational packet appropriate for the age and grade* *of each child annually, ensuring to include:*
- *description of the engagement program*
- *recommended roles for parents, students, teacher, and the School*
- *ways for a family to get involved*
- *survey regarding volunteer interests*
- *schedule of activities planned throughout the school year*
- *regular, two-way, and meaningful system for parents/teachers to communicate* [*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(b)(3)(B)(i)*]
- ***2.2:** How will the School ensure information related to school and parent programs, meetings,* *and other activities is provided to parents in a format and in a language that parents can* *understand (to the extent practicable)?*
- *How is relevant information provided in a variety of ways? (For example, paper copies* *made available, as well as social media posts, website links, parent apps, etc.?* [*ESSA § 1116(e)(5)*]
- ***2.3:** How does the School offer flexible opportunities for meetings with families?*
[*ESSA § 1116(c)(2)*]

CSMS will use the resources that have been provided by our district for communication as a multimedia approach. Thrillshare will be used as a tool to send texts, phone calls, and email to communicate school events as well as meetings in which families can be involved. We also have our school marquee outside and one inside as our families visit the building via monitor systems throughout the building. CSMS has assigned our parent involvement coordinator the duty of family communication. This role will serve to communicate events and meetings to our families through the school website, social media pages, as well as email and personal text, and the Conway Public Schools district web-based application. CSMS teachers will communicate with families using Schoology, Rooms through Thrillshare, family emails and calls, and parent-teacher conferences.

- *involvement in the education of their children*
- *volunteer activities*
- *learning activities and support classroom instruction*
- *participation in School decisions*
- *collaboration with the community*

- *purchase parenting books, magazines, and other informative material regarding* *responsible parenting through the School library, advertise the current selection, and* *give parents an opportunity to borrow the materials for review*

- *assistance with nutritional meal planning* [*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(b)(5)(B)(ii)(a-d)*]
CSMS will offer opportunities for our parents, which fosters parental skills to help parents build relationships to enhance student performance, both at home and school. The school also provides literature that is sent home to families on a variety of topics which parents deal with in the middle years. CSMS also will provide educational activities at our family nights that can be taken home to address academic skills. Our school will provide online resources through our parental involvement coordinator. This information will be pushed out through our school website as well as our social media pages. Teachers will hold conferences with parents individually to address test data and classroom performance. Parents are provided information on how to conduct educational activities and discussion topics for home to engage in parent-student interaction.

## <u>5: Coordination</u>

Describe how the School will coordinate with other organizations, businesses, and community partners, including alumni, to provide additional supports, services, and resources to families.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***5.1:** How does the School investigate and utilize community resources in the instructional* *program?*
[*ADE Rules Governing Parental Involvement Section 5.06*]

- ***5.2:** How does the School coordinate and integrate programs and activities with other Federal,* *State, and local programs? Some examples include:*
- *public preschool programs such as Head Start*
- *organizations/activities to help students transition to elementary, middle, high, and* *postsecondary schools or careers*
- *wraparound services that allow families to send their children to school ready and able* *to focus on learning* [*ESSA §1116(e)(4)*]
- ***5.3:** In what ways does the School enable the formation of a Parent Teacher Association or* *organization and ensure leaders of said organization will be included in appropriate decisions?*
[*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(b)(8)(B)(ii)*]

CSMS will direct parents to updated resources available on the school’s website. The resources include the school's parental involvement plan, the student handbook, a volunteer informational survey, list of recommended roles for parents/teachers/students and school, suggestions of ways parents can become involved in their child's education, a list of parental involvement activities planned for the current school year and information about the system that will be used to allow parents and teachers to communicate. Parents are welcome and encouraged to visit the school’s Parent Center, which is open anytime school is in session. Parent center is located in the front office of CSMS. Resources available to parents in the parent center include the following:

- Parent literature (brochures, magazines, newsletters, etc. on parenting advice specific to parents of middle level students)
- Computer to be used by parents to check grades of their student(s) and peruse educational websites if needed.
- Parental feedback form for questions, concerns, or comments. Feedback will assist parents with resolving parental concerns, including how to define a problem, whom to approach first, and how to develop solutions.
