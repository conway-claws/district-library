Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street June 10, 2025

<u>Members Present</u>: Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mr. Trip Leach, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Dr. Barrett Petty, Ms. Leona Walton

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Bryce Bennett, and Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Preston Echols, Greg Hughes, Eric King; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; Business Manager Stacy Rimmer; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Naylor called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Superintendent Collum led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- PTO President Appreciation – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized the District’s PTO Presidents for all their hard work for this year. Superintendent Collum thanked them for their service to the District. <u>Reorganization of the Board</u> **A motion to appoint Mrs. Franklin as Board President was made by Dr. Petty and seconded by** **Ms. Walton. The motion passed Unanimously at 6:07 p.m.** **A motion to appoint Ms. Walton as Board Vice President was made by Dr. Petty and** **seconded by Mrs. Hargis. The motion was passed unanimously at 6:07 p.m.** **A motion to appoint Mr. Cummings III as Board Secretary was made by Dr. Petty and** **seconded by Mrs. Franklin. The motion passed unanimously at 6:08 p.m.** <u>Resolution Naming Primary Board Disbursing Officer</u> **A motion to appoint Mr. Cummings III as the Primary Board Disbursing Officer was made by** **Mrs. Franklin and seconded by Dr. Petty. The motion carried unanimously at 6:11 p.m.**
