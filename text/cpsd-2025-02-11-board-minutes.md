Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street February 11, 2025

<u>Members Present</u>: Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mr. Trip Leach, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Andre’ Acklin, Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Carl Barger, Mr. Tom Kennedy

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Ben Darley, Sarah Jerry, Eric King; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; Business Manager Stacy Rimmer; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Naylor called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video featuring Joshua Bell, Ascending Student of the Month from Jim Stone Elementary School. She welcomed Principal Mark Lewis, who introduced Joshua. Joshua led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Laura Tinsley – Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Conway High School Principal Worley to introduce Laura Tinsley, Conway High School teacher, for receiving a Presidential Award for Excellence in Mathematics and Science Teaching.
- Conway High School State Beta Club Achievements – Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Conway High School Principal Worley to introduce the Beta Club and highlighted their achievements for the school year.
- Top Performance Scores on ATLAS 2024 – Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized the following Top 5% Performance schools in the District on ATLAS 2024: Julia Lee Moore Elementary School and Woodrow Cummins Elementary School. Mrs. Ryals also recognized the Top 10% Performance school, Carolyn Lewis Elementary School.
- District Recognition of Black History Month – February is Black History Month. Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented the Board with a presentation of pictures from students and staff celebrating Black History Month and highlighted events that will take place throughout the District this month. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:20 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Mr. Kennedy.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – January 14, 2025, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in January 2025
- Approval of Purchase Orders

- Approval of List of Capital Assets Retired 07/01/2023 – 06/30/2024
- Authorized Signature Authority for Deputy Superintendent Black to execute documents on behalf of the Superintendent in his stead
- Authorized Signature Authority for Assistant Superintendent Ashley to execute facility- related documents, including construction contracts with architects/contractors, etc., on behalf of District
- Appointment of District Treasurer Dr. Andy Ashley
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of School Choice Applications
- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline.
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- School Election Update – Superintendent Collum shared a candidate guide from the Arkansas School Boards Association.
- CTE Video – Superintendent Collum presented the next video in the series. This video highlights the CTE department.
## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Dr. Andy Ashley invited Stacy Rimmer, Business Manager, to present the financial report for January. **Mr. Barger made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mrs. Franklin and passed unanimously at 6:34 p.m.**
- Proposed Budget of Expenditures 2026-2027 and 2025 School Election Sample Ballot – Scott Beardsley, First Security Beardsley, presented the Proposed Budget of Expenditures 2026- 2027 and sample ballot for approval. **Mr. Leach made the motion to adopt the attached Proposed Budget of Expenditures with** **Tax Levy for Fiscal Year Beginning July 1, 2026 to and including June 30, 2027 and to** **approve the related Sample Ballot. The motion was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 6:37 p.m.**
## <u>Information Item</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Siri Briggler, Elementary Curriculum Coordinator, and Matt Kelly, Ruth Doyle Middle School Principal, presented the academic report on Empowering Every Learner.
## <u>Action Item</u>

- Cenergistic Service Agreement – Dr. Andy Ashley, Assistant Superintendent, presented to the Board for approval the Cenergistic Service Agreement. **Mr. Kennedy made the motion to approve Cenergistic Service Agreement, as presented.** **The motion was seconded by Mr. Leach and passed unanimously at 6:56 p.m.**

<u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mr. Acklin and seconded by Mrs. Hargis, the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 6:58 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**

- Resignations
- Elections
## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 7:00 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session to consider a personnel matter. The Board exited Executive Session at 8:24 p.m.

<u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at** **8:24 p.m. on a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Mr. Barger.**
