Minutes of Board of Education Special Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street May 27, 2026

<u>Members Present</u>: Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty, Mrs. Ashley Lyon

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jason Black; Deputy Superintendent Dr. Karen Lasker; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Bryce Bennett; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Preston Echols, Kelli Gordon, Shawn Higginbotham, Eric King, Shanda New; General Counsel Shasta Wagner; Strategic Planning Consultant Dr. Jeff Standridge; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board Vice President Walton called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

Superintendent Black led the audience in the Pledge of allegiance.

## <u>Patron Comments</u>

- Jimmie Cavin – Mr. Cavin spoke to the Board about the hiring process of the Director of Finance, Executive Session, and the Superintendent’s contract.
- Tyler Moses – Mr. Moses proposed creating a policy requiring Board meeting agendas to be finalized no later than 24 hours before Board meetings with an update at the beginning of meetings for emergency changes.
## <u>Strategic Advisor Report</u>

- Dr. Jeff Standridge – Dr. Standridge stated he was withdrawing his proposed contract addendum and does not want the District to issue his final monthly retainer payment for the month of June. He also highlighted work for the District during this school year.
## <u>Process of Board Member Appointment</u>

- Mrs. Shasta Wagner – Mrs. Wagner, General Counsel, reviewed the process and presented an example of a score sheet that was discussed in the Board Work Shop today to the Board for use in the appointment of Board members.
## Mr. Cummings III made the motion to adopt the procedure of the Board Member

**Appointment Score Sheet, as presented. The motion was seconded by Dr. Petty and passed unanimously at 6:00 p.m.**

## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 6:01 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session for the purpose of employment. The Board exited Executive Session at 7:19 p.m.

<u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Dr. Petty and seconded by Mr. Cummings III, the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations at 7:21 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**

- Resignations
- Elections
## <u>New Business</u>

- Mrs. Hargis asked that the Board look at the hiring process for the District and wants the Board to be able to view the applicants employment applications for Central Office positions. <u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at** **7:26 p.m. on a motion made by Mr. Cummings III and seconded by Dr. Petty.** Respectfully submitted,
