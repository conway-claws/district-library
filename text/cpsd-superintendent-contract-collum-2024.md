Ul. EMPLOYMENT

2.1 The Superintendent is to serve as the chief executive officer and educational leader of the district.
Superintendent shall have those duties and responsibilities and obligations as set forth by state and federal law,
regulations of the State Board of Education, and the Conway board of education that exist at the time of the
execution of this agreement and as may be hereinafter amended, changed, or modified by any of the above-

mentioned agencies.

2.2 The Superintendent cannot be reassigned from the position of Superintendent to another position
without the Superintendent’s expressed written consent. OR The board reserves the right to reassign the
Superintendent but with only his expressed written consent to different duties from time to time during the term of
this contract without a loss of pay or benefits. Such action shall only be done after notice and due process to the

Superintendent and in accordance with the state law and Board policies.

2.3 Superintendent shall always during the term of this contract, any renewal or extension thereof,
hold and maintain a valid certificate required of a Superintendent in the State of Arkansas issued by the Arkansas

Department of Education.

2.4 Superintendent acknowledges and agrees that the obligations and duties set forth in this contract
are his primary duties and responsibilities. However, the Superintendent has the right to undertake consulting work,
speaking engagements, writing, teaching a college or university course, lecturing, or other professional duties and
obligations. Provided, however, that this other work shall not interfere in a material and substantial manner with the

Superintendent’s obligations set forth above.

Ill. COMPENSATION

3.1 The Superintendent will be paid an annual salary $241,285 per year, to be paid in equal monthly
installments consistent with board policy. At any time during the terms of this Agreement, the Board may in its
discretion, review and adjust the salary of the Superintendent, but in no event shall the Superintendent be paid less

than the salary set forth in this section 3.1.

3.2 Should the District increase the base salary of all certified personnel employed by the district
during the term of this agreement, the Superintendent shall receive the same percentage rate increase in salary as

received by all certified personnel employed by the district.

3.3 The District shall pay or reimburse for all reasonable expenses incurred by the Superintendent for

school-related business or school trips.

3.4 The district shall provide the Superintendent an automobile allowance in the sum of $1,000 per
month, for all travel for District business purposes within the district, which travel allowance shall be in lieu of
payment of mileage reimbursement, gasoline, or other vehicle upkeep or maintenance charges. This automobile
allowance shall be paid in addition to the annual salary stated in Section 3.1 of this contract. Travel expenses for
travel outside the district will be reimbursed on a per mile basis in accordance with district policy as it is for other
administrative employees employed under twelve-month contracts; provided however, that the Superintendent is

hereby authorized to use a District owned vehicle to travel outside the district for district purposes.

3.5 The district shall provide all benefits that are provided to other certified personnel from time to
time. The district shall also pay the Superintendent annually for the terms of this contract a stipend of $15,000.00
per year in addition to his salary as provided in paragraph 3.1. The superintendent may use this stipend amount
annually at his discretion for any of the following compensation or benefit options: [supplemental retirement plan
contribution, annuity contribution, base salary increase, or a one-time retention incentive payment.] The
superintendent will notify the finance office annually in writing the specified allocation of these benefits defined in

this section 3.5.

3.6 The Superintendent agrees to continuously reside within the Conway School District as a term and

condition of his employment by the Board.

3.7 The district shall contribute to the ATRS Arkansas Teacher Retirement System on behalf of the
Superintendent as required by law. The district shall also pay to the Superintendent additional compensation in the

amount ($1500) per month in addition to his annual salary as set forth above.

3.8 The superintendent may take up to 20—23days of vacation in addition to the normal scheduled
school holidays provided to other school personnel. Superintendent agrees the days will be taken at such time that

will least interfere with the duties set forth in this contract.

3.9 Superintendent will be reimbursed on or before June 30" of each year for any unused vacation or

sick days not used within the school year at his daily rate.

At the Superintendent’s discretion, he may defer or “roll over” any unused vacation or sick leave days to

the following year and be reimbursed at the daily rate of pay in the current or subsequent year. Both unused

vacation and sick days will be reimbursed to the Superintendent at his current daily rate of pay at the end of a fiscal
year on or before June 30". If the Superintendent defers either unused vacation days or sick days to a following
year, the reimbursement for those days may be paid at the Superintendent’s discretion with written notice to the
payroll department. If the contract is terminated, the Superintendent will be paid in full for any unused sick or

vacation days at his current daily rate of pay.

3.10 The Superintendent shall devote the Superintendent’s time, attention and energy to the direction,
administration, and supervision of the district. The Board, however, encourages the continued professional growth
of the Superintendent through the Superintendent’s active attendance and participation in appropriate professional
meetings at the local, regional, state, and national levels. The district shall pay all professional dues incurred by the

Superintendent as well as dues for one civic club and the Chamber of Commerce.

IV. EVALUATION

4.1 Each year by or before the regularly scheduled January Board in which this contract is in force, the
Board and the Superintendent shall meet in Executive Session for the purpose of reviewing the performance of the

Superintendent.

42 The evaluation of the Superintendent shall always be conducted in such a manner as to preserve

confidentiality to the extent allowed by law.

4.3 The evaluation format, form and procedure shall be in accordance with the Board’s policies or
procedures and state and federal law. The Board shall require the Superintendent to provide a self-evaluation and

report regarding the Superintendent’s performance as part of the evaluation process.

V. NON-RENEWAL

3.1 In the event that the Board intends to act to terminate this Contract prior to its termination date
without the Superintendent’s written concurrence, the Superintendent shall be entitled to a due process hearing
before the Board of Education prior to the occurrence of any purported act of termination. Due process shall include

a written notice of the reasons why the Board of Education intends to terminate this Employment Contract and the

right to appear before the Board. In the event of termination, the salary and benefits of the Superintendent shall
terminate immediately if terminated because of material breach of this contract. The salary and benefits of the
Superintendent shall be paid in full for the remainder of this contract if termination occurs for any other causes. The
Superintendent has the right to be represented at the hearing by a representative of the Superintendent’s choice and a
right to a written decision describing the results of the hearing. The Board shall also have the right to be represented
at the hearing by a representative of its choice. Witnesses may be called by either party. These provisions do not
constitute a waiver of any rights that the Board or the Superintendent may have to enforce this Contract in the courts

under contract or other applicable law.

VI. MISCELLANEOUS

6.1 This Contract shall be governed by the laws of the State of Arkansas.

6.2 This Contract embodies the entire agreement between the parties hereto. Except as may otherwise
be provided, no subsequent alteration, amendment, change or addition to this Contract shall be binding upon the
parties unless reduced to writing and duly authorized and signed by each of them. The Board retains the right to
repeal, change or modify any policies which it has adopted or may hereafter adopt, subject however, to restrictions
contained in the state statutes and other applicable law. To the extent the Board amends a policy or adopts a new
policy which the Superintendent believes affects this contract, the Superintendent shall provide written notice to the
Board. Upon notice, such policy or amendment shall not alter this contract, but shall be part of the negotiations for

any future contracts between the District and Superintendent.

63 In the event of any conflict between the terms, conditions, and provisions of this Contract the
provisions of the Board’s policies or any permissive state or federal law, then unless otherwise prohibited by law,
the terms of this contract shall take precedence over the contrary provisions of any Board policies, procedures, and

practices, or any such permissive law during the term of the contract.

6.4 In the event any one or more of the provisions contained in this Contract shall, for any reason, be

held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, such invalidity, illegality or unenforceability shall not affect any other

provision thereof, and this Contract shall be construed as if such invalid, illegal, or unenforceable provision had

never been contained herein.

6.5 Any notice required or permitted to be delivered hereunder shall be deemed to be delivered, when

deposited in the United States mail, postage prepaid, certified mail, return receipt required, addressed to either party.

6.6 The District agrees that it shall defend, hold harmless and indemnify the Superintendent from any
and all demands, claims, suits, actions and legal proceedings brought against the Superintendent in the
Superintendents individual capacity or in his official capacity as agent and employee of the District for acts or
errors and omissions arising within the scope of his employment, which indemnity shall include costs and attorney
fees; provided, however, that the matter arose while the Superintendent was acting within the scope of the
Superintendent’s employment with the District in accordance with the Board’s policies and excluding any criminal
charges; and provided that such liability coverage is within the authority of the Board to provide under state law,
except in no case will individual Board members be considered personally liable for indemnifying the
Superintendent against such demands, claims, suits, actions or any legal proceedings. The district’s obligations
pursuant to this paragraph may be met by the purchase of a policy of liability insurance which includes coverage for

the Superintendent.

6.7 This Contract shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the Superintendent, his successor,
assigns, heirs, executors, and personal representative and shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the Board,

its successors, and assigns.

6.8 Both parties shall have had the opportunity to seek the advice of counsel.

employees as well as strategies to support their growth and well-being, improving instructional practices,

CEAL mentoring programs, and opportunities for continuing education.

e Safety and Security: Enhance the safety and security of all schools, conduct comprehensive evaluations to
continually assess and improve safety measures, emergency preparedness, and security protocols. Our
objective is to provide a secure learning environment that promotes the physical and emotional well-being

of all students, staff, and visitors.

Performance Targets

Ark, Code Ann § 6-17-123- Superintendent Contract Shall Contain:

1. District Level Student Achievement
Based on the District’s 2022-2023 Report Card, the percentage of District students who tested ready or exceeding

reflected on the Report Card as determined by new calculations modifications using the new ATLAS assessment

system.

The superintendent’s performance goal is to improve the percentage of the designated letter grade percentage during
a three-year period beginning July 1, 2025. The 2023-2024 Report Card with the new ATLAS assessment system

will establish a new baseline and letter grades for each public school in Conway.
