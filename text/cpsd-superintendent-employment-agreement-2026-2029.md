Superintendent Employment Agreement
Conway Public School District

The Board of Directors of the Conway Public School District (hereinafter “Board”) and Mr.
Jason Black (hereinafter “Superintendent”) agree to the following terms:

1. Employment

a. The Board agrees to employ the Superintendent from July 1, 2026 to June 30,
2029, with all the powers and duties vested in the position by applicable laws,
regulations, and Board policies, until such time that this agreement expires or is
terminated. The Superintendent agrees to accept the employment and faithfully
discharge the duties incident to it.

b. During the term of this Contract the Superintendent agrees to devote his/her full
attention to the performance of these duties and will perform no other gainful
employment or profession, including, but not limited to outside consulting.

c. Prior to June 30, 2026 and prior to the start of each Contract Year thereafter, the
Board shall consider, in executive session, whether this Contract shall be renewed
for an additional year or years and shall notify the Superintendent, in writing, of
such action. Renewal or non-renewal shall be solely at the discretion of the Board.
Unless the Board chooses, by a majority vote, not to extend the Agreement, the
Agreement shall be extended in force and effect for an additional one-year period
on the 30" day of June of each year of the primary or extended term.

2. Compensation

a. The Total Cash Compensation to be paid to the Superintendent effective July 1,
2026, shall be at an annual rate of $265,000, prorated over the term of this
Contract and payable in equal monthly installments consistent with board policy.

3. Duties

a. The Superintendent shall serve as the chief executive officer of the Conway
School District and shall have the powers necessary to perform this duty. All
district staff shall be subject to the direction of the Superintendent and shall be
recommended for hire, rehire, or termination by the Superintendent. The
Superintendent shall tender an official transcript of all post-secondary course
work, a copy of his or her teaching license, proof of age and any other documents
necessary to comply with state or federal law. The Superintendent shall maintain
his or her license in good standing. Any disciplinary suspension of the

Superintendent’s license by the Professional Licensing Standards Board shall
constitute a material breach of this Contract.

4, Fitness for Duty
a. The Superintendent represents that he or she is fully qualified to serve as

Superintendent, and is fit and able to perform all physical and intellectual duties
of the office.
5. Benefits

a. The Superintendent shall have the same fringe benefits as other contracted full
year employees, including vacation, sick, personal days, holidays, and shall
follow all personnel policies concerning these benefits. The Superintendent may
transfer in sick leave days from another Arkansas school district as provided by
law.

b. The Superintendent may elect to receive an annual transportation allowance of
Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000) in lieu of the use of a District-owned or
District-leased vehicle. If the Superintendent elects use of a District-provided
vehicle, the Superintendent shall be permitted unlimited use of the vehicle, and
the District shall provide a fuel card for vehicle operating expenses.

c. During the term of this Agreement, the Superintendent shall be entitled to
participate in all fringe benefits generally provided to full-time administrative
employees of the District, on the same basis and subject to the same policies,
including but not limited to health insurance, life insurance, retirement benefits,
vacation leave, sick leave, and such other benefits as may be approved by the
Board from time to time. Effective July 1, 2026, the Superintendent shall be
credited annually with fifteen (15) days of vacation leave, fifteen (15) days of sick
leave, and two (2) personal days per year. Vacation leave, sick leave, and personal
leave shall accrue and be used in accordance with District policy, except as
otherwise provided herein. Upon separation from employment with the District
for any reason, the Superintendent shall be compensated for any unused accrued
vacation leave and sick leave at the Superintendent’s then-prevailing daily rate of
pay. In addition, the Superintendent shall be reimbursed on or before June 30 of
each year for any unused vacation or sick leave not utilized during the school year
at the Superintendent’s daily rate of pay. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the
Superintendent may, at his discretion, elect to defer or roll over unused vacation
or sick leave to the following year.

d. The district shall pay or reimburse the Superintendent for all reasonable and
necessary expenses incurred in the performance of official duties or district
related travel in accordance with district policy and applicable state and federal
law.

6. Disability
a. In the event the Superintendent shall be disabled and unable to perform his or her

duties under this Contract by reason of sickness, accident, or other cause beyond
his or her control, and such disability continues for more than sixty (60) days, the

Board may terminate this Contract. In the event of termination due to disability,
the Superintendent shall continue to receive the salary provided for under
paragraph 2 of this Agreement for a period of four (4) months from the date the
Superintendent becomes disabled and has exhausted any sick leave available to
him under paragraph 5 hereof. The Board’s decision and determination as to the
disability of the Superintendent shall be final.
7. Professional Development Activities/Community Engagement
a. The Superintendent is encouraged and expected to participate in professional
activities that will tend to enhance his or her professional competence and keep
him or her abreast of developments in education and educational administration
and his or her reasonable expenses incident to such activities including meetings,
workshops, seminars, and other such programs; and his dues in professional
organizations will be reimbursed. The Superintendent is also encouraged and
expected to participate in community and civic activities, and the District shall
Pay any membership fees and other reasonable expenses incurred by the
Superintendent in connection with such participation.
8. Equipment
a. The Board will provide the Superintendent with all necessary technology to
perform the functions of his or her job, which shall include at least a computer
and a school owned cell phone, The Superintendent shall use school owned and
provided technology only for school and business related purposes.
9. Evaluation
a. The Board shall evaluate the Superintendent at least annually, using the last
adopted evaluation instrument for the Superintendent, or the evaluation
instrument required by law, if one exists. If individual evaluations are completed
by board members, each individual evaluation as well as any composite
evaluation will be shared with the Superintendent and retained in the
Superintendent’s personnel file. In addition to the Evaluation Instrument, the
Superintendent Performance Targets outlined in the Appendix of this Employment
Contract will be assessed. If the Board does not use an evaluation instrument, but
Votes to extend the Contract of the Superintendent, that vote shall constitute an
evaluation of the Superintendent’s job performance. The failure of the Board to
evaluate the Superintendent or to extend the Contract of the Superintendent into
the future after an evaluation of the Superintendent shall not constitute a material
breach of this Contract.
b. Performance Targets- The Board and the Superintendent agree that it is in the best

interest of the District for all performance targets, established pursuant to Ark. Code
Ann. § 6-17-123, be ambitious and outcome-focused. Such goals and performance

targets shall be mutually developed and documented in the Appendix to this
Agreement. Failure to fully achieve one or more individual performance target shall
not, by itself, constitute a material breach of this Agreement. Rather, progress toward

such goals shall be reviewed and considered by the Board as part of the
Superintendent’s annual performance evaluation.
10. Suspension

a. If, by majority vote, the Board votes to suspend the Superintendent from the
performance of his or her duties under this Contract, the Superintendent will
surrender all school district property with the exception of his or her district
owned cell phone, and shall remain available to the school district for
consultation, advice, and to return to duties. Every day that the Superintendent is
not available to the school district shall be accounted for as a vacation day, or, if
appropriate documentation from a physician is presented and subject to district
policy, a sick day(s). If a suspended Superintendent wishes to accept other
employment during a term of suspension, to resign from his or her Agreement, or
to retire as the word is defined by the Arkansas Teacher Retirement System, the
Board agrees to immediately release him or her from his or her contractual
obligation to be available to the district. At the end of 60 consecutive days of paid
suspension, unless the Agreement has expired on its own terms, the
Superintendent will be recalled to duty unless terminated.

11. Separation and Termination

a. Separation Without Cause.
The Board may separate the Superintendent from employment without cause upon
written notice to the Superintendent. In the event of separation without cause, the
District shall pay the Superintendent severance equal to eighteen (18) months of
the Superintendent’s base salary and District-paid benefits. Such severance shall
constitute the Superintendent’s exclusive remedy for separation without cause.
b. Termination for Material Breach or Other Just Cause.
In the event that a majority of the Board votes to initiate termination proceedings
against the Superintendent for material breach of this Agreement or other just
cause, written notice shall be provided to the Superintendent, and the
Superintendent shall be afforded an opportunity for a hearing before the Board.
The decision of the Board shall be final.
c. Notice and Opportunity to Cure.
Except in cases involving criminal conduct, moral turpitude, or conduct
presenting an immediate risk to student or employee safety, the Superintendent
shall be provided written notice of any alleged material breach or other just cause
and shall be given a reasonable opportunity, not less than sixty (60) days, to cure
such breach prior to termination.

d. Definition of Material Breach and Just Cause.
For purposes of this Agreement, a “Material Breach” or “Just Cause” shall

be limited to serious misconduct or failure of stewardship that materially impairs
the Superintendent’s ability to serve as chief executive officer of the District.
Material Breach or Just Cause shall include, but not be limited to, the following:

(i) Criminal Conduct. The Superintendent being formally charged with or
convicted of a felony or other crime involving moral turpitude, fraud,
dishonesty, abuse of office, or violence, where there exists indisputable
evidence supporting such charge, or upon conviction of any criminal offense
during the Superintendent’s tenure that materially undermines the
Superintendent’s ability to serve the District.
(ii) Fiscal Distress. After twenty-four (24) consecutive months of
employment, the District being designated as being in Fiscal Distress as
defined by Ark. Code Ann. §6-20-1901 et seq., shall constitute a Material
Breach of this Agreement, provided that the Board has approved and upheld
the Superintendent’s recommendations regarding reductions in force (RIF),
non-renewal of employment agreements, and other corrective personnel
actions; and the Board has not acted contrary to the Superintendent’s
recommendations regarding contracts, hiring, or expenditures that, if
followed, would have reasonably prevented the District from being placed in
Fiscal Distress status.
(c) Licensure or Child Welfare Findings. Any adverse or disciplinary
finding by the Professional Licensure Standards Board, or any substantiated
or “true” finding of child abuse or maltreatment under applicable state law.
(d) Improper Personal Relationships. Entering into, or engaging in, a
dating, romantic, or sexual relationship with a current District employee
during the Superintendent's tenure of office.
(e) Ethical Misconduct. Gross negligence, willful misconduct, or a material
Violation of law, Board policy, or ethical standards that materially impairs the
Superintendent’s ability to perform the duties of the position.

e. Effect of Termination for Cause.

In the event of termination for material breach or other just cause, the

Superintendent shall be entitled to compensation earned through the effective date

of termination and payment for any unused accrued leave as otherwise provided

in this Agreement, but shall not be entitled to severance.

f. Transition Cooperation.

In the event of separation from employment, the Superintendent agrees to

reasonably cooperate in the transition of leadership for a period of up to sixty (60)

days, provided such cooperation does not interfere with other employment and

Temains subject to the severance provisions of this Agreement.

12. Waiver

a. The failure of either party to insist, in any one or more instances, upon
performance of any of the terms or conditions of this Contract shall not be
construed as a waiver or relinquishment of any right granted hereunder or of the

future performance of any such terms or conditions, but the obligations of either
party shall continue in full force and effect.
13. Severability
a. In case any provision of the Contract shall be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, the
validity, legality, and enforceability of the remaining provisions shall not in any
way be affected or impaired thereby.
14, Entire Agreement
a. This instrument contains the entire Contract of the parties. It may not be changed
orally, but only by an agreement in writing signed by all parties thereof.
15. Indemnification; Legal Representation
a. The District shall indemnify and hold the Superintendent harmless from any and
all claims, actions, suits and proceedings at law or equity brought against the
Superintendent in his or her capacity and arising out of his or her reasonable and
lawful actions as Superintendent and employee of the Board; provided, however,
that the District reserves the right to select such counsel to represent the
Superintendent subject to the Superintendent’s consent, such consent not to be
unreasonably withheld.
16. Governing Law
a. This Contract shall be governed by the laws of the State of Arkansas.
17, Amendment.
a. This Agreement cannot be amended, modified or revised unless done in writing
and signed by the Board and the Superintendent.
18. Binding Effect.
a. The terms and conditions contained in this Agreement will bind and inure to the

benefit of the parties, their respective heirs, executors, administrators, successors
and assigns.

Superintendent Performance Targets

. District Level Student Achievement

a. Grade Level Measurement (Literacy)

i. For 24-25 the percentage of District Students who tested at grade level
was 44%. The superintendent is given the performance target to increase
the percentage of District students who test at grade level:

1. In the 26-27 test cycle to 51%.

School Level Student Achievement (required for any district school that received a
C, D, or F letter Grade)
a. Grade Level Measurement (Literacy)

i. The superintendent is given the performance target to increase the
percentage of students at each campus with a letter grade of C or below
who test at grade level by seven (7) percentage points by the 26-27 test
cycle.

Graduation Rate (Latest data is 23-24)
a. District Level
i. The present 4 year cohort graduation rate for the district is 87.82%
measuring the students who should have been in 9th grade in the
2020-2021 school year based on their age. For students entering the 4 year
cohort in 2025-2026 the superintendent’s performance goal is to improve
the 4 year cohort graduation rate to 90% or above for the 28-29 school
year.
