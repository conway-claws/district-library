## GOAL 1: Literacy Achievement & Growth

### <u>Area of Concern/SMART Goal:</u>

By May 2026, increase the percentage of students scoring “Ready” or above on the ATLAS assessment in ELA from 37.74% to 47%, and increase growth for the lowest 25% of students in ELA from 50% to 60%, by implementing a comprehensive literacy framework aligned with the Science of Reading.

Rationale: While the school demonstrates strong growth (65.76%) overall in ELA, proficiency rates remain well below the state average. The lowest 25% of students are showing signs of positive growth, but this subgroup requires sustained, intensive support to maintain momentum.

### <u>Strategies:</u>

1. **Benchmark with Integrity (K–4):** All K–4 teachers will fully implement the Benchmark Advance Reading Program as designed, ensuring core instruction is delivered consistently and aligned with the curriculum pacing.
2. **Use of the Amplify Tier II Intervention Program with Integrity (K–4):** Amplify will be implemented with integrity to deliver structured Tier II interventions. Activity teachers will assist in small group delivery to support student learning outcomes.

3. **Progress Monitoring Using DIBELS 8th Edition:** All students, including those performing below, at, or above benchmark, will be progress monitored regularly using DIBELS. Data will be used monthly to drive instructional adjustments and identify student needs.
4. **Multi-Tiered System of Supports (MTSS) with RTI Process:** The RTI process will guide instructional decisions. Students demonstrating limited progress will receive modified or intensified interventions based on DIBELS, Benchmark, and Amplify data.
5. **WIN (What I Need) Intervention Block Implementation:** A designated daily WIN block will be used for targeted instruction. Teachers, interventionists, and activity staff will provide differentiated support through both in-class and pull-out models.
6. **95% Group Phonics Instruction with Integrity (K–4):** Teachers will implement the 95% Group Phonics curriculum with integrity, providing explicit and systematic phonics instruction that builds foundational literacy skills in all learners.
7. **RISE Framework Implementation with Integrity (K–4):** All teachers will utilize RISE-aligned practices, including structured literacy routines and decodable texts, to support phonics and phonemic awareness development in early literacy.
8. **Monthly Progress Monitoring via Data Walls and RTI Meetings:** A school-wide data wall will be used to track and visualize student progress. Monthly RTI meetings will focus on analyzing trends, making grouping decisions, and adjusting supports as needed.
9. **Weekly PLC Collaboration and Data-Driven Planning:** Teachers will meet weekly in PLCs to collaboratively analyze student data, unpack standards, and plan instruction. Emphasis will be placed on accelerating growth for students in the lowest performance bands.
10.**Instructional Coaching and Lesson Internalization:** Administrators and the Instructional Coach will support lesson internalization and planning sessions to ensure literacy instruction is purposeful, responsive to data, and implemented with integrity.
### <u>Professional Development:</u>

- **DIBELS Training:** Staff will receive training on administering DIBELS and analyzing results to guide instruction.
- **WIN Implementation:** Teachers will be supported in forming WIN groups and planning targeted instruction based on student data.
- **Amplify Data Use:** Staff will learn how to interpret and use Amplify data to update the data wall and adjust instruction.
- **Data-Driven Interventions:** Teachers will be trained to make timely changes to interventions based on student progress.
- **Collaborative Planning:** Teachers will meet weekly with the Instructional Coach and administrators to review data and internalize lessons.
- **RTI/Data Team Support:** The data team will help monitor student progress and determine who needs RTI support.

## GOAL 2: Mathematics Proficiency and Engagement

### <u>Area of Concern/SMART Goal:</u>

By May 2026, increase the percentage of students scoring “Ready” or above on the ATLAS interim assessments in Mathematics from **39.62% to 50%**, and increase the growth of the lowest 25% in math from **40.91% to 50%**, by strengthening engagement, personalized instruction, and utilizing data-driven practices.

### <u>Strategies:</u>

1. **Use of Eureka Math Squared with Integrity (Grades K–4):** Teachers will implement the Eureka Math Squared curriculum with integrity, ensuring that all lessons are paced and taught according to the curriculum guidelines to ensure mastery of grade-level content.
2. **Data-Driven Differentiation:** Teachers will regularly analyze data from ATLAS Interim assessments and formative checks to differentiate instruction for all students, including those performing below, at, or above grade level.
3. **Small Group Instruction and Math Interventions:** Tier II and Tier III math interventions will be implemented based on data, with small group instruction tailored to meet students' specific needs in foundational math skills.
4. **Engaging Hands-On Learning Activities:** Teachers will incorporate engaging, hands-on math activities, including real-world problem-solving tasks and math games, to increase student interest and engagement.
5. **Daily Math Fluency Practice:** Teachers will include daily math fluency practice, focusing on building fact recall and problem-solving speed. This will include both individual practice and group work.
6. **Monthly Data Reviews and Actionable Adjustments:** Teachers will review student data monthly, adjust instructional strategies, and make any necessary changes to interventions, ensuring that every student’s needs are met.
7. **Collaborative Planning for Math Instruction:** Teachers will engage in weekly collaboration with the Instructional Coach and administrators to share best practices, review student performance data, and plan targeted math lessons.
8. **Parental Engagement in Math Learning:** Monthly math-related family engagement activities will be designed to encourage parental involvement in math learning at home and increase student motivation.
### <u>Professional Development:</u>

- **Math Focus During Staff Meetings:** Every staff meeting will include a dedicated math focus, allowing teachers to discuss strategies, share insights, and refine practices for math instruction.
- **Family Engagement Techniques:** PD will include strategies for involving parents in math learning at home through monthly family engagement activities.
- **Data Analysis and Differentiation:** Teachers will participate in professional development on how to effectively analyze data from ATLAS Interim assessments and differentiate math instruction
