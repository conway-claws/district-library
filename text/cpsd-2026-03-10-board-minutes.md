Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street March 10, 2026

<u>Members Present</u>: Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Mr. Trip Leach, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty, Mrs. Ashley Lyon

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jason Black; Interim Deputy Superintendent Dr. Karen Lasker; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Bryce Bennett; Directors: Preston Echols, Kelli Gordon, Shawn Higginbotham, Eric King, Shanda New; General Counsel Shasta Wagner; Strategic Planning Consultant Dr. Jeff Standridge; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Champion Student Recognition</u>

Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, introduced Amelia Waits, Champion Student of the Month from Bob and Betty Courtway Middle School, and presented a video featuring Amelia. Superintendent Black presented her with a certificate. Amelia led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Conway High School Girls Bowling State Champions – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized the Conway High School Girls Bowling team for winning the state championship for the third year.
- Board Member Recognition – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized Dr. Naylor, Jr. for serving on the Board for the last 4 years.
- Career Fair Recap Video by UCA-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video of the Career Fair created by UCA student reporter Ana Brandon for UCA News 6.
## <u>Patrons Comments</u>

- Kathy Dolan – Ms. Dolan spoke to the Board about her concerns with the literacy rate and asks for clarity on the progress of the District.
- Alyce Hardee – Ms. Hardee spoke to the Board about her concerns about things happening during Board meetings.
- Audie Alumbaugh – Ms. Alumbaugh spoke to the Board about the use of District funds.
- Tami Marsh – Mrs. Marsh spoke to the Board about her concerns with the District’s attorneys at last month’s Board meeting.
- Jimmie Cavin – Mr. Cavin spoke to the Board about honesty, transparency, and trust. Dr. Kathy Sweere and Dr. Bennett addressed concerns patrons have about literacy.

Board members discussed concerns over the proposed settlement from the FOIA lawsuit.

## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Election Update – Superintendent Black gave an update on the school election. He asked General Counsel Mrs. Wagner to discuss the timeline of when the Board reorganizes after elections and run-off elections. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 7:18 p.m. on a motion made by Mr.** **Cummings III and seconded by Mr. Leach.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – February 10, 2026, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in February 2026
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of List of Capital Assets Retired 07/01/24 – 06/30/2025
- Approval of E-Rate Resolution
- Approval of 2026-2027 Statement of Assurances
- Approval of Appointment of District Treasurer – Shawn Higginbotham
- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline.

The** **motion was seconded by Dr. Petty and passed unanimously at 7:34 p.m.**
