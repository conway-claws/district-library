# 8.34—CLASSIFIED PERSONNEL FRINGE BENEFITS

A one-time retirement bonus payment shall be paid to all full-time personnel who have terminated employment in the Conway Public School District and meet all requirements for retirement under the Arkansas Teacher Retirement System/State Employees Retirement System. The computation for such bonus payment shall be developed by the superintendent’s office and approved by the Conway Public School District Board of Education.

A lifetime athletic pass shall be given to all full-time classified staff members who have terminated employment in the Conway School District and meet all requirements for retirement under the Arkansas Teacher Retirement System/ Arkansas Public Employees Retirement System.

Staff shall have a fringe benefit of being allowed to enroll their children in any elementary school of their choice provided space is available.

## Classified Personnel Fringe Benefit Formula

Retirement bonus payment shall be computed using the following formula:

$10.00 x each year through the first ten (10) years of employment in Conway Public School District, plus one (1) dollar per additional year experience in Conway Public School District.

Upon full retirement from the Conway Public Schools in accordance with the rules and guidelines of the Arkansas Teacher Retirement System an employee will be paid $20.00 for each unused sick leave day. This one-time payment will occur after the employee’s last day of employment. Payment for sick leave days will be credited to the employee’s primary position only. (The primary position is defined as the position that requires the employee to work the most

hours.) In addition, the employee will receive $10.00 for each year of experience that he/she worked in his/her primary position.

An employee will qualify to be paid at a greater amount for unused full sick leave days for his/her primary position if he/she meets both of the following qualifications: (1) has worked in a full-time capacity (defined as having worked at least twenty [20] hours per week) in the Conway Public Schools for at least 10 years; and (2) has at least ninety (90) days of accumulated unused sick leave. If the employee meets both of these conditions, he/she will be reimbursed at the rate of $35.00 for each unused sick day up to the ninetieth (90 th ) full day of unused sick leave. For any full day of unused sick leave above ninety (90) days, the employee will be reimbursed at their daily rate of pay as of the last day of their contract.

In the case of the death of an employee of the Conway Public Schools, the employee’s estate will be paid the amount that the employee would have received under the same terms and conditions as if he/she had fully retired.
