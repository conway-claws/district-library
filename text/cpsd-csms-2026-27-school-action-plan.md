# Carl Stuart Middle School School Improvement Plan

# 2026-2027

## <u>Area of Concern</u>

Carl Stuart Middle School implemented a flexible and responsive third period in order to respond to student assessment data in the 2025 school year. According to aggregated ATLAS interim data from the 2026 school year, 40% of Carl Stuart Middle School students demonstrate proficiency in English Language Arts (ELA). The remaining 60% of students are performing below the proficiency threshold, with 24% at Level 1 and 35% at Level 2.

Students who are identified as level one students demonstrate a “limited understanding of knowledge and skills,” while students who are identified as level two “demonstrate a basic understanding of knowledge and skills required of the grade-level standards.”

## <u>Goal</u>

On the 2025 ATLAS Summative, 52.87% of students achieved proficiency; however, on the Winter ATLAS Interim, 45% were proficient.

Carl Stuart Middle School’s goal is to increase student proficiency from 52,87% to 55%.

## <u>Strategies</u>

Data Review and Analysis

- Regularly scheduled team data meetings will be conducted in order to review common formative assessments (CFAs), identify trends, and pinpoint student needs.
- Instructional facilitators, administrators, and teachers will examine which standards or question types students struggled with in order to inform instruction.
- Teachers will identify students in the bottom quartile and deliver targeted Tier II instruction using data-driven small-group interventions aligned to identified skill deficits.
Tiered Intervention

- Identify students performing below proficiency and invite students to small-group intervention sessions based on specific standards.
- Reading interventionists will provide targeted Tier II comprehension interventions aligned to identified student needs, based on ELA/Reading PLC data analysis and ongoing formative assessments.
- 5th and 6th grade students will be enrolled in a guided intervention model that differentiates instruction by providing targeted intervention or enrichment based on ongoing assessment data.
Student Engagement

- Continue to implement Robert Marzano High-Yield Instructional Strategies
- Coach students on identifying areas of growth through learning ladder discussions.

# 2026-2027

Carl Stuart Middle School has established clear behavioral expectations and systems of support for students who struggle to meet them. During the 2024–2025 school year, the school implemented a common behavior matrix outlining expectations across all settings. In 2025–2026, Carl Stuart strengthened its approach by implementing a discipline referral process that distinguishes between classroom-managed and office-managed behaviors, while also empowering teachers to collaborate through Student Intervention Teams (SIT) to provide Tier II behavioral support. Building on this foundation, Carl Stuart aims to refine and strengthen its behavior systems and goals for the 2026–2027 school year.

## <u>Goal</u>

As of April 3, 2026, Carl Stuart Middle School is on goal to meet its 2025-2026 goal of reducing student disciplinary incidents by 10% or 115 incidents. As of April 3, 2026 we have decreased discipline by 117 incidents or 11.4%.

For the 2026-2027 school year, Carl Stuart would like to decrease exclusionary discipline rates (i.e. out-of-school suspension and in-school suspension) by 15% through the use of Tier II interventions.

## <u>Strategies</u>

Positive Behavior Interventions and Supports (PBIS)

- Track pro-social behavior through Educators’ Handbook and recognize students who positively contribute to our school culture.
Building-Wide Behavior and Procedural Expectations

- Implement common language and procedures for the building in order to ensure smooth transitions throughout the day.
- Implement common expectations for behavior (e.g. treating others with respect). Utilize WIN time to educate students about respect, compassion, etc.
- Proactively discuss and teach expectations for all areas of our school.
Build Strong Student-Adult Relationships

- Work to facilitate discussions among teachers regarding which students need additional support and then pair them with a mentor.
- Provide time for teachers to meet in Student Intervention Teams (SIT) and develop Tier II behavior goals for students through communicating with home and check-in/check-out systems.
Restorative Discipline Practices

- Addressing conflict through student mediation
- Student reflection document to ensure student understands how they did not meet expectations.
