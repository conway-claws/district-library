Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street October 10, 2023

<u>Members Present</u>: Mr. André Acklin, Mr. Jason Sandefer, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Trey Geier

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Clint Ashcraft, Ben Darley, Kelli Gordon, Paula Harris, Keisha Jefferson, Eric King, Jason Lawrence, Dr. Tammy Woosley; Chief Financial Officer Robyn Keene; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Acklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video featuring Naeem Ramos- Martinez, Champion Student of the Month from Bob and Betty Courtway Middle School. She welcomed Principal David Yates, who introduced Naeem. Superintendent Collum presented Naeem with a certificate. Naeem led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- National Merit Scholarship Program Semifinalists – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Conway High School Principal Dr. Worley to introduce the District’s National Merit Semifinalists: Ellie Feng, Kaylee Francis, Evangeline Montgomery, Jacob Regehr, Henry Ross, James Xin, and Tim Yan.
- Blue Heart Community Partner Award –Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video featuring Gayle Lamey, Crossing Guard at Ida Burns Elementary and thanked her for her dedication and support to Conway Public Schools.
- National Principals Appreciation Month – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized the District’s Principals for National Principals Appreciation Month and thanked them for their dedication to Conway Public Schools. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:15 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mrs. Franklin and seconded by Mr. Leach.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – September 12, 2023, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in September 2023
- Approval of Act 1599 Resolution
- Approval of Annual Equity Compliance Report
- Approval of Transportation Buses for Sale

- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline.
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- School Election Update – Superintendent Collum discussed the school election timeline for the upcoming election in March.
- Champions Community Circles Event – Superintendent Collum discussed the plans for the Champions Community Circles event at Bob and Betty Courtway Middle School on October 17 th from 6:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m.
## <u>Chief Financial Officer’s Report</u>
