Minutes of Board of Education Special Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street November 6, 2025

<u>Members Present</u>: Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Interim Superintendent Jason Black; Interim Deputy Superintendent Karen Lasker; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Bryce Bennett; Directors Gynger Campbell, Preston Echols, Greg Hughes, Eric King, Shanda New; General Counsel Shasta Wagner; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Hearing Officer Keith Billingsley; Employee’s Attorneys Chad Trammel and Melody Piazza; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Interim Superintendent Black led the audience in the Pledge of allegiance.

The Board entered Executive Session to discuss a settlement offer for William Lindsey at 6:05 p.m. and exited Executive Session at 6:48 p.m.

A personnel hearing was conducted for a recommendation by Interim Superintendent Black to terminate William Lindsey’s employment contract.

General Counsel Shasta Wagner called witnesses for testimony.

The Board called a recess at 8:25 p.m. and reconvened at 8:34 p.m.

General Counsel Shasta Wagner and Attorneys Chad Trammel and Melody Piazza continued with witness testimonies.

The Board called a recess at 9:42 p.m. and reconvened at 9:55 p.m.

Following testimony, the Board entered Executive Session for the consideration of termination at 11:25

p.m. and exited Executive Session at 12:41 a.m.
# Ms. Walton made a motion to accept the recommendation of the Interim Superintendent to

# terminate the employment contract of William Lindsey. The motion was seconded by Dr.

**Petty on a 5-2 vote at 12:42 a.m. Voting for the motion were Mrs. Franklin, Ms. Walton, Mr.** **Cummings III, Mr. Leach, and Dr. Petty. Voting against the motion were Dr. Naylor, Jr. and Mrs. Hargis.**

The Board entered Executive Session to discuss Interim Superintendent Black’s employment reassignment recommendations at 12:42 a.m. and exited Executive Session at 12:55 a.m.

# Dr. Naylor, Jr. made a motion to accept the recommendation of the Interim Superintendent

# to reassign Principal Joey Moon to the Head Basketball Coach position. The motion was

# seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 12:55 a.m.

# Mrs. Hargis made a motion to accept the recommendation of the Interim Superintendent to

# reassign Assistant Principal KaRon Branch to the Principal position at Raymond and Phyllis

**Simon Middle School. The motion was seconded by Mr. Leach and passed unanimously at 12:56 a.m.**

<u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at 12:56 a.m.** **on a motion made by Mr. Cummings III and seconded by Mrs. Hargis.**

Respectfully submitted,

<u>/s/ Sheila Franklin /s/ Leo Cummings III</u> President Board Secretary
