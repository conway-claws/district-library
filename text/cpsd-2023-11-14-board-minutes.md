She welcomed Principal Yvonne Sturdivant, who introduced Avery. Superintendent Collum presented her with a certificate. Avery led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Annual Report to the Public – Superintendent Collum presented the 2023 Annual Report to the Board and to the public and presented a video.
- School Election Update – Superintendent Collum presented an update on the school election. Two Board positions will be on the ballot: Zone 4, Position 6 and Zone 5, Position
3. In Zone 4, Board member Trip Leach seeks re-election and Ruthan Browne seeks election. In Zone 5, Board member Trey Geier seeks re-election and Bill Milburn is seeking election. No millage increase is being sought. The total proposed school tax levy of 38.1 mills represents the same rate presently being levied.
- All Staff Bonus Preview – Superintendent Collum discussed the proposed one-time bonus of $750.00 for all current employees.
## <u>Chief Financial Officer’s Report</u>

- All Staff Bonus Proposal – Robyn Keene recommended that the Board provide a one-time bonus of $750.00 non-recurring bonus for all current employees. The total cost of the bonus including fringe benefits for all fund sources will be approximately $1,135,000.00. **Mrs. Hargis made the motion to approve the one-time $750.00 non-recurring bonus for all** **current employees. The motion was seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr. and passed unanimously at 6:56 p.m.**
- Board Financial Report – Robyn Keene presented the financial report for October.
## Mr. Sandefer made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion

## was seconded by Mrs. Franklin and passed unanimously at 6:59 p.m.
