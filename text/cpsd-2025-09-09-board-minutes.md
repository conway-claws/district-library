Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Patrons Comments</u>

- Bill Dial – Mr. Dial spoke to the Board about the Career Center and how beneficial it is to students and the District.
- Knowles Adkisson – Mr. Adkisson spoke to the Board about artificial intelligence in education. He talked about the Board’s view on artificial intelligence and asked the Board to ask the superintendent candidates their views on it.
- Antonia Byrd – Ms. Byrd spoke to the Board about the increase of pay for paraprofessionals in the District and thanked them for it but feels it still falls short.
