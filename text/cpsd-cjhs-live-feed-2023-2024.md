CJHS Highlights: Thelma Moton

Thelma Moton, a trailblazing force, is not just the Founder and CEO of Choosing to Excel (C2E) in Central Arkansas; she's a relentless advocate for youth and families, boasting over 30 years of impactful commitment. Born amidst twelve siblings in Twin Groves, Arkansas, Thelma, a product of both segregated and integrated education, is driven by a profound mission to transform the lives of Arkansas youth one choice at a time.

With an annual reach of over 10,000 youth spanning multiple counties, C2E is a beacon of positive change.

Beyond the numbers, C2E's Nurturing Families component is a lifeline for parents and teen parents, fostering healthy interactions, reducing teen pregnancy, and providing crucial support for preventive healthcare. Thelma's brainchild, the Youth Leadership Initiative, empowers young voices, emphasizing community involvement and decision-making. Their rallying cry, "Nothing About Us Without Us," echoes the belief that every young person deserves a voice and recognition.

Thelma, married for 50 years with eleven children and seventeen grandchildren, is more than a leader—she's a catalyst for change. With a mantra of "Keep The End In Mind," she understands that combining information with trusting relationships in various settings creates lasting bonds, fostering behavioral change and optimal health.

Thelma's journey is a testament to collaboration, weaving together community leaders, school boards, parents, and local governing bodies. She doesn't just promote the "CHOICE TO EXCEL"; she lives it, embodying the belief that everyone, regardless of circumstances, deserves a safe, nurturing haven because, in Thelma's world, "they matter."

Mr. Maull was born November 24, 1970 in Dequeen, Arkansas. H was the first male child of Felix and Annie Maull. He states that he strives to pass that on to his students--Learn as much as you can. Do your best and be willing to accept the results.  

In 1989, Mr. Maull graduated from DeQueen High School as an honor student, ranked 7th in his class. In the fall of 1995, Mr. Maull became the first African-American mathematics teacher to be hired in the Conway Public School District. In that same year he married the love of his life and two children- Nathan and Sarah- were added to this union.  

During his tenure, Mr. Maull has taught 8th grade math, Inclusion math,  Accelerated Algebra 1, and Accelerated Geometry.  He has also served on several educational committees both local and nationwide.

He states, "My greatest achievements do not lie in what  I have done in my life;  but rather when I see my children, students and everyone I encounter succeed."

In 2011, Penny's life took another significant turn when she met her husband, Jacob Curran, on a blind date. Her daily efforts reflect her dedication to making the Conway community a more inclusive and welcoming place for individuals of different cultural backgrounds and ethnicities, ensuring that her impact extends beyond the classroom.
