**3.1** CJHS builds staff capacity to work with parents as equal partners through training at different times during the school year. School staff will be aware that parents play an integral role in assisting student learning and only through working together as two part team will

students reach optimal levels of individual success. As a bi-fold team, at school and home we will work through challenges to assist our students. Staff will show their ability to welcome parents into school and seek their parental support and assistance during the following times:

- Spring Jr. High Parent Night/Orientation-Annually
- Open House-Annually
- Parents are given a school progress report and an overview of the following:
- Student academic progress and absences - 4 times a year
- Parent Teacher Conferences-Bi-annually
- Parent Orientation night for incoming 8th grade parents
These times will be for staff and parents to discuss how to meet the needs for students in an individualized approach, as well as, implement plans for staff and parents to work together. These times can also be used to educate staff and parents on effective ways to resolve concerns through the handbook. As situations arise, staff will work with parents to identify problems and the proper chain of command to communicate through. Parents will understand that teachers are the first line of defense in correcting any problems in the learning experience of their child and that the teachers are going to be the most aware of how to deal with the issues. Also, working with the teacher will be the best approach to develop solutions. Building staff capacity starts with understanding that creating open lines of communications is the start to developing trust and respect with parents. Once this is fostered we can work together in the best manner possible to meet the needs of the students in our classrooms.

**4.1** CJHS prioritizes the dissemination of timely and comprehensive information regarding the school's curriculum, academic assessments, and expected student achievement benchmarks through various channels. This vital information is meticulously curated and made easily accessible to stakeholders, prominently featured on the school's official website. Additionally, to foster a strong partnership with parents and ensure transparency, a dedicated back-to-school parent meeting is organized each fall. During these informative sessions, parents are provided with in- depth explanations and insights into the curriculum utilized at CJHS, the methodologies of academic assessments employed, and the specific achievement levels that students are expected to attain. This collaborative approach aims to empower parents with the knowledge and understanding necessary to actively

support their children's educational journey and foster a conducive learning environment both at home and within the school community.

**4.2** At CJHS, we are committed to supporting parents in navigating the requirements of Title I, understanding how to effectively monitor their child's progress, and collaborating with educators to enhance their children's academic achievements through tailored approaches. This essential guidance is readily available on the school's website and is comprehensively communicated to parents during the annual back-to-school parent meeting held in the fall. Furthermore, to ensure ongoing support and engagement, information on monitoring their child's progress and fostering effective communication with school staff is reiterated throughout the year during conferences, facilitated by the administration, counselors, or teachers as needed. This proactive and inclusive approach aims to empower parents with the knowledge and resources necessary to actively participate in their child's educational journey and foster a collaborative partnership between home and school. Other approaches include HAC and parent-access Schoology codes.
**4.3** CJHS actively collaborates with parents to enhance their understanding of strategies aimed at improving their children's academic performance. Parents are urged to initiate communication with individual teachers whenever they have concerns regarding their child's academic progress. Through this collaborative effort, parents and teachers work together to identify personalized solutions tailored to the unique needs of each student. In some instances, practical assistance is provided to parents in the form of hands-on materials and resources to be utilized at home, along with guidance on their effective implementation. Additionally, school staff offer training sessions to equip parents with the necessary skills to utilize technological resources both within and outside the classroom setting, further enriching the educational experience for students and fostering a strong partnership between the school and parents.
**4.4** Parents will be provided an opportunity to volunteer in a variety of activities which may include but are not limited to the following clubs and/or organizations:
- PAWS
- Interest Inventory will be distributed to parent volunteers to determine preference for service, abilities, and area of interest.
- Parent Involvement Plan
- Athletics – Football, Basketball, Baseball, Soccer, Track, Wrestling, Volleyball, Bowling, Cheer and Dance

- Fine Arts – Band, Choir, Art, and Drama
- Academic Clubs – Beta, Foreign Language, NHD and FFA, FBLA, FCCLA, Quizbowl
- Student Organizations – Student Council
- CatNation Incentives
- Male Mentor program
**4.5** CJHS actively advocates for and supports responsible parenting through both verbal encouragement and tangible actions. School staff engage in direct conversations with parents, both face-to-face and via social media platforms, to discuss various opportunities and optimal moments for parents to engage actively in their children's academic journeys. These discussions encompass avenues for parental volunteerism and involvement, as well as updates on school activities and scheduled events that encourage parental participation. Moreover, CJHS maintains a dedicated Parent Resource Center located in the school's front office waiting area. This resource hub offers a wealth of informational materials, including parenting books, magazines, and other relevant resources, which parents can peruse and even borrow to review at home. By providing such comprehensive support and resources, CJHS endeavors to empower parents to play an active and informed role in their children's education, fostering a collaborative partnership between the school and parents for the benefit of student success.
**4.6** CJHS offers guidance to parents on integrating developmentally appropriate learning activities into the home environment through various channels. This valuable information is readily accessible on the school website and is also shared with parents during the annual back-to-school parent meeting held in the fall. Additionally, parents can request this information, and school staff may proactively provide it when they observe a need. School administrators ensure that parents are informed about utilizing the Division of Elementary and Secondary Education website and how it can contribute to improving their child's academic outcomes. Furthermore, teachers and counselors may initiate discussions with parents regarding topics such as nutritional meal planning and personal hygiene, tailored to the individual student's needs. These conversations underscore the importance of meeting the child's basic needs to enable them to excel academically both in school and in life. By fostering such collaboration and communication, CJHS strives to empower parents with the knowledge and resources needed to support their children's holistic development and academic success.
