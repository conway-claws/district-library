Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street January 9, 2024

<u>Members Present</u>: Mr. André Acklin, Mr. Jason Sandefer, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Trey Geier

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Ben Darley, Kelli Gordon, Paula Harris, Keisha Jefferson, Sarah Jerry, Eric King, Jason Lawrence; Chief Financial Officer Robyn Keene; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Acklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- ASBA Master Board Member Award-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized Board member Linda Hargis for receiving the Master Board Member award from Arkansas School Boards Association for completing at least fifty (50) hours of board development training. Dr. Collum presented Mrs. Hargis with a plaque.
- Board Appreciation – January is School Board Appreciation Month. Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented the Board with a presentation of pictures from students and staff from all the schools thanking the Board for their service to the District.
## <u>Champion Student Recognition</u>

- Jaxon McCray-Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video featuring Jaxon McCray, Champion Student of the Month from Carl Stuart Middle School. She welcomed Principal Gynger Campbell, who introduced Jaxon. Superintendent Collum presented him with a certificate. Jaxon led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance. [Mr. Sandefer left the meeting at 6:11 p.m.] <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:14 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – December 12, 2023, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in December 2023
- Approval of Purchase Order
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of Act 1599 Resolution

- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline. The Board discussed the Act 1599 Resolution and asked Principal Mark Lewis questions about the application process for this position.
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Board Member Training Hours Report – Superintendent Collum reported to the public the number of professional development training hours the Board members received in calendar year 2023. [Mr. Sandefer entered the meeting at 6:17 p.m.]
## <u>Chief Financial Officer’s Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Robyn Keene presented the financial report for December. **Mrs. Franklin made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mr. Sandefer and passed unanimously at 6:21 p.m.**
- 2022-2023 Audit Report – Mrs. Keene invited Mark Glover, HCJ CPAs & Advisors, PLLC, to present the 2022-2023 Audit.

## <u>Information Items</u>

- CEAL Leadership Program Update – Assistant Superintendent Duncan presented an update on the CEAL Leadership Program.

The** **motion was seconded by Mr. Geier and passed unanimously at 7:05 p.m.** <u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mr. Sandefer and seconded by Mrs. Hargis, the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 7:05 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**
