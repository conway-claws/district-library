Important information for Advanced Placement Exams at Conway High School:

Students are responsible for knowing when and where their testing takes place. If you have questions, please contact your teacher, Mr. Hendricks (Hendricksm@conwayschools.info) or Dr. Woosley (Woosleyt@conwayschools.info) for assistance. 

The AP Testing Schedule can be found at https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/ap-exams-overview/exam-calendar

Testing Times: 
8:00AM (please report to location by 7:30AM)
12:00PM (please report to location by 11:30AM) 
Students who have multiple exams in one day (8:00AM and 12:00PM) will need to bring their own lunch. Time will be provided between testing to eat. 

Testing Reminders:
Leave your phones in your car or turned off. Phones may be placed on a table at the front of the room; however, if your phone rings, you will be removed from testing and the College Board will not score your exam.  
Arrange for transportation if you are taking an afternoon exam. Exams can run long and there is always a chance testing will not end before buses leave Conway High School. 

Student Absence from an AP Exam:
Students who do not take their exam will are responsible for a $40 fee to reimburse the district. Student names will be added to the fines and fees list.
If you are sick, or there is a family emergency, please contact Dr. Woosley before the end of the day in order to qualify for a late exam. Please note that The CollegeBoard makes the final decision to allow a late exam.
If there is a testing conflict (e.g. AAA sanctioned game) a test can be rescheduled. 
Students who miss an exam will not receive the weighted GPA for the course.

📣 Exciting News! Our Boys and Girls State representatives are heading to their respective events from May 26th to May 31st! 🎉 The Boys will be at UCA and the Girls at Harding University.

Congratulations to Barrett Carter and Kaeden Ingram, who will be attending Boys State, and to Ava Clagett (not pictured), Mia Farfan-Martinez, Ava Hamilton, Cecilia McCarthy-Galvan, and Elyse Ortega, who will be attending Girls State.

This journey began in January with informational sessions led by last year's participants. After a thorough application process and anonymous panel review in February, students were selected based on their leadership, teamwork, school involvement, and interest in politics.

Arkansas Girls State, sponsored by the ALA Department of Arkansas since 1942, has trained over 55,000 young women in citizenship and government. Similarly, Arkansas Boys State, since 1940, has immersed high school juniors in a week-long civic education program, preparing them as future leaders.

### 2024-06-01 · Conway High School (id 6071751)

Conway High School Students who are age 16 or older must have a second dose of the meningococcal vaccine (MCV4) if they received their first dose before the age of 16. Please note this is a state mandated requirement.

Appointments can be made at your Healthcare Provider, the Faulkner County Health Department (501-450-4941) and most pharmacies.

Please send the updated immunization record to Conway High School, fax 501-450-4884, Attention; School Nurse or Immunization records can be emailed to Amy Kordsmeier, RN at kordsmeiera@conwayschools.info

Immunization exemptions may be obtained from the Arkansas Department of Health Immunization Division at https://onlineimmunizationexemption.adh.arkansas.gov.
