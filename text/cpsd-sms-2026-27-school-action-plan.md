## Raymond & Phyllis Simon Middle School 2026-2027 School Improvement Plan

## <u>Area of Concern</u>

Based on Spring 2026 ATLAS data, 35% of students in grades 5–7 are performing at or above grade level in reading. Deficits remain in comprehension, vocabulary, and fluency, indicating a continued need for a systematic and explicit instructional alignment to the Science of Reading.

## <u>Literacy Goal</u>

By May 2027, the percentage of students in grades 5–7 scoring at or above grade level on the ATLAS Reading assessment will increase by 10%, as measured by ATLAS summative, interim assessments, and CFAs.

## <u>Strategies</u>

- Implement High-Quality Instructional Materials (HQIM) aligned to the Science of Reading with fidelity (Benchmark Reading, 95% fidelity).
- Provide explicit, systematic instruction in phonics, fluency, vocabulary, and comprehension.
- Embed literacy strategies across all content areas (disciplinary literacy).
- Utilize data-driven instruction through frequent CFA and interim data analysis cycles.
- Implement targeted Tier II and continue Tier III reading interventions based on student need.
- Expand student reading engagement through book clubs and community partnerships.
## <u>Professional Development</u>

- Ongoing district-supported PD on Science of Reading and HQIM implementation.
- Training on data analysis protocols and instructional decision-making.
- Targeted PD for teachers on high-quality Tier II instructional strategies (e.g., *Rite Flight*) to strengthen their ability to effectively implement interventions during core instruction, small group instruction, and WIN time.
- PLC cycles focused on lesson internalization, student work analysis, and intervention planning.
- Teacher training on disciplinary literacy strategies across content areas.
- Instructional coach-led modeling, co-teaching, and feedback cycles.

## <u>Area of Concern</u>

Discipline data indicates a continued high number of office discipline referrals resulting in ISS, OSS, Saturday School and detention, leading to loss of instructional time and negatively impacting school climate.

## <u>School Climate & Behavior Goal</u>

By May 2027, Simon Middle School will reduce ISS and OSS days by an additional **20%** (from 2025–2026 data) through implementation of PBIS and restorative practices.

## <u>Strategies</u>

- Implement a Multi-Tiered System of Supports (MTSS) for behavior.
- Strengthen PBIS framework with consistent expectations and reinforcement systems.
- Implement restorative practices to address conflict and repair harm.
- Provide Tier II and Tier III behavioral interventions.
- Conduct regular data analysis of discipline trends.
- Utilize Behavior RTI team for targeted student support.
- Continued implementation of grade level SIT (Student Intervention Teams)
- Implement two Behavior Interventionists.
- Incorporate a weekly, structured Social Emotional Learning (SEL) object lesson that helps students build skills such as self-awareness, emotional regulation, empathy, and responsible decision-making.
## <u>Professional Development</u>

- PBIS and MTSS training for all staff
- Restorative practices training
- Ongoing PLC/faculty sessions on behavior strategies
- Training on conducting Functional Behavior Assessments (FBAs)
- PD Palooza 2026
