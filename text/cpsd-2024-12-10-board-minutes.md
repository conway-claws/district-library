## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Staff Bonus Proposal – Superintendent Collum recommended that the Board provide a one- time bonus based on the following years of experience in education for all current CPSD employees: $3,000 for 30+ years, $2,500 for 20-29 years, $1,500 for 10-19 years, and $1,000 for 0-9 years. Employees voted by ballot for either Option 1 – amounts based on years of experience in education; or Option 2 – an amount of $500 to each employee. Out of 1,031 responses, Option 1 received 98.9% of the votes while Option 2 received 1.1% of the votes. **Mr. Barger made the motion to approve the bonus proposal, as presented, for all current** **CPSD employees. The motion was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at** **6:24 p.m.**
## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Dr. Andy Ashley presented the financial report for November. **Mrs. Franklin made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mr. Leach and passed unanimously at 6:29 p.m.**
## <u>Information Item</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Dr. Ashley and Director Eric King presented the academic report on Safety and Security Updates to Protect Instructional Time.
## Action Item

- Approval of 2025-2026 School Calendar – Director Gynger Campbell presented the proposed 2025-2026 School Calendar. Two calendar options had been approved by the Personnel Committees. Both options followed all rules and regulations required by statute. The options were presented to the staff for a vote. Out of 1,031 responses, Option A received 72.6% of the votes while Option B received 27.4% of the votes. **Mr. Kennedy made the motion to approve Option A, as presented, as the official calendar** **for the 2025-2026 school year. The motion was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 6:55 p.m.** <u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Mr. Leach, the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 6:55 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**
- Resignations
- Elections
