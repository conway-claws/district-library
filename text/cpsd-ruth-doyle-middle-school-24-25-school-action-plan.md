# Ruth Doyle Middle School School Action Plan

# 2024-2025

For the 2024-2025 school year, Ruth Doyle Middle School will continue to focus on the demonstrable needs of our students to ensure a successful middle school academic career. These include student academic improvement, behavioral, and social-emotional well-being supports. RDMS aims to improve and supplement the academic, behavioral, and emotional success of students using a variety of data, tools, and methods. This will involve improving and continuing to develop our school-wide programs to meet the prescriptions of Arkansas Act 1084. Also, RDMS will focus on specific strategies involving our Student Response Team (SRT), a designated intervention period during the school day, improvements to school-wide behavioral expectations, and a social-emotional curriculum to be implemented to all students.

## <u>Student Support</u>

## Student Response Team (SRT)

The SRT consists of administrators, counselors, and a district social worker. This team has been established and devotes its efforts to reviewing student referrals in the areas of academics, behavior, and social/emotional well-being of students on a monthly basis. The individuals that make up this team serve the school and are responsible for all of the listed areas in some capacity. The SRT focuses on identifying possible controllable factors and creating an individualized plan for the student. The SRT committee reviews overall attendance, academic, behavioral, and teacher input data and makes recommendations as needed for each student. The committee identifies target students for additional support. Student referrals are made by our certified faculty based on classroom data and observations from the school environment. Certified staff training for this program occurs during August professional development. This training includes the purpose, protocol, and past data. As each referral is reviewed by the SRT, an individualized plan for the student is made. This individualized plan is delegated to the appropriate team member to be implemented based on the scope of his/her practice. This plan may include parents, the referring teacher, and/or all of the student’s teachers to better serve him or her. The SRT collects teacher feedback, such as communication, improvement, or lack of improvement for students referred to this team. Another goal of the team is to serve our students, while also making our processes more efficient for students, teachers, and families. As of the time of writing this school action plan, forty-four referrals have been made during the 2023-2024 school year. RDMS plans to continue utilizing this team to ensure we further support our students.

## <u>Academic Support</u>

Ruth Doyle Middle School faculty and staff work very hard to ensure our students receive the best possible education and academic support. RDMS received an Overall ESSA Index Score of 75.05 during the 22-23 academic year (State Avg. 66.74) and a Public School Rating of a ‘B’ from The Arkansas Department of Education: Division of Elementary and Secondary Education (ADE DESE) (A=75.59 and above). ADE DESE describes a school’s value-added growth model used in Arkansas as answering the question: How much did a student grow this year compared to how much we thought he/she would grow based on what we know about his/her achievement in prior years (the student’s score history)? The school value-added growth score (VAS) is calculated from Arkansas standardized assessments. A score of 80 indicates students met academic growth expectations. A score above 80 indicates the students exceeded academic growth expectations. A score below 80 indicates the students did not meet academic growth expectations.

RDMS plans to analyze reporting data from ADE DESE released in the spring of the 2024-2025 academic year to assess the effectiveness of our school’s academic and intervention needs. Ruth Doyle Middle School will continue to use district and state-approved curriculum resources and strategies for traditional learning opportunities in the classroom. Teachers will assess and monitor student progress using screeners in math, language arts, and informal/formal classroom assessments in all other courses. Teachers will implement Tier I and Tier II intervention strategies in the classroom for learners. This may include one-on-one or small group instruction. Each teacher will be provided incoming student classroom achievement, interim data, ACTAspire data, and report cards in order to identify strong and weak skills/standards to focus on during the 2024-2025 academic year. Students’ 2024-2025 classroom achievement, interim data,

and report cards will be monitored in real-time utilizing Schoology and Edulastic reports to help guide teachers, counselors, and instructional paraprofessionals. Individual teachers, with our instructional facilitators, will monitor, utilize, and share data during scheduled collaborative meetings and will attempt to identify the specific reason(s) for student deficit (prerequisite skills missing, strategy issue, carelessness, apathy, attendance, etc.) Deficit areas may be addressed in any or all of the following:

- Involvement of Student Response Team to address possible controllable factors (attendance, lack of commitment, etc.)
- Whole group, small group or individual reinforcement
- Specific learning skills or strategies explicitly taught and practiced
- Additional practice and intervention on specific skills, identified by data, during a new designated intervention period that has been created in the school day.
## <u>Behavioral Support</u>

Ruth Doyle Middle School faculty will build upon, by August 2024, previous training in behavior basics and Building Relationships & Communicating High Expectations from *The New Art and Science of Teaching* and our school’s positive and negative behavior protocols and procedures. Administrators and counselors will develop school-wide goals based on data gathered from a needs assessment survey given to teachers during the 2023-2024 academic year, that identifies current strengths and deficits in the current perspectives of faculty. Administrators and teachers will evaluate our common language, expectations, and protocols for all Ruth Doyle Middle School classrooms to encourage consistency throughout the school. By August 2024, administrators, counselors, and teachers will collaborate and provide feedback with the expressed purpose to improve our school’s positive behavioral interventions and supports. RDMS will work to improve, better promote, and reiterate building-wide behavior expectations, positive behavior incentives/supports, and our developed three-tiered behavior RTI process.

Training Timeline:

- April/May 2024 - Needs assessment/feedback survey completed by teachers
- 2024-2025 August PD-Collaborative development of school-wide behavior goals.
- 2024-2025 August PD-Ruth Doyle Middle School improvement goals from stakeholder feedback on positive and negative behavior protocols
- April/May 2025 - Needs assessment/feedback survey completed by teachers
## <u>Social & Emotional Support</u>

*Character Strong* focuses on fostering the whole child with research-based, vertically aligned lessons that teach social-emotional skills, character building, and well-being. Ruth Doyle Middle School will utilize *Character Strong’s* 5th through 7th grade social & emotional learning curricula and professional learning services. *Character Strong* will be integrated with our 5th through 7th grade Health and Safety standards. School guidance counselors will also develop supplemental character development materials for students. All RDMS students take our health/PE course throughout the school year. Therefore, all students will recieve this support. Health/PE teachers will provided periodic updates to all RDMS faculty on topics covered. These updates will help reinforce and inform teachers on strategies given to students to handle a variety of situations that may occur during the school year. RDMS counselors, through referrals in our SRT process, will provide service and support to students that have been referred for social & emotional support. Our counselors have training and expertise that will provide students with supplemental support in these areas.

The development, improvement, and implementation of each support above will supplement each other for the success of the student body for the 2024-2025 school year. Discipline, academic, and counseling data improvement is and will continue to be quantified to allow for the tailoring of any one of the supports above to maximize their positive impact on our students.
