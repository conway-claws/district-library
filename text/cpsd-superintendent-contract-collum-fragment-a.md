3 .4 The district shall provide the Superintendent an automobile allowance in the sum of $1,000 per month, for all travel for District business purposes within the district, which travel allowance shall be in lieu of payment of mileage reimbursement, gasoline, or other vehicle upkeep or maintenance charges. This automobile allowance shall be paid in addition to the annual salary stated in Section 3.1 of this contract. Travel expenses for travel outside the district will be reimbursed on a per mile basis in accordance with district policy as it is for other administrative employees employed under twelve-month contracts; provided however, that the Superintendent is hereby authorized to use a District owned vehicle to travel outside the district for district purposes.

3.5 The district shall provide all benefits that are provided to other certified personnel from time to
time. The district shall also pay the Superintendent annually for the terms of this contract a stipend of $15,000.00 per year in addition to his salary as provided in paragraph 3 .1. The superintendent may use this stipend amount annually at his discretion for any of the following compensation or benefit options: [supplemental retirement plan contribution, annuity contribution, base salary increase, or a one-time retention incentive payment.] The superintendent will notify the finance office annually in writing the specified allocation of these benefits defined in this section 3.5.

3.6 The Superintendent agrees to continuously reside within the Conway School District as a term and
condition of his employment by the Board.

3.7 The district shall contribute to the ATRS Arkansas Teacher Retirement System on behalf of the
Superintendent as required by law. The district shall also pay to the Superintendent additional compensation in the amount ($15 00) per month in addition to his annual salary as set forth above.

3.8 The superintendent may take up to 23days of vacation in addition to the normal scheduled
school holidays provided to other school personnel. Superintendent agrees the days will be taken at such time that will least interfere with the duties set forth in this contract.

3 .9 Superintendent will be reimbursed on or before June 30thof each year for any unused vacation or sick days not used within the school year at his daily rate.
