### 2023-06-05 · Conway Public Schools (id 4114548)

The application window for In-district Waivers is now open.
If you reside in the Conway School District zone and wish for your child to attend another school, you may apply for a waiver. It is important to note that waivers will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis; and until classes are full. 
Please scan the QR code attached to this post or visit the Waiver section of the website for more info.

### 2023-06-13 · Conway Public Schools (id 4136219)

CPSD will experience an extended system outage this summer due to our yearly Student Information database updates and extra software updates at the state level. Between June 23rd and July 14th, transcripts, report cards, and other data will not be available. If you require a transcript or report card for your student, please submit your request to the building before June 20th. Our team will process the backlog of requests after the update is completed. We appreciate your patience during that time. Thank you for your understanding.
