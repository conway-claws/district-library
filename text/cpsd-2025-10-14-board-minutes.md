Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street October 14, 2025

<u>Members Present</u>: Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Interim Superintendent Jason Black; Interim Deputy Superintendent Dr. Karen Lasker; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Bryce Bennett; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Preston Echols, Kelli Gordon, Eric King, Shanda New; General Counsel Shasta Wagner; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Interim Superintendent Black led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Recognitions</u>

- Wampus Cat Pride o Hispanic Heritage Art Contest Winners – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media
Relations, invited Ms. Melinda Fraser, ESOL Facilitator, to recognized the overall winners in the Hispanic Heritage Art Contest: Lola O’Connor, Conway Junior High School; Trinity Cohee, Conway Junior High School; Amelia Thomas, Ellen Smith Elementary School; Isabel Choc, Conway High School; and Coniah Williams, Raymond and Phyllis Simon Middle School. o National Principals Appreciation Month-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized the District’s Principals for National Principals Appreciation Month and thanked them for their dedication to Conway Public Schools.

<u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:10 p.m. on a motion made by** **Dr. Petty and seconded by Mr. Cummings III.**

Items on the Consent Agenda were:

- Approval of Minutes – September 9, 2025, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in September 2025
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- C.A.T. Forum Update – Interim Superintendent Black spoke about the Communication Accountability Transparency Forum that was held on September 18, 2025 at the Conway High School cafeteria.

- Business Expo – Interim Superintendent Black thanked the Conway Area Chamber of Commerce for inviting Dr. Standridge and himself to speak at the Conway Business Expo last week.
- Parent Teacher Conferences – Interim Superintendent Black briefed the Board about Parent Teacher Conferences being held Wednesday of this week.
- Fall Break – Interim Superintendent Black thanked everyone for their hard work this first nine weeks and wishes everyone an enjoyable fall break.
- School Board Election Update – Interim Superintendent Black presented the March school election timeline.
## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- District Financial Report – Dr. Andy Ashley presented the financial report for September. **Dr. Petty made the motion to approve the financial report for September, as presented.** **The motion was seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr. and passed unanimously at 6:22 p.m.**
## <u>Action Items</u>

- Paraprofessional Salary Schedule Increase – Director Gynger Campbell presented the Paraprofessional Salary Schedule Increase for approval. **Mr. Cummings III made a motion to approve the Paraprofessional Salary Schedule** **Increase, as presented. The motion was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 6:25 p.m.**
- Approval of Superintendent Search Advisory Committee – Dr. Jeff Standridge presented the proposed Superintendent Search Advisory Committee candidates to the Board. The Board drew numbers to determine the order of selection. Board members selected their nominees and the categories. Board President Franklin selected Leo Cummings III as the appointed Board Representative to serve on the committee. **Mrs. Hargis made a motion to appoint the selected Committee Members for the** **Superintendent Search Advisory Committee. The motion was seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr.** **and passed unanimously at 6:42 p.m.**
