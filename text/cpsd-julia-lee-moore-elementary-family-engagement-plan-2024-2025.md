## <u>1: Jointly Developed</u>

Describe how the School works with parents and families to develop and review relevant plans, policies, and strategies related to engagement.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***1.1:** How does the School-in collaboration with parents-establish an engagement plan that* *reflects the specific academic improvement needs of the School, and that includes programs* *and practices that enhance engagement and address the specific engagement needs of* *students and their families?*
[*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(a)*]

- ***1.2:** What efforts have been made to ensure adequate representation of parents and families* *of participating children in the process (Title I families)?*
[*ESSA § 1116(c)(3)*]

In collaboration with families, the staff at Julia Lee Moore will establish an engagement plan that reflects the specific academic needs of the school and targets the specific engagement needs of students and families. To ensure adequate and diverse representation of families in the process, JLM will send out a request for input to all families electronically, as well as on paper. This request for input will invite families to share thoughts, concerns, and/or ideas. JLM will recruit alumni, along with parents and community members, to be part of an advisory committee to review all input from families to establish the engagement plan. (1.1; 1.2)

## <u>2: Communication</u>

Describe how the School will communicate with and distribute information to parents and families.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***2.1:** How does the School distribute an informational packet appropriate for the age and grade* *of each child annually, ensuring to include:*
- *description of the engagement program*
- *recommended roles for parents, students, teacher, and the School*
- *ways for a family to get involved*
- *survey regarding volunteer interests*
- *schedule of activities planned throughout the school year*
- *regular, two-way, and meaningful system for parents/teachers to communicate* [*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(b)(3)(B)(i)*]
- ***2.2:** How will the School ensure information related to school and parent programs, meetings,* *and other activities is provided to parents in a format and in a language that parents can* *understand (to the extent practicable)?*
- *How is relevant information provided in a variety of ways? (For example, paper copies* *made available, as well as social media posts, website links, parent apps, etc.?* [*ESSA § 1116(e)(5)*]
- ***2.3:** How does the School offer flexible opportunities for meetings with families?*
[*ESSA § 1116(c)(2)*]

JLM will communicate with and distribute information to parents and families in the following ways:

- JLM will distribute an <u>informational brochure</u> to all families of each child at the Annual Report to the Public on August 15, 2024. The informational brochure will include the following information: An overview of JLM, the mission/vision/core values of JLM, the contact information for the principal and assistant principal, JLM enrollment, free & reduced statistics for JLM, how Title I funds assist JLM, standardized state test results, ethnicity breakdowns of JLM, and a QR code to scan for additional information. Additionally, at this time JLM will provide parents with a parent-friendly description of the <u>parental & family engagement plan</u> which includes a recommended roles for parents, students, and the school, ways for for families to get involved, a survey regarding volunteer interests, a schedule of parent workshops and PTO meetings, and how to maintain communication with their child’s teacher throughout the year. (2.1)
- JLM will use paper, digital, and face-to-face methods to communicate with parents regarding information related to school. To share meaningful information, the school will utilize the district website, the district app, email, text messages, push notifications, social media, and printed notes. Families can access all forms of digital communication in their native language. The school will utilize the district’s translator to translate printed communication. Additionally, teachers will also communicate regularly with parents/families and invite parents to communicate with them as well

through a variety of methods including, but not limited to, email, phone, the app, communication folders, interim progress reports, report cards, and printed notes. (2.2)

- Parent/Teacher conferences will be held twice per year at flexible times during the morning, afternoon, and evening. Translators will be scheduled to attend conferences for parents whose native language is one other than English. If parents are not able to attend conferences in person, teachers and/or staff members will arrange conferences to be held virtually or via phone. Additionally, PTO meetings will be scheduled at various times during the year to accommodate more parental/familial involvement. Some will be held during the day while others will be held in the evening. (2.3)

- JLM will involve parents of students at all grade levels in a variety of roles through the following (1) Parent workshops provided including demonstration and role playing by trained to improve students’ literacy and math skills in October 2024 and March 2025;
(2) Volunteer opportunities for parents/community members; (3) PTO meetings; (4) Attendance and/or participation in activities and after-school clubs such as Walk-run club, school musicals, field trips, and guest reading. (4.4)
- JLM promotes and supports responsible parenting through offering the following: (1) Resources, such as books or brochures, for parents to have, borrow, or check-out free of charge; (2) Parent Workshops including demonstration and role playing by trained to improve students’ literacy and math skills (4.5)
- JLM provides instruction to parents on how to incorporate developmentally appropriate learning activities in the home environment through offering the following: (1) Parent Workshops including demonstration and role playing by trained to improve students’ literacy and math skills; (2) An informational brochure including how parents/families can access the Division of Elementary and Secondary Education website; (3) Information via social media on nutritional meal planning (4.6)

## <u>5: Coordination</u>

Describe how the School will coordinate with other organizations, businesses, and community partners, including alumni, to provide additional supports, services, and resources to families.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***5.1:** How does the School investigate and utilize community resources in the instructional* *program?*
[*ADE Rules Governing Parental Involvement Section 5.06*]

- ***5.2:** How does the School coordinate and integrate programs and activities with other Federal,* *State, and local programs? Some examples include:*
- *public preschool programs such as Head Start*
- *organizations/activities to help students transition to elementary, middle, high, and* *postsecondary schools or careers*
- *wraparound services that allow families to send their children to school ready and able* *to focus on learning* [*ESSA §1116(e)(4)*]
- ***5.3:** In what ways does the School enable the formation of a Parent Teacher Association or* *organization and ensure leaders of said organization will be included in appropriate decisions?*
[*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(b)(8)(B)(ii)*]

Julia Lee Moore will coordinate with other organizations, businesses, and community partners, including alumni, to provide additional supports, services, and resources to families in various ways.

- The counselor and PTO will work to form partnerships with local organizations, businesses, and community members such as Salem United Methodist Church, First Presbyterian, Centennial Bank, RoadHog Barbeque, The Rotary Club, Conway Fire Department, and Conway Police Department. In collaboration with these partners, JLM will enhance the instructional program by hosting events such as Fall Festival, JLM Walkathon, Annual Report to the Public, Field/Enrichment Day, guest readers, and parent/family workshops. Additionally, JLM will provide resources such as food backpacks, library assistants, and dictionaries to students. The counselor will also utilize Charity Tracker, a community tracking system, to make sure the resources are being provided in an equitable manner. (5.1)
- In order to promote a smoother transition to kindergarten, JLM will hold preschool transition conferences for families of incoming kindergarten students who have special education needs. Additionally, the administrators from JLM and Simon Middle School will coordinate a visit for outgoing fourth grade students to visit Simon Middle School. During this visit, the administrators and counselors at Simon will provide students with an overview of fifth grade and a tour of the building. The district will partner with mental health and ABA providers, so that organization can provide ABA and/or mental health services to students. (5.2)

- The school sends out an invitation to all families at Julia Lee Moore to join the PTO at no charge via social media, the app, email, and a printed note. Additionally, the staff at JLM and the PTO work with community organizations to collaboratively host events and provide resources. (5.3)
