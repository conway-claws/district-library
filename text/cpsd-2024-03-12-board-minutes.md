Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street March 12, 2024

<u>Members Present</u>: Mr. André Acklin, Mr. Jason Sandefer, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Trey Geier

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Ben Darley, Kelli Gordon, Keisha Jefferson, Eric King, Jason Lawrence, Tammy Woosley; Chief Financial Officer Robyn Keene; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Acklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Conway Junior High Quiz Bowl State Champions-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Principal Preston Echols to introduce the Conway Junior High School Quiz Bowl State Champion team.
- Conway High School Unified Basketball State Champions-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Principal Kate Worley to introduce the Conway High School Unified Basketball State Champion team.
- Board Member Trey Geier Recognition – Superintendent Collum presented Mr. Geier with a plaque and thanked him for his service to the district.
## <u>Champion Student Recognition</u>

- Bruce Bolling-Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video featuring Bruce Bolling, Champion Student of the Month from Ellen Smith Elementary School. She welcomed Principal Shannon Riner, who introduced Bruce. Superintendent Collum presented him with a certificate. Bruce led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:17 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mr. Leach and seconded by Mr. Sandefer.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – February 13, 2024, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of Minutes – March 1, 2024, Special Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in February 2024
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of School Choice Applications

- Approval of Policy 1.24 Revision
- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline.
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Read Across America Week District Participation – Superintendent Collum highlighted district participation for Read Across America Week.
- Community Circles Update – Superintendent Collum gave an update on the Community Circles. The next one will be April 16, 2024 at Ruth Doyle Middle School.
- COSSBA Conference Update – Superintendent Collum highlighted the COSSBA conference in Dallas, TX in February.
## <u>Chief Financial Officer’s Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Robyn Keene presented the financial report for February. **Mr. Geier made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion was** **seconded by Mr. Leach and passed unanimously at 6:25 p.m.**
## <u>Information Items</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Ashley and Technology Coordinator Joel Dean presented the academic update on the State of Technology for Instructional Support.
- Proposed Policy Revisions – Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner presented the proposed changes for the Board to consider and vote upon at the April 2024 Board meeting.
## <u>Action Item</u>

- District Salary Schedules – Chief Financial Officer Robyn Keene presented revised Salary Schedules to the Board for approval. **Mr. Sandefer made the motion to approve Salary Schedules, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr. and passed unanimously at 6:36 p.m.** <u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Dr. David Naylor, Jr., the Board** **unanimously approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at** **6:36 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**
- Resignations
- Elections
## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 6:41 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session to consider a personnel matter. The Board exited Executive Session at 7:45 p.m.

## <u>Adjournment</u>

|Respectfully submitted,|No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at 7:45 p.m. on a motion made by Mrs. Franklin and seconded by Mrs. Hargis.|
