Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street January 13, 2026

<u>Members Present</u>: Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jason Black; Interim Deputy Superintendent Dr. Karen Lasker; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Bryce Bennett; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Preston Echols, Kelli Gordon, Shawn Higginbotham, Eric King, Shanda New; General Counsel Shasta Wagner; Strategic Planning Consultant Dr. Jeff Standridge; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Champion Student Recognition</u>

Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, introduced Siboney Moore, Champion Student of the Month from Conway Junior High School, and presented a video featuring Siboney. Superintendent Black presented Siboney with a certificate. Siboney led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- ASBA Master Board Member Award – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized Board Member Dr. Naylor, Jr. for receiving the Master Board Member award from Arkansas School Boards Association for earning at least fifty hours of Board development training.
- Board Appreciation – January is School Board Appreciation Month. Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented the Board with a presentation of students from all schools thanking the Board for their service to the District. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **Mr. Cummings III made the motion to approve the consent agenda. The motion was** **seconded by Ms. Walton and approved at 6:24 p.m. on 6-1 vote. Voting for the motion were** **Mrs. Franklin, Mr. Cummings III, Ms. Walton, Mr. Leach, Dr. Naylor, Jr., and Mrs. Hargis.** **Voting against the motion was Dr. Petty.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – December 9, 2025, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of Minutes – December 11, 2025, Special Board Meeting
- Approval of Minutes – December 17, 2025, Special Board Meeting
- Approval of Minutes – December 21, 2025, Special Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in December 2025

- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Enterprise Lease
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Superintendent Comments – Superintendent Black thanked the Board, staff, parents, and the community for their support and trust in him as Superintendent. He stated the focus will continue to be on students first. Priorities will be building strong collaborative teams; listening to students, families, staff, and teachers; and keeping Conway moving forward with clarity and purpose.
- Board Member Training Hours Report – Superintendent Black reported to the public the number of professional development training hours Board members received in calendar year 2025.
## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- Board Financial Report –Shawn Higginbotham, Director of Finance, presented the financial report for December. **Ms. Walton made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Dr. Petty and passed unanimously at 6:31 p.m.**
## <u>Action Item</u>

- Approval of Resolution Adopting the District’s Facilities Master Plan Dated February 1, 2026 – Assistant Superintendent Ashley presented the District’s Facilities Master Plan to the Board and asked for approval of the Resolution adopting the District’s Facilities Master Plan dated February 1, 2026 **Mr. Cummings III made a motion to approve the 2026 Master Plan Tab 02 Board** **Resolution adopting the District’s Facilities Master Plan dated February 1, 2026, as** **presented. The motion was seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr and passed unanimously at 6:40 p.m.**
## <u>Information Item</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Bennett invited Courtney Knight, Curriculum Coordinator, to present a District Math Plan update.
## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 6:49 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session for the purpose of employment and litigation updates. The Board exited Executive Session at 8:28 p.m.

<u>Approval of Superintendent’s Contract</u> **Mr. Cummings III made a motion to approve the Superintendent’s contract. The motion was** **seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr. and passed at 8:29 p.m. on a 6-1 vote. Voting for the motion were** **Mrs. Franklin, Mr. Cummings III, Ms. Walton, Mr. Leach, Dr. Naylor, Jr., and Mrs. Hargis.** **Voting against the motion was Dr. Petty.**

<u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Ms. Walton the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 8:29 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**

- Resignations
- Elections
## <u>New Business</u>

- Mrs. Hargis asked that a policy be created that outlines the district’s procedures for complaints filed against staff. Mrs. Wagner will review the current procedures and the process of creating or revising the policy at the February meeting. <u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at** **8:41 p.m. on a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Mr. Cummings III.** Respectfully submitted,
