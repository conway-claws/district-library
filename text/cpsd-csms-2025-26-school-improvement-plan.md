# Carl Stuart Middle School School Improvement Plan

# 2025-2026

## <u>Area of Concern</u>

Carl Stuart Middle School implemented a flexible and responsive third period in order to respond to student assessment data in the 2025 school year. According to aggregated ATLAS interim data from the 2025 school year, 45% of Carl Stuart Middle School students demonstrate proficiency in English Language Arts (ELA). The remaining 55% of students are performing below the proficiency threshold, with 21% at Level 1 and 34% at Level 2.

Students who are identified as level one students demonstrate a “limited understanding of knowledge and skills,” while students who are identified as level two “demonstrate a basic understanding of knowledge and skills required of the grade-level standards.”

## <u>Goal</u>

To increase the number of students meeting proficiency in ELA from 45% to 50%.

## <u>Strategies</u>

Data Review and Analysis

- Regularly scheduled team data meetings will be conducted in order to review common formative assessments (CFAs), identify trends, and pinpoint student needs. Instructional facilitators, administrators, and teachers will examine which standards or question types students struggled with in order to inform instruction.
Tiered Intervention

- Identify students performing below proficiency and invite students to small-group intervention sessions based on specific standards.
Student Engagement

- Continue to implement Robert Marzano High-Yield Instructional Strategies
- Coach students on identifying areas of growth through learning ladder discussions.
## <u>Professional Development</u>

Carl Stuart’s professional development plan will consist of:

- Utilization of Learning Ladders
- Targeted Tier II Intervention Instructional Strategies
- Response to Intervention
- Training on RtI Scheduler
## <u>Implementation Plan</u>

*Timeline*

- Beginning-of-Year: Building-Level Training on Utilization of Learning Ladders, Tier II intervention and Instructional Strategies, Response to Intervention, and RtI Scheduler.
- Beginning-of-Year: Conduct initial data review meetings to analyze ATLAS data and discuss student grade-level transitions.
- Middle-of-Year: Re-evaluate student performance data based on ATLAS Interim.

# 2025-2026

- Weekly: Invite students to WIN Time who are not meeting student proficiency in accordance with Common Formative Assessment data or learning ladders.
*Key Tasks*

- Conduct initial data review of ATLAS Summative data to identify students performing below proficiency.
- Schedule monthly team data meetings.
- Inviting students to Tier II instructional sessions with teachers
## <u>Plan Evaluation</u>

Determination of progress will be based on the following:

- Classroom walkthroughs
- Teacher feedback from Administrators and Instructional Facilitators
- Common Formative Assessment and ATLAS Interim Data
- PLC Meeting Minutes

# 2025-2026

Carl Stuart Middle School implemented a behavior matrix for the 2024-2025 school year. Although we have normalized expectations for student behavior, we feel that it is appropriate to increase the depth of behavior modification.

## <u>Goal</u>

Decrease student disciplinary incidents by 10% or 115 incidents.

## <u>Strategies</u>

Positive Behavior Interventions and Supports (PBIS)

- Ticket program for students who are meeting expectations in order to eat outside.
- Incentives that recognize students who positively contribute to our school culture (e.g. student of the month).
Building-Wide Behavior and Procedural Expectations

- Implement common language and procedures for the building in order to ensure smooth transitions throughout the day.
- Implement common expectations for behavior (e.g. treating others with respect). Utilize WIN time to educate students about respect, compassion, etc.
- Proactively discuss and teach expectations for all areas of our school.
Build Strong Student-Adult Relationships

- Work to facilitate discussions among teachers regarding which students need additional support and then pair them with a mentor.
Restorative Discipline Practices

- Addressing conflict through student mediation
- Student reflection document to ensure student understands how they did not meet expectations.
## <u>Professional Development</u>

Carl Stuart’s professional development plan will consist of:

- Defining restorative discipline and identifying its key components
- Collaborative development of a tiered behavior plan.
## <u>Implementation Plan</u>

- *Timeline*
- Monthly: RtI Behavioral Meeting with Teachers
- Monthly: Goal for reducing incidents
- Monthly: Utilize WIN Time for teaching common expectations
- Weekly: Ticket Incentive with Focus on Specific Behavior or Expectation
- Quarterly: Review discipline data with building-leadership team
- Beginning-of-Year: Review Behavior and Procedures Matrix
- *Key Tasks*
- Re-Evaluate Behavior and Expectations Matrix

# 2025-2026

- Develop WIN Time lessons on behavior expectations and school values
- Draft needed documents for restorative discipline for students.
## <u>Plan Evaluation</u>

Determination of progress will be based on the following:

- Quarterly discipline incidents will decrease
- The number of students resolving conflict on their own will increase
- Students participating in Carl Stuart’s PBIS system will increase
