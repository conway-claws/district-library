Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street November 11, 2025

<u>Members Present</u>: Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Ms. Leona Walton, Mr. Leo Cummings III, Mr. Trip Leach, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Dr. Barrett Petty

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Interim Superintendent Jason Black; Interim Deputy Superintendent Dr. Karen Lasker; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Bryce Bennett; Directors: Gynger Campbell, Preston Echols, Kelli Gordon, Greg Hughes, Eric King, Shanda New; General Counsel Shasta Wagner; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Franklin called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

## <u>Champion Student Recognition</u>

Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, introduced Ella Davenport, Champion Student of the Month from Ellen Smith Elementary School, and presented a video featuring Ella.

Champion Student Ella Davenport and Brigadier General Chad Bridges, the Adjutant General of Arkansas, led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Conway High School JROTC – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Brigadier General Chad Bridges to introduce the JROTC Cadet leadership and speak about the program.
- National Merit Semi-Finalists-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Conway High School Principal Worley to recognize the National Merit Semi-Finalists: Caroline Chrysler and Will Schlientz.
- Conway High School Volleyball State Champions – Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Athletic Director Greg Hughes to recognize the Conway High School Volleyball team and Coach Crow for winning the state championship for the second consecutive year.
## <u>Patrons Comments</u>

- Jimmie Cavin – Mr. Cavin spoke to the Board about hypocrisy and the recent employee termination. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 5:58 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mr. Cummings III and seconded by Ms. Walton.**
## Items on the Consent Agenda were:

- Approval of Minutes – October 14, 2025, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of Minutes – November 6, 2025, Special Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in October 2025
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of Act 1599 Resolution
- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline. [Dr. Naylor, Jr. arrived for meeting at 5:59 p.m.]
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Annual Report to the Public – Interim Superintendent Black presented the 2025 Annual Report to the Board and to the public.
## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- Proposed Budget of Expenditures 2027-2028 and 2026 School Election Sample Ballot – Scott Beardsley, First Security Beardsley, presented the Proposed Budget of Expenditures 2027- 2028 and sample ballot for approval. [Mr. Leach left meeting at 6:21 p.m. and returned at 6:23 p.m.] **Mr. Leach made the motion to adopt the attached Proposed Budget of Expenditures with** **Tax Levy for Fiscal Year Beginning July 1, 2027 to and including June 30, 2028 and to** **approve the related Sample Ballot. The motion was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 6:26 p.m.**
- Board Financial Report – Dr. Andy Ashley invited Shawn Higginbotham, Director of Finance, to present the financial report for October. **Mrs. Hargis made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr. and passed unanimously at 6:31 p.m.**
- Staff Bonus Proposal – Shawn Higginbotham, Director of Finance, proposed that the Board provide a one-time bonus for all employees based on the following years of experience in education: $3,000 for 30+ years, $2,500 for 20-29 years, $1,500 for 10-19 years, and $1,000 for 0-9 years. **Dr. Naylor, Jr. made the motion to approve the staff bonus proposal, as presented for all** **current CPSD employees pending PPC review. The motion was seconded by Mr.** **Cummings III. and passed unanimously at 6:37 p.m.**
## <u>Action Item</u>

- Proposed Policy 4.47 Revision – Shasta Wagner, Chief Legal Officer, presented the proposed changes to policy 4.47 Possession and Use of Personal Electronic Devices.

**Dr. Petty made a motion to approve policy 4.47 revision with a waiver of the 30 day read,** **as presented. The motion was seconded by Mr. Leach and passed unanimously at 6:38 p.m.**

## <u>Information Items</u>

- Strategic Planning Update – Dr. Jeff Standridge presented a Strategic Planning update.
- Superintendent Search Update – Dr. Jeff Standridge presented an update in the Superintendent search process. Dr. Standridge and the Board discussed which candidates will be interviewed. **Mrs. Hargis made the motion to interview the top three candidates and the number six** **candidate from the search advisory committee selection process; Jason Black, Tamekia** **Brown, Bruce Gearing, and Jennifer Barbaree. The motion was seconded by Dr. Naylor Jr.** **and approved at 6:59 p.m. on a 4-3 vote. Voting for the motion were Mr. Cummings III,** **Mr. Leach, Dr. Naylor Jr. and Mrs. Hargis. Voting against the motion were Mrs. Franklin,** **Ms. Walton, and Dr. Petty.**
- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Dr. Bryce Bennett presented a review of the campus ratings and grades report. Dr. Bennett also reviewed a math plan update.
## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 7:20 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session for the purpose of considering employment and resignation. The Board exited Executive Session at 7:54 p.m.

<u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mr. Cummings III and seconded by Mrs. Hargis the Board** **unanimously approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at** **7:55 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**

- Resignations
- Elections The Board called a recess at 7:55 p.m. to reset the Board Room for the Employee Hearing and reconvened at 8:22 p.m.
## <u>Employee Hearing</u>

An employee hearing was conducted for a recommendation by Interim Superintendent Black to terminate Barbara Tucker’s employment contract.

The Board called a recess at 9:43 p.m. and reconvened at 9:53 p.m.

Following continued testimony, the Board entered Executive Session at 11:03 p.m. for the consideration of termination and exited Executive Session at 11:46 p.m.
