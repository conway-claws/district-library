1: Jointly Developed

A parent and family engagement committee has been formed to include school administrators, school
staff, and parents. This committee is responsible for collaboratively developing an Engagement Plan
that outlines how parents, school staff, and students share the responsibility for improving school
academic development.

1.1 Establishing the Engagement Plan
@ The committee and current PTO will meet regularly to review the school’s academic
improvement needs and develop a plan that includes programs and practices to enhance
family involvement to ensure all family groups are represented and engaged.

1.2 Ensuring Adequate Representation
e Aschool survey will be created by the administrative team for the 2024-2025 school year to
provide essential data from our families. This survey will help us understand parents’
perspectives on engagement and identify areas for improvement.
