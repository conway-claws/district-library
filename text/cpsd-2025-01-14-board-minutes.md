Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street January 14, 2025

<u>Members Present</u>: Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mr. Trip Leach, Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Andre’ Acklin, Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Carl Barger, Mr. Tom Kennedy

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Bryce Bennett, Gynger Campbell, Ben Darley, Kelli Gordon, Paula Harris, Eric King; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; District Financial Business Manager Stacy Rimmer; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Andria Pulver

Board President Naylor called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video featuring Bryston Thomas, Ascending Student of the Month from Marguerite Vann Elementary School. She welcomed Principal Shanda New, who introduced Bryston. Superintendent Collum presented Bryston with a certificate. Bryston led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- Laura Crow – Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized Conway High School Volleyball Coach Laura Crow. Coach Crow was named the 2024 All Arkansas Prep Volleyball Coach of the Year.
- Board Appreciation – January is School Board Appreciation Month. Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented the Board with a presentation from students and staff from all the schools thanking the Board for their service to the District. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:14 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Mr. Barger.** Items on the Consent Agenda were:
- Approval of Minutes – December 10, 2024, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in December 2024
- Approval of Petitions for Transfer of Students
- Approval of Act 1599 Resolution – Lonnie Flannery
- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline.
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Board Member Training Hours Report – Superintendent Collum reported to the public the number of professional development training hours the Board members received in calendar year 2024.
- Conway Public Schools Public Relations Media Campaign Update – Superintendent Collum and Kaitylyn Ryals presented an update on the Public Relations Media Campaign.
- Academic Branding Update, Video, Logo – Superintendent Collum and Kaitlyn Ryals presented an academic branding update. They presented a new video and logo for the district.
- Podcasts Series Update – Superintendent Collum and Kaitlyn Ryals presented an update on the new podcasts series.
## <u>District Financial Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Dr. Andy Ashley presented the financial report for December. **Mr. Leach made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion was** **seconded by Mrs. Franklin and passed unanimously at 6:35 p.m.**
## <u>Information Item</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Director Bryce Bennett presented the academic report on student assessment data planning and development. Board Member Acklin requested an executive session prior to approving the personnel report.
## Mr. Kennedy made the motion to table the Personnel Approval until after Executive Session.

## The motion was seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 6:58 p.m.

## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 7:00 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session to consider a personnel matter. The Board exited Executive Session at 8:40 p.m.

<u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mr. Kennedy and seconded by Mr. Barger, the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 8:40 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**

- Resignations
- Elections <u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at** **8:41 p.m. on a motion made by Mr. Kennedy and seconded by Mr. Leach.** Respectfully submitted,

|/s/Dr. David Naylor, Jr. Board President|/s/Linda Hargis Board Secretary|
