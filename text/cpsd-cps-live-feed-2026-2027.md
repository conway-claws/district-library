Welcome New Conway School Board Members!

The Conway Public Schools Board of Education held a special meeting tonight Friday, August 7th, 2026 for Personnel Approval and Board Member Candidate Interviews for the open At-Large Board position. During the meeting, a motion was made to approve all resignations and elections; however, the decision regarding the Part-Time Finance position will be postponed until the Regular School Board Meeting on Tuesday, August 11, 2026.

The Board Member Candidate Interviews started with Zone 4 Board position with the only candidate being Ms. Mary Lajeunesse. The School Board made a motion to appoint Ms. Mary Lajeunesse for the Zone 4 School Board Member position. The School Board made a motion to appoint Dr. Matthew White, out of 7 candidates, for the Zone at Large School Board Member position.
