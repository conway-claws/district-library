Minutes of Board of Education Regular Meeting Conway Public Schools Administration Building 2220 Prince Street May 14, 2024

<u>Members Present</u>: Mr. André Acklin, Dr. David Naylor, Jr., Mrs. Linda Hargis, Mr. Trip Leach, Mr. Jason Sandefer, Mrs. Sheila Franklin, Mr. Bill Milburn

## <u>Members Absent</u>: None

<u>Others Present</u>: Superintendent Jeff Collum; Deputy Superintendent Jason Black; Assistant Superintendents: Dr. Andy Ashley, Dr. Megan Duncan, Dr. Karen Lasker; Directors: Ben Darley, Paula Harris, Sarah Jerry, Eric King, Jason Lawrence, Tammy Woosley; Chief Financial Officer Robyn Keene; Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner; Coordinator of Media Relations Kaitlyn Ryals; Recording Secretary Donna Story

Board President Acklin called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

## <u>Wampus Cat Pride</u>

- CEAL Mentor Program Presentation (Conway Educational Aspiring Leader) – Assistant Superintendent Duncan recognized employees that participated in the CEAL Mentor Program. Superintendent Collum presented them with a certificate.
- Special Employee Recognition – Superintendent Collum welcomed Mr. Mike Mertens and Dr. Mike Hernandez with AAEA to present Director Jason Lawrence the Arkansas School Plant Managers Association Plant Manager of the Year Award.
- Conway High School Coding Team-Kaitlyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, invited Principal Kate Worley to introduce the Coding Team and recognize them for winning the state championship.
## <u>Champion Student Recognition</u>

- Teagan Grider-Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, presented a video featuring Teagan, Champion Student of the Month from Sallie Cone Preschool Center. She welcomed Principal Heather Leavell, who introduced Teagan. Superintendent Collum presented her with a certificate.
- Aiden Eslinger-Kaitylyn Ryals, Coordinator of Media Relations, recognized Aiden, Champion Student of the Month from Conway High School. She invited Aiden to speak to the Board. She also welcomed Principal Kate Worley to speak about Aiden. Superintendent Collum presented him with a certificate. Teagan and Aiden led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance. <u>Approval of Consent Agenda</u> **The consent agenda was unanimously approved at 6:25 p.m. on a motion made by** **Mr. Sandefer and seconded by Mrs. Hargis.**

Items on the Consent Agenda were:

- Approval of Minutes – April 9, 2024, Regular Board Meeting
- Approval of Minutes – April 25, 2024, Special Board Meeting
- Approval of the List of Bills Paid in April 2024
- Approval of Purchase Orders
- Approval of Petition for Transfer of Students
- Approval of School Choice Applications
- Approval of School Action Plans
- Approval of E-Rate Resolution
- The Board agreed to uphold the recommendation of the Administrative Review Committee and Administration concerning students’ discipline.
## <u>Superintendent’s Report</u>

- Graduation Update – Superintendent Collum invited Principal Kate Worley to give an overview of the graduation plans for the Class of 2024 and presented a video produced by Aiden Eslinger.
- Presentation of 2023 Conway High School yearbook – Principal Kate Worley presented the yearbook to the Board.
- Teacher Appreciation – Superintendent Collum thanked the district’s teachers for all they do for the students and district. The Board members also thanked the teachers.
## <u>Chief Financial Officer’s Report</u>

- Board Financial Report – Robyn Keene presented the financial report for April. **Mrs. Franklin made the motion to approve the financial report, as presented. The motion** **was seconded by Mr. Leach and passed unanimously at 6:49 p.m.**
- Staff Compensation Proposal – Robyn Keene proposed two options to the Board to consider a non-recurring bonus for all current employees: Option A, $1,500 or Option B, $1,000. **Mr. Milburn made the motion to transfer surplus funds from the building fund to provide** **all current employees a one-time, non-recurring staff bonus of $1,500. The motion was** **seconded by Mrs. Hargis and passed unanimously at 6:55 p.m.**
## <u>Information Items</u>

- Student Academic Achievement Report (Act 425) – Assistant Superintendent Duncan recognized Ms. Moton and the men volunteering in the mentor program and presented a video featuring the mentors.
- Early Childhood Program Update – Assistant Superintendent Duncan gave an update on the early childhood program, Fueling Brains. The district will be receiving a grant from the Department of Education and a partnership grant with Arch Ford Education Service Cooperative. We will be the leading cohort for the state of Arkansas.
- Proposed Policy Revisions – Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner presented the proposed changes for the Board to consider and vote upon at the June 2024 Board meeting.

- Ark. Code Ann. § 6-13-619 – Chief Legal Officer Shasta Wagner updated the Board on the changes to Ark. Code Ann. § 6-13-619 (e) about executive sessions that went into effect May 1, 2024. <u>Personnel Approval</u> **Upon a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Mrs. Franklin, the Board unanimously** **approved the following personnel recommendations of the Superintendent at 7:15 p.m., as presented in the agenda:**
- Resignations
- Elections
## <u>Executive Session</u>

At 7:18 p.m., the Board went into Executive Session to consider a personnel matter. The Board exited Executive Session at 8:36 p.m.

<u>Adjournment</u> **No further business to come before the Board, the meeting was unanimously adjourned at** **8:36 p.m. on a motion made by Mrs. Hargis and seconded by Dr. Naylor, Jr.**
