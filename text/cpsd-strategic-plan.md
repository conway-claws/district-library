# Executive Summary

Conway Public School District (CPSD) stands at a defining moment. Gleaned from a year-long listening tour, across thousands of voices—students, parents, educators, business leaders, and community members—one message is unmistakable: *CPSD is not simply a school district; it is* *a cornerstone of Conway’s identity, economic vitality, and future.* As such, this community believes deeply in its schools but expects them to perform and to lead.

At the heart of that belief are the people of CPSD. Teachers, staff, and school leaders are consistently recognized as the district’s greatest strength. Classrooms across the district reflect a culture of care, commitment, and connection, where students are known, supported, and encouraged to succeed. Families express confidence in the relationships their children have with their teachers, and the community demonstrates a strong willingness to invest in the continued success of its schools. There is also widespread acknowledgement that a monumental amount of work has taken place in the past few months, beginning with the launch of the ***Strategic Readiness Plan*** in early Summer 2025, resulting in focused and sustained effort across the district to execute against major priorities.

## Strengths

- Exceptional educators and strong school-level culture
- Deep community pride and support
- Students generally feel safe and supported
At the same time, the data reveals an equally important truth. While CPSD is a district with strong foundations, it is also experiencing significant and growing system-level challenges that must be addressed with urgency and discipline if CPSD is to meet the needs and expectations of its community.

Across stakeholder groups, there is a perception of excessive variation in student outcomes from school to school, suggesting that while many campuses are performing at a high level, that level of excellence is not yet system-wide. Families and staff alike point to variability in academic rigor, access to support, and overall student experience—highlighting the need for stronger alignment, clearer expectations, and greater coherence across the District.

In addition, stakeholders express concern about trust and communication at the District level, particularly around the clarity and consistency of decision-making. While trust remains strong within individual schools, there is a noticeable gap at the system level that impacts confidence and engagement.

At the classroom level, student behavior and disruptions to learning are frequently cited as barriers to instructional effectiveness, reinforcing the need for consistent expectations and support structures across all campuses. Finally, facility constraints and aging infrastructure, combined with the pressures of a growing community, are creating operational challenges that impact both the learning environment and the District’s ability to scale effectively.

## Challenges

- Inconsistent academic outcomes across schools
- Trust and communication gaps at the Board/District level
- Student behavior disrupting instruction
- Aging, and sometimes inadequate, facilities and systems
As part of this planning process, CPSD also specifically engaged its District Leadership Team (DLT)—a group of experienced administrators and instructional leaders representing schools and central office functions—to provide direct input on the most critical areas the District must improve over the next three to five years.

While many districts struggle with culture, the DLT affirmed that CPSD’s culture remains a significant strength. Teachers, leaders, and staff are deeply committed to students and take pride in their work. However, leaders also identified some specific structural and operational challenges that, if addressed, would significantly accelerate student outcomes, as well as organizational effectiveness.

- ***Instructional Coherence:*** Leaders called for strong Tier 1 instruction, aligned curriculum (especially in reading and math), and consistent district-wide academic expectations in order to reduce variability from campus to campus.
- ***Staff Support & Retention:*** Teachers and staff are experiencing increasing workload strain. There is a need for additional support (paras, interventionists, etc.), improved new-hire onboarding, and competitive compensation structures.
- ***Systems & Organizational Clarity:*** Processes are often inconsistent or unclear. Leaders emphasized the need for streamlined systems, defined roles, and stronger alignment between Central Office and campuses.
All of these insights, from both internal and external constituents and patrons, reveal a critical distinction. CPSD’s strengths are mostly <u>cultural</u>. Its challenges, on the other hand, are mostly <u>structural</u> or <u>operational</u>. This is a positive reality as weak or toxic cultures are much more difficult to transform than structural and operational challenges.

As stated, the District does not suffer from a lack of commitment, talent, or belief. Instead, it is experiencing the ***natural pressures of a system that is increasing in complexity, where*** ***structures, alignment, and communication have not fully kept pace with the strength of its people.***

This creates multiple gaps that we must address through this Strategic Plan:

- High aspirations for excellence, but inconsistent student outcomes
- Strong individual schools, but inconsistent system-wide employee & student experience
- High trust within buildings, but lower trust at the District level
- Dedicated educators, but increasing strain on time, resources, and support

Taken together, these gaps <u>do not</u> reflect a lack of capability—but rather a system that must evolve to match the strength of its people and the expectations of its community.

## Building the Foundation

The consistent feedback from our community stakeholders, as well as from district-wide leadership indicates that the work ahead is clear. We must build on the many strengths of CPSD to create an aligned, consistent, and high-performing ***system***. In other words, ***we*** ***cannot simply chase outcomes, we must build a system that produces those outcomes*** ***consistently.*** This is the foundation upon which we will generate sustained success over the long-term.

This strategic plan is designed to do exactly that. It aligns the district around a small number of clear, high-impact priorities—focused on improving academic outcomes, strengthening learning environments, investing in people, creating operational excellence, and restoring trust through engagement and accountability.

It is important to note that this plan does not start from scratch. It builds on the District’s existing priorities, leverages the focused effort of the past few months, honors the voice of the community, and expands what CPSD already does well. At the same time, it introduces the clarity, discipline, and focus required to move from a collection of strong schools to a cohesive, high-performing system.

## The Opportunity Ahead

CPSD is not in a position of weakness—it is in a position of <u>opportunity</u>.

Few districts have:

- The level of community support CPSD enjoys
- The quality and commitment of its teachers and staff
- The willingness of stakeholders to engage and improve
With the right alignment of strategy, leadership, and resources, Conway Public Schools has the potential to become ***a premier district of choice—recognized not only for its people, but for*** ***the success of every student, and the consistent performance of the system.***

This plan reflects a simple but powerful conviction:

***We do not need to fix our people. We need to lead effectively, while building a system*** ***worthy of our people. And in doing so, ensure that every student—regardless of school,*** ***classroom, or pathway—experiences the very best of Conway Public Schools.***

## The Strategic Execution Framework

The Strategic Execution Framework defines how Conway Public Schools translates vision into sustained results through a disciplined, aligned system. Governance begins with the School Board and is informed by the Superintendent and the Superintendent’s Advisory Council, while execution is driven through the expanded District Leadership and Project Conway4Ward committees that coordinate and communicate across the District. This structure ensures that the North Star sets clear direction, Strategic Priorities focus the effort, and Strategic Goals translate that focus into measurable outcomes—ultimately tracked through Key Performance Indicators to ensure accountability and continuous improvement.

**CPSD School Board -**Provides governance, policy direction, and fiduciary oversight to ensure the District operates in alignment with its mission and delivers results for students and taxpayers.

**CPSD Superintendent (ELT/DLT) -**This sole employee of the School Board serves as the chief executive officer of the district, responsible for all other employees, as well as for the academic outcomes, culture, financial management, and day-to-day operations. The Superintendent leverages a decentralized leadership model through the Executive Leadership Team (ELT) and the District Leadership Team (DLT), which includes directors, supervisors, and campus leaders.

**Superintendent’s Advisory Council -**Serves as a strategic thought partner, offering insight, feedback, and community perspective to strengthen alignment, trust, and informed decision-making.

**Project Conway4Ward Committees -**Function as another element of the District’s advisory framework—reviewing plans/goals and helping to coordinate action across Academics, Arts, Activities, and Athletics to ensure consistent, system-wide implementation.

**Our North Star -**Defines the District’s ultimate direction and purpose—establishing a clear, shared mission, vision, and core values that guide all decisions, priorities, and actions.

**Strategic Priorities -**Identify the District’s highest-impact focus areas, concentrating effort and resources on the few initiatives that will drive the greatest improvement in student outcomes and employee engagement.

**Strategic Goals (5-Year | 3-Year) -**Translate priorities into measurable goals with targets over time, creating a clear roadmap for progress and ensuring disciplined execution at every level.

**CPSD Annual Plan of Work -**Provides a crystal clear plan, with specifics deliverables and assigned accountabilities, directly tied to the accomplishment of the Three and Five-year Strategic Goals.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) -**Provide the data and metrics used to monitor progress, ensure accountability, and inform continuous improvement across the District week-to-week and month-to-month.

# CPSD School Board

The School Board serves as the governing body of CPSD, responsible for setting the vision, policies, and strategic direction of the district while ensuring accountability to students, taxpayers, and the community. The Board hires and evaluates the Superintendent as its sole employee, delegates day-to-day operations to that Superintendent, and focuses its role on governance—approving budgets, adopting policies, ensuring legal and ethical compliance, and monitoring student outcomes and district performance. In doing so, the Board acts as the steward of public trust, balancing fiscal responsibility, academic excellence, and community expectations, while maintaining a clear distinction between governance (Board) and management (Superintendent).

# Superintendent’s Advisory Council

Originally established as the Strategic Plan Steering Committee, once the long-range plan is approved by the CPSD School Board, this Steering Committee will be replaced with a Superintendent’s Advisory Council, representing one of the most significant elements of the future of Conway Public School District. The Council will serve as a high-impact advisory body composed of respected community leaders, business partners, parents, educators, with student representation, convened to provide strategic insight, strengthen alignment, and deepen trust between the school district and the community it serves. This group does not govern or manage operations; rather, it functions as a thought partner to the Superintendent, offering diverse perspectives on key initiatives, emerging challenges, and long-term opportunities.

The Council meets regularly—typically quarterly—with a clear agenda tied to the District’s strategic priorities. Members review progress, provide candid feedback, and help identify opportunities for innovation, partnerships, and resource alignment. Importantly, the Council also serves as a two-way communication bridge, helping the District better understand community needs and sentiment while equipping members to serve as informed ambassadors for the District’s direction and progress.

At its best, the Superintendent’s Advisory Council becomes a force multiplier for the District—accelerating strategic execution, increasing accountability, and improving community engagement— ensuring that Conway Public Schools remains responsive, forward-looking, and deeply connected to the community it serves.

# Project Conway4Ward Advisory Committees

One of the most consistent themes emerging from the District's listening tour was the need for a more consistent student experience across schools, programs, and campuses. ***Project*** ***Conway4Ward*** expands the District's advisory framework by aligning the four pillars of Academics, Athletics, Arts, and Activities around a shared set of expectations, priorities, and performance standards. Each pillar will be guided by a volunteer advisory committee, co-chaired by community and District leaders, to provide strategic input, foster continuous improvement, and help ensure high-quality, consistent experiences and outcomes for every student, regardless of where they attend school or participate.

Rather than operating as independent programs, each area will function as an integrated part of a unified system focused on developing the <u>whole</u> student. Through clearly defined plans, shared accountability, and consistent leadership, the framework ensures that every school and every program is moving in the same direction, at an appropriate pace, toward the same outcomes. By bringing structure, focus, and coherence to these four critical domains, Conway Public Schools will move forward as one aligned system, delivering exceptional results for every student, every day.

## Academics

This area is focused on ensuring every student is fully prepared for success beyond high school—whether in college, career, or life. This includes strengthening core learning in math and reading, developing a clear graduate profile and pipeline, expanding early childhood (Pre-K) collaboration, and implementing strong mentorship and behavior support systems to drive student achievement.

## Athletics

Athletics is focused on developing student leaders through competition, teamwork, and discipline. It includes strengthening coaching and athlete development, ensuring equitable support across all sports and facilities, and prioritizing student health, safety, and equipment to create high-quality athletic experiences.

## Arts

The Arts is centered on expanding access to creative expression and performance opportunities for all students. This includes growing programs such as band, choir, orchestra, and theatre, improving practice and performance spaces, and continuing to invest in both visual and performing arts to foster creativity, confidence, and well-rounded student development.

## Activities

Activities emphasize building student belonging, leadership, and engagement through extracurricular involvement. It supports clubs, organizations, and leadership programs (such as Beta Club, FBLA, FFA, FCCLA, and Key Club), while also expanding opportunities so every student has access to meaningful participation beyond the classroom.

## Overall Intent of the Model

Together, these four areas represent a whole-child, “360-degree” development strategy—aligning academic rigor with creativity, leadership, and physical development—while also creating opportunities for community partnerships, sponsorships, and external support to enhance each area.

# CPSD Annual Plan of Work

The ***Annual Plan of Work*** is designed to help translate the Strategic Plan from vision into disciplined, measurable action. Each year, the Superintendent and Executive Leadership Team will identify a focused set of key initiatives directly aligned to the five Strategic Priorities and their associated goals. These initiatives are then broken down into specific and clear deliverables, defined timelines, individual accountabilities, and measurable outcomes, ensuring that every major effort across the District is intentional and strategically aligned. Each initiative is assigned a responsible owner, with explicit expectations for performance and progress.

To ensure accountability and transparency, the Annual Plan of Work is supported by monthly/quarterly performance review cadence, including progress reviews, data tracking, and public reporting through a district scorecard. Success is not defined by activity, but by results—measured through clearly established KPIs tied to student outcomes, operational effectiveness, and stakeholder trust. This structure creates organizational clarity, aligns resources and effort across departments and schools, and establishes a culture where commitments are visible, progress is monitored, and results are delivered.

## Resource Optimization & Alignment (ROA)

Resource Optimization & Alignment (ROA) is the District’s disciplined approach to ensuring that every dollar, position, and program is strategically aligned to its highest priorities and delivering measurable value for students. Grounded in the principles of Financial & Operational Excellence, ROA involves a systematic review of spending patterns, staffing models, and program effectiveness to identify opportunities to eliminate inefficiencies, reallocate resources, and invest more deeply in high-impact areas.

ROA will be deployed as an integral component of the ***Annual Plan of Work***, with each department and school evaluating how its resources align to the Strategic Priorities and established goals. Through this process, leaders will identify specific actions to optimize resources, with clear deliverables, timelines, and accountability measures. Progress will be monitored through the District’s monthly/quarterly review cadence, ensuring that resource decisions are clear, data-driven, and continuously refined to maximize student outcomes.

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are the District's primary **leading indicators**—the critical measures used to monitor organizational performance and predict progress toward achieving the Strategic Plan's long-term goals. While the Strategic Goals measure the outcomes we ultimately seek to achieve, KPIs monitor the effectiveness, consistency, and quality of the work being performed each week and month to produce those outcomes.

To maintain focus on what matters most, each major functional area will identify a small set of meaningful indicators that provide timely insight into organizational health, execution, and performance. These measures will be reviewed regularly by district leadership to celebrate progress, identify emerging challenges, support data-informed decision making, and make course corrections before small issues become significant problems.

Together, the Strategic Goals, Annual Plan of Work, and Key Performance Indicators form an integrated performance management system that aligns strategy, execution, accountability, and continuous improvement across the District.

- The Strategic Plan establishes ***who we are*** and ***where we're going.***
- Our Strategic Goals and Targets determine ***the desired outcomes we strive to*** ***achieve*** across the 3-year and 5-year planning horizons.
- The Annual Plan of Work defines ***what we'll do this year.***
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) monitor ***how well we're executing*** on a week-to-week and month-to-month basis.

# Final Summary

This Strategic Plan aligns people, systems, and partnerships to support our students, strengthen our staff, and connect Conway Public Schools to the future of our community. We must remain focused on our “North Star” and refuse to be distracted by shiny objects, rabbit trails, or noise.

If we are able to do this, the resulting work will be focused and intentional, from which the resulting outcomes will be built to endure.
