Bell to Bell, No Cell 🚫
As a reminder, CPSD students are expected to follow our “Bell to Bell, No Cell” expectations during the school day.📌 A student is not considered to be in possession of a personal electronic device if:It’s stored in their bag (and not accessed),It’s stored in a locker (and not accessed), orIt’s stored in their vehicle (and not accessed) — except as permitted by law.📲 Follow-up for Infractions:1️⃣ First Violation: Device Confiscation, Warning, Parent Contact, Student Pick-Up2️⃣ Second Violation: Device Confiscation, Parent Contact, Detention, Parent Pick-Up3️⃣ Third Violation: Device Confiscation, Parent Meeting, Saturday School/In-School Suspension, Parent Pick-Up🚨 Additional Violations: Device Confiscation, Parent Meeting, ISS/OSS, Parent Pick-Up🔗 View the full policy here: https://5il.co/3l9nqLet’s all work together to keep classrooms focused and distraction-free! 💙📚 #CPSD #BellToBellNoCell #StudentSuccess

### 2025-08-17 · Matthew Coatney (id 9677428)

Good afternoon, Vann Families,

Tomorrow, August 18th, begins the first day of school for the 2025-2026 school year. We are so excited to have all of the students start tomorrow. Below are just some reminders and tips that will help the 1st day go smoothly for all. If you are on campus and have any questions or need help, you can ask anyone in a green Marguerite Vann T shirt. All of our staff members will be wearing a green shirt and will be ready to help. 

Morning Drop OffFront doors will open at 7:30am. All students will report to the cafeteria. Breakfast is from 7:30-7:55am. (students will still get a breakfast if they come in late)Students who are not eating/done eating will be dismissed to their classrooms at 7:45amThe tardy bell will ring at 8am and instruction will begin.Kindergarten students only have until 9am on the first day to report to class. For morning drop, be sure to pull all the way forward so that we can fit as many cars in as possible to unload.If you need to walk your student to the front door or they are not ready to get out of the car and walk in, please park in a parking spot. That way the line can continue to move efficiently. 

You can walk your student to class on the first day of school only. (Kindergarten students ONLY will get to be walked to class again on August 25th). Please do not park your vehicle by the sidewalk in the school parking lot. That is where cars will be moving and dropping students off. 

Students and parents will stay in the cafeteria, tomorrow morning, until it is time to dismiss students to class at 7:45am. 

We will have a morning breakfast/meet and greet for our Kindergarten families from 8-9:15am during the 1st day of school. After you drop your student off to class you can come to the library for some breakfast and meet other Kindergarten families. 

If you did not get a car pick up tag at Open House, be sure to stop by the office and get one. You will need the car rider tag to pick your student up in the carline. 

DismissalWe will begin dismissing students at 3:15pmIf your student is a walker, then you will pick them up on the side of the building inside the gated playground area. If you are picking your student up in a car, then you will need to pick them up from the front of the building. You will need to enter Carl Stuart Rd from Hubbard rd. You can get to Hubbard Rd from Timberpeg Ct, College Ave, or Bruce St. Carl Stuart is a one way street from 7am-9am & 2pm-4pm. You will not be able to get onto Carl Stuart Rd from Salem Rd during those times. When you get onto Carl Stuart Rd, you can go on either side of the road as we will have two lanes. A staff member will be outside to call for your student. Make sure you have your car rider tag. Those two lanes will merge into ONE LANE as you pull into the school parking lot. Be sure to pull all the way forward until the car in front of you stops. Even if you see your student, be sure to pull all the way forward. That way we can fit as many cars in as possible. 

With it being the first week of school, please be patient with us during car dismissal, as it takes longer than normal that first week with dismissal. 

Thank you,
Marguerite Vann Elementary

Dear Families,

Over the next few weeks, your second grader will participate in a districtwide assessment. Each spring, Conway School District screens all second grade students using a nationally normed measure that evaluates students’ reasoning abilities in verbal, quantitative, and nonverbal areas.

This assessment helps us better understand each student’s learning strengths and academic potential.
