### 2024-02-15 · Woodrow Cummins Elementary (id 5582429)

We are in the middle of sick season and wanted to give a few gentle reminders about some of the CPSD Health Guidelines, as well as, some other recommendations and requests. 

1. Students are prohibited from carrying  or possessing any form of medication with the exception of inhalers or epi-pens. Medications needing to be administered by the nurse, must be brought into the school by a parent/guardian.  

 2. Students with a temperature of 100.0 or above will be excluded from school.  In order to return to school, students must remain fever free for 24 hours without fever reducing medication. 

3. Any students determined to have had vomiting or diarrhea, will be excluded from school and shall remain home until free of those conditions for 24 hours without the aid of medication. 

4. Please send a seasonally appropriate change of clothing with EVERY student, regardless of age, in his or her backpack. 

Please see the attached graphics for more CPS Health Guidelines.
