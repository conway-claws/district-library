## <u>1: Jointly Developed</u>

Describe how the School works with parents and families to develop and review relevant plans, policies, and strategies related to engagement.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***1.1:** How does the School-in collaboration with parents-establish an engagement plan that* *reflects the specific academic improvement needs of the School, and that includes programs* *and practices that enhance engagement and address the specific engagement needs of* *students and their families?*
[*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(a)*]

- ***1.2:** What efforts have been made to ensure adequate representation of parents and families* *of participating children in the process (Title I families)?*
[*ESSA § 1116(c)(3)*]

- **1.1** Jim Stone Elementary’s Parent and Family Engagement Coordinator meets with school administrators, PTO representatives, parents, and teachers in the fall to review the current parent and family engagement plan, and address new needs of our current students and families. This meeting will take place on or around Friday, September 13th at 2:00 pm in the PD lab. During this meeting, the stakeholders will discuss last year’s successes and what changes need to be made for the upcoming school year to better accommodate our changing student and family population. New goals to enhance family and student engagement are suggested, discussed, and added to the current parent and family engagement plan. Another meeting in the spring is planned to evaluate the current plan and suggest changes for the next school year.
- **1.2** The fall and spring Parent and Family Engagement meetings are advertised in the school newsletter, teacher classroom newsletters, and social media outlets to encourage attendance. The meeting is open to any school stakeholders that want to attend. We will also send out a ZOOM link in case someone can not attend physically, but would like to participate.
## <u>2: Communication</u>

Describe how the School will communicate with and distribute information to parents and families.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***2.1:** How does the School distribute an informational packet appropriate for the age and grade* *of each child annually, ensuring to include:*
- *description of the engagement program*
- *recommended roles for parents, students, teacher, and the School*
- *ways for a family to get involved*
- *survey regarding volunteer interests*
- *schedule of activities planned throughout the school year*
- *regular, two-way, and meaningful system for parents/teachers to communicate* [*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(b)(3)(B)(i)*]
- ***2.2:** How will the School ensure information related to school and parent programs, meetings,* *and other activities is provided to parents in a format and in a language that parents can* *understand (to the extent practicable)?*
- *How is relevant information provided in a variety of ways? (For example, paper copies* *made available, as well as social media posts, website links, parent apps, etc.?* [*ESSA § 1116(e)(5)*]
- ***2.3:** How does the School offer flexible opportunities for meetings with families?*
[*ESSA § 1116(c)(2)*]

- **2.1** Every fall, a parent guide will be provided to our families via email and a copy will be added to Jim Stone Elementary’s school website. A paper copy can be provided if needed. The parent guide will also be translated into Spanish. The parent guide will include:
- description of the engagement program
- recommended roles for parents, students, teacher, and the school
- ways for families to get involved
- survey regarding volunteer interests
- schedule of activities planned throughout the school year
- regular, two-way, and meaningful system for parents/teachers to communicate
- **2.2** Jim Stone Elementary provides relevant information to families through a variety of means including the school’s weekly newsletter, newsletters from the classroom teachers, push notifications, and via the school Facebook page. Paper copies will be provided if requested. Relevant information will also be translated into Spanish.
- **2.3** Jim Stone Elementary offers flexible opportunities for meetings with families. PTO meetings are open to all stakeholders and a copy of the meeting minutes sent via email to families for those unable to attend. These meetings are also offered on ZOOM. For parents that wish to meet with an administrator or teacher, parents may call the school to set up a meeting time convenient for both parties. Jim Stone Elementary has a yearly Back to School Bash/Open House during the evening before school starts in the fall to allow parents to meet the school faculty and ask questions. Jim Stone Elementary also hosts two Parent and Family Engagement events during the school year. Jim Stone Elementary teachers are also available for phone conferences as requested and can be reached via email.

## <u>5: Coordination</u>

Describe how the School will coordinate with other organizations, businesses, and community partners, including alumni, to provide additional supports, services, and resources to families.

*Guiding Questions*

- ***5.1:** How does the School investigate and utilize community resources in the instructional* *program?*
[*ADE Rules Governing Parental Involvement Section 5.06*]

- ***5.2:** How does the School coordinate and integrate programs and activities with other Federal,* *State, and local programs? Some examples include:*
- *public preschool programs such as Head Start*
- *organizations/activities to help students transition to elementary, middle, high, and* *postsecondary schools or careers*
- *wraparound services that allow families to send their children to school ready and able* *to focus on learning* [*ESSA §1116(e)(4)*]
- ***5.3:** In what ways does the School enable the formation of a Parent Teacher Association or* *organization and ensure leaders of said organization will be included in appropriate decisions?*
[*A.C.A. § 6-15-1702(b)(8)(B)(ii)*]

**5.1** Jim Stone Elementary's Parent and Family Engagement Committee as well as the PTO will investigate how different community resources may be used in our instructional programs by

reaching out to various community organizations throughout the year to enlist assistance when needed. We are partnered with Woodland Heights Baptist Church and they provide assistance and volunteer at school events. We also partner with the University of Central Arkansas to host an academic family event. School faculty and staff members are encouraged to use the volunteer surveys to build and develop partnerships and involve our parents in a variety of roles that will help our students achieve the challenging state academic standards. The volunteer surveys will be kept in the Parent and Family Engagement binder.

- **5.2** Jim Stone Elementary communicates with Sallie Cone PreSchool to provide a smooth transition to our school. We also take our fourth grade students to tour the middle school so that they will know what to expect when moving to middle school. We will host a meeting with kindergarten students and families prior to the first day of school to allow them the opportunity to tour the building and meet the teachers. New students who receive special education services will have a transition conference where parents are able to meet the staff and ask questions pertaining to their students' services.
- **5.3** Jim Stone Elementary maintains an active Parent Teacher Organization. The leaders of the PTO, as well as an alumnus of Jim Stone will be part of the Parent Advisory Committee. PTO meetings are held monthly with the principal, parents, and teachers. Meeting agendas and minutes are sent out before and after meetings along with an invitation to join our PTO.
